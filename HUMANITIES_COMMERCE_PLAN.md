# HUMANITIES & COMMERCE — the perfect-system plan

> Companion to SANDBOX_TAXONOMY.md (8 engine types). Same rules apply: closed-world
> content from the corpus (14,198 CBSE + 9,433 CISCE nodes), solver-able fraction
> classified honestly (counted, never estimated), step contract on every numeric
> family, independent auditor as acceptance gate.

## The core insight for non-numeric subjects

Humanities/literature questions are long-form — a "solver" in the maths sense
doesn't exist for "explain the causes of 1857". But a perfect system IS buildable
because it has three layers, and we already own one of them:

1. **EXAMINER LAYER (built — the trust stack).** `examiner_mode.grade_answer()`
   grades any long answer against the corpus node's MARKING_POINTS
   (per-point keyword scoring + CopyWorthy diff), accepts photos via
   `/api/examiner/grade-image`. This is the universal checker for every
   non-numeric answer in every humanities/literature subject — no new
   technology needed, just corpus MARKING_POINTS quality (the 59-header spec
   already mandates them).
2. **DETERMINISTIC MICRO-ENGINES (the solver-able fraction — build these).**
   Each humanities subject has a genuinely deterministic core; classify it per
   book, then build engines exactly like family modules: input → checked
   output → steps.
3. **EVIDENCE MACHINE (long-answer trainer).** Claim → quote/cause (corpus
   text) → mechanism/device → effect chains; every link corpus-backed. The
   student builds the chain, the engine validates each link against the node.

## A. Humanities, subject by subject

### History (est. solver-able ~30%, rest examiner-graded)
- Chronology orderer: drag events/dates into order, auto-checked from corpus
  dates (E6 timeline engine — histciv sandbox exists, needs corpus-depth pass).
- Cause-chain validator: causal graph per chapter from corpus; student orders
  causes → engine checks necessity/sufficiency edges.
- Map-history tie-in via E5: empires, routes, freedom-movement centres plotted
  and checked.
- Source attribution quiz: "who said/wrote this" from corpus quotes.

### Civics / Political Science (~35% deterministic)
- Seat-vs-vote arithmetic: vote shares → seats under FPTP, coalition majority
  counter (fully numeric → full family treatment + step contract).
- Constitution matcher: article ↔ provision flash-quiz from corpus.
- Parliament flow simulator (E4): bill stages, what breaks when LoP defects,
  enumerated powers checked stepwise.

### Geography (~45% deterministic — richest humanities subject for engines)
- E5 geo sandbox exists; add: climate-graph reader (given a station's rainfall/
  temp table → identify season/region, checked), monsoon mechanism sim
  (differential heating → wind reversal, E4), population-pyramid builder
  (age-sex bars → growth stage classification, E1), contour/relief reader,
  time-zone arithmetic (fully numeric).
- Resource/industry location logic chains (validate against corpus factors).

### Psychology (~40% — statistics overlap carries it)
- Self-run experiments: reaction time, memory curve (serial position), Stroop —
  the student IS the subject; results plotted and interpreted (E3).
- Statistics reuse: mean/median/mode/correlation families exist in maths/econ
  modules — route psychology phrasings to them.
- Disorder-symptom matcher and Piaget/Vygotsky stage classifiers from corpus
  (deterministic lookup + why-steps).

### Sociology (~30%)
- Demography arithmetic: CBR/CDR/growth-rate/doubling-time (numeric, full
  contract).
- Sampling simulator (E4): population → sample → bias demo.
- Urbanisation/graph reading engines (census tables from corpus).

## B. Literature (English + Hindi + other languages)

- **Meter/laya scanner (the flagship deterministic engine).** Hindi/Urdu matra
  counting is fully rule-based in Devanagari; English stress-pattern scanning
  (iambic/trochaic) is syllable-count + stress heuristics. Poem in → metre
  identified with per-line breakdown + steps. Genuinely solver-able.
- **Device spotter:** metaphor/simile/personification/alliteration/hyperbole —
  corpus examples as the closed world; quiz + explain with why-steps.
- **Quote attribution + character-relationship graph + plot-timeline builder**
  (E6) for the prescribed texts (closed world: the corpus's literature nodes).
- **Writing format-checker (deterministic!):** letter/article/report/story
  scaffolds checked mechanically — sender/receiver/date/subject present,
  salutation correct, paragraph count, word-limit compliance. Rubric beats are
  checkable without any AI judgement.
- **Essay scaffold (PEEL/PQE)** + examiner grading on submit.
- **langym sandbox exists** — upgrade it into this: poem scanner, device
  trainer, writing format-checker, comprehension generator from corpus passages.
- Hindi-specific: sandhi/samas splitting (rule-based), muhavare/lokoktiyan
  matcher, alankaar classifier — all deterministic from grammar rules.

## C. Commerce (Accounts + Economics are already in-flight; Business Studies is the gap)

- **Already covered:** accountancy_families (agents finishing exhaustiveness:
  bills of exchange, partnership admission/retirement, company accounts,
  cash-flow, NFP) and economics_families (cost curves, correlation, full macro).
  AccountancySandbox + EconomicsSandbox shipped.
- **THE FLAGSHIP — Virtual Company (E7 Document & Ledger engine):** the student
  runs one continuous firm: transactions → journal (golden-rules checked per
  entry) → ledger → trial balance (must tally) → adjustments (depreciation,
  BRS, bills) → final accounts → ratio analysis. Every accountancy family
  becomes a checkpoint in one living business; wrong entries are caught at the
  step they break, not at the end. This is the commerce equivalent of the
  chemistry virtual lab — one engine that exercises every family.
- **Business Studies system (the missing subject):**
  - Case simulator (E4): scenario → student picks the management function/
    principle/marketing decision → modeled outcome + why (corpus case banks).
  - Organisational-structure builder (E2): construct formal/informal structures,
    delegation vs decentralisation paths checked.
  - Management-thought timeline (E6): Taylor/Fayol/Maslow/Herzberg progression.
  - Numeric overlap is real: working capital, break-even, stock/ex-stock
    arithmetic route to accountancy families.
- **Entrepreneurship lab (E1, fully deterministic):** break-even
  (BEP = FC/(P−VC)), unit economics, cash-flow projector, working-capital gap —
  pure family modules + a slider lab.

## D. Build order (same pattern that worked for the sciences)

| Wave | Content | Gate |
|---|---|---|
| H1 | Literature micro-engines (meter scanner, device spotter, format-checker) + examiner wiring for literature nodes | auditor + harness per engine |
| H2 | Civics seat arithmetic + chronology + cause-chain; geography pyramid/climate/time-zone engines | step contract on numeric ones |
| H3 | Business Studies case simulator + entrepreneurship lab + Virtual Company ledger engine | corpus-closed content check |

Each wave: book inventory → solver_able classification (counted) → engines/
families → step contract assertions → independent audit → both trees.

## Honest expectations

Humanities will never be 100% "solver" — that's correct, not a gap. The perfect
system = deterministic engines where determinism exists, examiner grading
everywhere else, and the evidence machine to TRAIN long answers rather than fake
auto-solve them. The corpus already carries MARKING_POINTS and diagnostics for
these subjects; the engines derive everything from it.
