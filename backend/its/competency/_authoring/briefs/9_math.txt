DEFINITION: Linear patterns in real-life situations are relationships where one quantity changes at a constant rate with respect to another, expressible in the form y = mx + c (where m is the constant rate of change and c is the initial value), observed in contexts like taxi fares (fixed charge + per-km charge), mobile phone bills (rental + per-minute cost), temperature conversion (C = 9C/5 + 32), and simple interest (I = Prt, linear in time at fixed rate).
WALKTHROUGH: Imagine you take a taxi. The driver says: "Rs 30 base fare, plus Rs 12 per kilometer." After 1 km, you pay 30 + 12 = Rs 42. After 2 km, 30 + 24 = Rs 54. After 5 km, 30 + 60 = Rs 90. Notice the pattern: the total fare increases by Rs 12 for every additional km — the rate of change is constant (12 Rs/km). This is a linear pattern. The equation is: Fare = 12 × (distance) + 30, or y = 12x + 30, where m = 12 (rate per km) and c = 30 (base fare). Now compare with a different pattern: the area of a square (A = s²). If s = 1, A = 1; s = 2, A = 4; s = 3, A = 9. The increase is not constant (1, 3, 5) — this is non-linear (quadratic). The key test: if the difference between consecutive values is constant, the pattern is linear. The key insight: linear patterns are everywhere in daily life — billing, rates, conversions — and recognizing them lets you predict values without calculating each one.

DEFINITION: Natural numbers (denoted by ℕ) are the set of counting numbers {1, 2, 3, ...} used for ordering and quantifying discrete objects. DEFINITION: Heron's Formula calculates the area of a triangle when the lengths of all three sides (a, b, c) are known, without requiring the height. The formula is Area = √[s(s-a)(s-b)(s-c)], where s = (a+b+c)/2 is the semi-perimeter (half the perimeter) of the triangle.

Heron, an ancient Greek mathematician, discovered we can find the area using only the three side lengths. First, we find the semi-perimeter s by adding all three sides and dividing by 2. The key insight is that the semi-perimeter acts as a "balancing point" that makes the formula work for any triangle shape.
