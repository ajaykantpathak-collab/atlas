WALKTHROUGH: Imagine you walk into a Big Bazaar or DMart on a weekend: you pick up a trolley, navigate aisles labelled "Staples," "Fresh Produce," "Home Care," select branded items with visible price tags, and pay at a checkout counter—no salesperson persuades you, no credit is offered, and you carry bags home. Now contrast this with a neighbourhood kirana store where the owner knows your preferences, offers monthly credit, and delivers to your doorstep. Why does the supermarket model work for weekly stock-up trips but not for emergency midnight purchases?
