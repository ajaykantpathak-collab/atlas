DEFINITION: Field Survey Design is the systematic process of planning a primary data collection exercise by defining clear research objectives, selecting appropriate sampling techniques, constructing data collection instruments (questionnaires, interview schedules, observation checklists), and organizing logistical, ethical, and analytical frameworks before entering the field. It transforms a geographical question into an actionable, replicable fieldwork protocol that yields reliable spatial and attribute data for analysis.
