DEFINITION: Secret Reserve is a reserve that is not disclosed in the balance sheet, created by deliberately understating assets (e.g., charging excessive depreciation, undervaluing inventory) or overstating liabilities (e.g., excessive provisions, treating contingent liabilities as actual liabilities), resulting in reported profits lower than actual profits.

For example, if your machine will last 10 years but you depreciate it as if it lasts 5 years, you charge double depreciation – profit goes down, but you've secretly built a reserve. The key insight: secret reserves are not illegal if within reasonable limits; they are a prudence tool.
