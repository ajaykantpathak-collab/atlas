## ELI5
Three friends start with similar pocket money in 1980: Chen's allowance compounds near 10% for decades, Arjun's at 6-7%, Parvez's fades to 3%. GDP growth rate measures the annual percentage change in inflation-adjusted output within a country's domestic territory; per capita income divides GNI at purchasing power parity by mid-year population, expressed in international dollars for comparison. The destinations differ with the engines: China's $22,029 against India's $9,047 and Pakistan's $5,501 - sustained high growth means shifting into high-productivity sectors, not just growing.

## DEFINITION
GDP Growth Rate measures the annual percentage change in the inflation-adjusted market value of all final goods and services produced within a country's domestic territory during a given period, while Per Capita Income (PCI) is the ratio of a country's Gross National Income (GNI) at Purchasing Power Parity (PPP) to its mid-year population, expressed in international dollars to enable cross-country comparison of average living standards.

## THIS_FORMULA
Real GDP Growth Rate (%) = [(Real GDP_t - Real GDP_{t-1}) / Real GDP_{t-1}] × 100; Per Capita Income (PPP $) = GNI (PPP $) / Mid-year Population; Sectoral Labor Productivity Ratio = (Sector GVA Share %) / (Sector Workforce Share %)

## FORMULA_VARIABLES
Real GDP_t = Inflation-adjusted GDP in current year (constant base-year prices); Real GDP_{t-1} = Inflation-adjusted GDP in previous year; GNI (PPP $) = Gross National Income at Purchasing Power Parity in current international dollars; Mid-year Population = Average population during the year (millions); Sector GVA Share % = Sector's Gross Value Added as percentage of total GVA; Sector Workforce Share % = Sector's employment as percentage of total workforce.

## FORMULA_UNITS
Real GDP Growth Rate: % per annum; Per Capita Income: PPP $ (current international dollars); Sectoral Labor Productivity Ratio: dimensionless (ratio of percentages); Population: millions; GNI/GDP: trillion PPP $.

## WORKED_EXAMPLE_1
PROBLEM: Using Table 8.2 and 8.3 data, compute the contribution of each sector to China's overall GDP growth in 2014-18. Given: Agriculture growth 3.1% (weight 8%), Industry 5.3% (weight 38%), Services 7.1% (weight 54%). Verify if the weighted sum equals the reported overall growth (assume ~6.0% from context).

STEPS:
- Step 1: Identify sectoral growth rates (g_i) and GVA shares (w_i) for China 2014-18 from Table 8.4 and 8.3: Agri g=3.1%, w=8%; Ind g=5.3%, w=38%; Ser g=7.1%, w=54%.
- Step 2: Recall the formula for sectoral contribution to overall GDP growth: Contribution_i = w_i × g_i (where w_i is in decimal form, e.g., 8% = 0.08).
- Step 3: Calculate each sector's contribution: Agriculture = 0.08 × 3.1 = 0.248 pp; Industry = 0.38 × 5.3 = 2.014 pp; Services = 0.54 × 7.1 = 3.834 pp.
- Step 4: Sum contributions: 0.248 + 2.014 + 3.834 = 6.096 percentage points ≈ 6.1% overall GDP growth.
- Step 5: Interpret: Services contributed 63% (3.834/6.096) of China's growth despite industry's larger weight, because services grew faster. Industry contributed 33%, agriculture only 4%.
- Step 6: Contrast with India 2014-18 (Table 8.4): Agri 3.1% (w=18%), Ind 6.9% (w=28%), Ser 7.6% (w=54%). India's industry contribution = 0.28×6.9=1.93 pp vs China's 2.01 pp — similar absolute contribution despite lower weight, due to higher growth rate.

REASONING: This decomposition (growth accounting) reveals the structural drivers of growth. China's growth was service-led even in 2014-18, but industry still contributed significantly due to large weight. India's industry punched above its weight. The method uses the identity: Σ(w_i × g_i) = g_overall, which holds exactly for value-added shares.

ANSWER: China 2014-18 sectoral contributions: Agriculture 0.25 pp, Industry 2.01 pp, Services 3.83 pp. Total ≈ 6.1% GDP growth. Services drove 63% of growth.
