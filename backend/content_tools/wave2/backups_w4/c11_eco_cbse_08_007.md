## ELI5
Why does an urban Chinese family outlive a rural Pakistani one on similar 1980 starting incomes? The Human Development Index answers with three normalized indices - health (life expectancy at birth), education (mean and expected years of schooling), standard of living (GNI per capita at PPP) - combined as a geometric mean and ranked into four tiers. The scoreboard: China 0.797, India 0.685, Pakistan 0.544, with China leading every component - life expectancy 78 against 72 and 67.6, schooling 8.0 against 6.9 and 4.3. Current policy plus historical investment.

## DEFINITION
The Human Development Index (HDI) is a composite statistic of life expectancy, education (mean years of schooling and expected years of schooling), and per capita income (GNI per capita at PPP) indicators, used to rank countries into four tiers of human development. It is calculated as the geometric mean of three normalized dimension indices: Health Index (life expectancy at birth), Education Index (mean years of schooling and expected years of schooling), and Standard of Living Index (GNI per capita at PPP).

## COMMON_MISCONCEPTION
Students believe HDI ranking directly reflects current government policy effectiveness. They see China's rank 78 vs India's 130 and conclude "China's current policies are better." This ignores the textbook's explicit finding: "these improvements were attributed not to the reform process but the strategies that China adopted in the pre-reform period" - specifically, pre-1978 commune-based health/education infrastructure. The misconception confuses stock (accumulated human capital) with flow (current policy outputs), and attributes path-dependent outcomes to recent reforms.

## THIS_FORMULA
HDI = (Health Index × Education Index × Income Index)^(1/3) where Health Index = (LE - 20)/(85 - 20), Education Index = (MYSI + EYSI)/2 with MYSI = (MYS - 0)/(15 - 0), EYSI = (EYS - 0)/(18 - 0), Income Index = (ln(GNIpc) - ln(100))/(ln(75000) - ln(100))

## FORMULA_VARIABLES
LE = Life Expectancy at birth (years); MYS = Mean Years of Schooling (adults 25+); EYS = Expected Years of Schooling (children); GNIpc = Gross National Income per capita at PPP (constant 2017 international $); MYSI = Mean Years of Schooling Index; EYSI = Expected Years of Schooling Index; ln = natural logarithm

## FORMULA_UNITS
LE: years; MYS: years; EYS: years; GNIpc: PPP international dollars (2017 constant); All indices: dimensionless (0 to 1); HDI: dimensionless (0 to 1)

## COMPUTATION_TYPE
algebraic

## WORKED_EXAMPLE_1
PROBLEM: Using Table 8.5 data, calculate the Education Index for India given: Mean Years of Schooling (MYS) = 6.9 years, Expected Years of Schooling (EYS) = 12.2 years (assumed from HDI 2023 data). Use goalposts: MYS min=0, max=15; EYS min=0, max=18. Show all steps.
STEPS:
- Step 1: Identify the formula for Education Index components: MYSI = (MYS - 0) / (15 - 0); EYSI = (EYS - 0) / (18 - 0); Education Index = (MYSI + EYSI) / 2
- Step 2: Calculate MYSI for India: MYSI = (6.9 - 0) / 15 = 6.9 / 15 = 0.460
- Step 3: Calculate EYSI for India: EYSI = (12.2 - 0) / 18 = 12.2 / 18 = 0.678
- Step 4: Compute Education Index: (0.460 + 0.678) / 2 = 1.138 / 2 = 0.569
- Step 5: Interpret: India's Education Index of 0.569 indicates it has achieved 56.9% of the maximum possible education attainment (15 years mean schooling, 18 years expected schooling)
REASONING: The Education Index averages two sub-indices capturing stock (adult education) and flow (children's expected education). Goalpost normalization converts years into comparable 0-1 scale. Arithmetic mean of sub-indices reflects equal weighting of current and future human capital.
ANSWER: Education Index for India = 0.569 (MYSI = 0.460, EYSI = 0.678)

## WORKED_EXAMPLE_2
PROBLEM: Calculate the Income Index for China and Pakistan using Table 8.5 GNI per capita PPP values (China: $22,029; Pakistan: $5,501). Goalposts: min=$100, max=$75,000. Use natural logarithm. Compare the results and explain what the difference reveals about diminishing returns to income in HDI.
STEPS:
- Step 1: Recall Income Index formula: (ln(GNIpc) - ln(100)) / (ln(75000) - ln(100))
- Step 2: Compute denominator (constant): ln(75000) - ln(100) = 11.225 - 4.605 = 6.620
- Step 3: Calculate China's numerator: ln(22029) - ln(100) = 10.000 - 4.605 = 5.395
- Step 4: Calculate China's Income Index: 5.395 / 6.620 = 0.815
- Step 5: Calculate Pakistan's numerator: ln(5501) - ln(100) = 8.613 - 4.605 = 4.008
- Step 6: Calculate Pakistan's Income Index: 4.008 / 6.620 = 0.605
- Step 7: Compare: China's index (0.815) is 35% higher than Pakistan's (0.605), but China's GNIpc is 4x Pakistan's ($22,029 vs $5,501). The logarithmic transformation captures diminishing marginal utility of income for human development.
REASONING: Logarithmic scaling reflects that each additional dollar contributes less to human well-being at higher income levels. This prevents high-income countries from dominating HDI solely through income. The 4x income gap translates to only 0.21 index gap, showing HDI's design to balance income with health/education.
ANSWER: China Income Index = 0.815; Pakistan Income Index = 0.605; Difference = 0.210 (despite 4x income ratio)
