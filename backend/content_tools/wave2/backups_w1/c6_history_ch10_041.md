## DEFINITION
The expansion of British rule in India refers to the process by which the East India Company, starting from Bengal, brought diverse regions under its control through military conquest, political treaties, and direct annexation.

## WALKTHROUGH
Imagine you are playing a game of territory capture on a large map where your goal is to grow your influence from one small corner to the entire board. The key insight is that the British rarely relied on just one method; they shifted their tactics based on who they were facing.
