## ELI5
Subtracting an integer is adding its opposite - the one rule that rebuilds the whole topic. Five minus minus three becomes five plus three, because taking away a debt is receiving money. At floor plus four and need floor minus two? That is subtracting six, the same as adding minus six. Change the sign, change the direction, then walk the line as usual.

## DEFINITION
Subtraction of integers is the mathematical operation of finding the difference between two integers, which is equivalent to adding the additive inverse (the opposite) of the number being subtracted.
## COGNITIVE_LEVEL
apply
## MASTERY_CRITERIA
The student must be able to convert any subtraction expression into an addition expression using the rule "a - b = a + (-b)" and solve it accurately using a number line or token model.
## SCOPE_BOUNDARY
Covers subtraction of integers, including positive and negative numbers and zero. Excludes algebraic variable manipulation, absolute value proofs, or complex properties of integers.
## WALKTHROUGH
Imagine you are in a building with a lift that goes both above and below the ground floor (0). If you are on the +2 floor and you want to go to the -3 floor, you need to think: "How many floors down do I have to press?" Subtraction is just a way of finding the "movement" needed to get from your starting point to your target point. Instead of just taking away, think of it as "adding the opposite." If you see a subtraction sign, change the sign of the next number and add it instead; for example, subtracting a negative 3 is the same as moving up 3. The key insight is that every subtraction problem is actually an addition problem in disguise.

**Takeaway:** subtraction of integers is adding the additive inverse - keep-change-change turns every difference into a walk you already know. **In the exam:** in subtraction questions, rewrite keep-change-change first, then add on the line or with tokens. **Check yourself:** send the walkthrough's lift from plus four to minus two and read the trip as adding a negative. **Why students miss it:** students subtract signs blindly - the flagged mix-up is a no to that; taking away a negative is a gain, and the line proves it every time.

## COMMON_MISCONCEPTION
Students often treat the subtraction sign as a "remove" instruction only, failing to realize that subtracting a negative number acts like adding. They often calculate 3 - (-2) as 1 because they subtract the 2 from the 3, ignoring the negative sign in the middle.
## ON_FAILURE
If a student fails, have them draw a "Building of Fun" number line. Ask them: "If you are on -2 and the operator removes a negative (a red token), what happens to your total?" Show them that removing a "debt" or "red token" is the same as gaining a positive value.

## WORKED_EXAMPLE_1
PROBLEM: A submarine is at 40 metres below sea level (-40m). It descends another 30 metres. What is its new position?
STEPS:
- step 1: Identify starting position (-40) and movement (down 30 means -30).
- step 2: The expression is (-40) - (+30).
- step 3: Apply the rule: (-40) + (-30).
- step 4: Add the two negative values: -(40 + 30) = -70.
- step 5: The new position is 70 metres below sea level.
REASONING: Since the submarine is already below sea level, descending further is moving deeper into the negative range, which is addition of two negatives.
ANSWER: -70 metres
## WORKED_EXAMPLE_2
PROBLEM: A thermometer shows -5°C. The temperature rises to 2°C. What is the difference in temperature?
STEPS:
- step 1: Identify start (-5) and target (2).
- step 2: Use the rule: Target - Start = Movement (2 - (-5)).
- step 3: Convert to addition: 2 + (+5).
- step 4: Calculate the sum: 7.
- step 5: The temperature rose by 7 degrees.
REASONING: The distance between two points on a number line is found by subtracting the start from the target; the double negative creates a positive movement.
ANSWER: 7°C
## WORKED_EXAMPLE_3
PROBLEM: You have a bank balance of -₹100. You deposit ₹150. What is your new balance?
STEPS:
- step 1: Current balance is -100. Deposit is a positive gain of +150.
- step 2: Expression: -100 + 150.
- step 3: Identify the larger magnitude (150) and smaller magnitude (100).
- step 4: Subtract 100 from 150 to get 50.
- step 5: Apply the sign of the larger magnitude (+).
REASONING: When adding a negative and a positive, you find the difference and keep the sign of the larger value, reflecting the transition from debt to credit.
ANSWER: +₹50
