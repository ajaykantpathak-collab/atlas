## ELI5
While the young Himalayas look jagged and sharp, southern India sits like a solid, flat table — the Peninsular Plateau, a triangle of some of the oldest, hardest rocks on the planet. Think of India as a jigsaw puzzle where the southern piece is the oldest and strongest: formed when the continents were still shuffling into their current places, long before the Himalayas existed. The Aravalli range and the Western and Eastern Ghats act as the walls holding up this giant high platform. Old rock barely changes, which is why the plateau is the stable heart of the peninsula.

## DEFINITION
The Peninsular Plateau is a massive, triangular-shaped tableland in southern India composed of some of the oldest, hardest rocks on our planet. It acts as an ancient, stable foundation that was formed long before the Himalayas began to rise.
