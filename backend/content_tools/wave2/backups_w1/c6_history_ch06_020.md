## ELI5
Medieval culture grew on two stems: words and stone. Kalhana's Sanskrit chronicle ran beside the first great regional literatures, while temple architecture climbed toward the sky in stone. Bhakti saints carried learning out of the courts and into the street languages, and the culture of the era is exactly that widening - from court Sanskrit to common tongues, from forts to temple towns.
