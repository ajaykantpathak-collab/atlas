## ELI5
Notebooks at ₹40 each, a school bag at ₹200, one bill of ₹600 - how much did the notebooks cost? The systematic method unwinds the bill in reverse: strip the bag's ₹200 first, then undo the multiplication by the price, and the count of notebooks stands alone. Reverse the order the bill was built in, doing each undo to both sides, and the unknown falls out with no guesswork.

## DEFINITION
Solving a linear equation using the systematic method involves applying inverse operations sequentially to both sides of an equation to isolate the variable and find its unique numerical value.
## COGNITIVE_LEVEL
apply
## MASTERY_CRITERIA
Can systematically perform inverse operations in the correct order to isolate the variable in multi-step linear equations and verify the solution by substitution.
## SCOPE_BOUNDARY
Covers linear equations in one variable requiring addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division to solve, but excludes simultaneous linear equations or quadratic equations.
## WALKTHROUGH
Imagine buying some notebooks at ₹40 each and a school bag for ₹200, spending a total of ₹600. How do you find the cost of one notebook? In Class 6, you learned to form algebraic expressions and balance simple equations. Now, we use a systematic step-by-step method to undo operations one by one. The key insight is to reverse the operations in the opposite order of operations (PEMDAS backwards) to peel away numbers surrounding the variable. We always perform the exact same mathematical operation on both sides to keep the equation balanced.

**Takeaway:** a linear equation unwinds in reverse: the last operation done to the unknown is the first one undone, on both sides every time. **In the exam:** strip constants before coefficients - subtract the standalone number, then divide by the multiplier - and finish by substituting the answer into the original bill. **Check yourself:** the stationery bill shows it: ₹600 minus the ₹200 bag leaves the notebooks' share, and dividing by the ₹40 price counts the notebooks - the same reverse run solves the algebra. **Why students miss it:** students divide before stripping the added constant and grind to a halt - the order is the entire method. The bill was built forward one operation at a time, so it unwinds backward one inverse at a time; substitution at the end is the receipt proving the reverse run truly lands.

## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_1

## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_1
The goal is to isolate the variable ($x$) on one side of the equation. If a student answers "to find the number," it shows they understand the general intent but lack the precise algebraic vocabulary. If they say "to clear the numbers," it reveals confusion about mathematical operations.

**A wrong answer (saying 'find the number') reveals the student understood the intent but not the algebra - the precise goal is isolating the variable on one side so the other side simply reads its value.**

## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_2
When solving the equation $4x + 7 = 23$, why must we subtract 7 from both sides before dividing by 4, rather than dividing by 4 first?
## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_2
We reverse operations in the reverse order of calculation (undoing addition/subtraction before multiplication/division). A student who thinks you can divide first often forgets to divide the constant term 7, resulting in fractions like $x + \frac{7}{4} = \frac{23}{4}$, which makes the problem harder instead of simpler.
## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_3
Solve the linear equation systematically: $5x - 3 = 22$. Show the value of $x$.
## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_3
Steps:
1. Given equation: $5x - 3 = 22$
2. Add 3 to both sides to undo subtraction: $5x - 3 + 3 = 22 + 3 \implies 5x = 25$
3. Divide both sides by 5 to undo multiplication: $\frac{5x}{5} = \frac{25}{5}$
4. Simplify: $x = 5$
If a student gets $x = 3.8$ or $x = 125$, it shows they either added instead of subtracting, or multiplied instead of dividing during the inverse operation step.
## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_4
Compare the two equations: $2(x + 3) = 14$ and $2x + 3 = 14$. Why do these equations require a different first step in their systematic solution?
## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_4
In $2(x + 3) = 14$, the 2 multiplies the entire parentheses, so we can either divide by 2 first or expand the bracket. In $2x + 3 = 14$, the 3 is added only to $2x$, so we must subtract 3 first. A student who applies the exact same first step to both equations does not understand how parentheses group terms in algebraic expressions.

## COMMON_MISCONCEPTION
Students frequently apply an inverse operation to only one side of the equation, or they perform the correct operation on both sides but forget to change the sign when moving terms across the equals sign. For example, in solving $4x - 8 = 12$, a student might write $4x = 12 - 8$ instead of $4x = 12 + 8$, failing to use the correct additive inverse.
## ON_FAILURE
Remind the student that an equation is like a balanced weighing scale: whatever is done to the left side must be done to the right side without exception. Write out the operation explicitly under both sides using different colored ink (e.g., write "+ 8" under both left and right sides) to ensure balance is maintained before computing.

## WORKED_EXAMPLE_1
PROBLEM: Rahul buys 4 identical geometry boxes and pays a delivery charge of ₹30. His total bill is ₹250. Set up a linear equation and use the systematic method to find the cost of one geometry box ($x$).
STEPS:
- Step 1: Identify given information and form the equation. Let $x$ be the cost of one geometry box. Total cost = $4x + 30 = 250$.
- Step 2: Determine the first inverse operation needed. To undo the addition of 30, subtract 30 from both sides.
- Step 3: Perform the subtraction: $4x + 30 - 30 = 250 - 30 \implies 4x = 220$.
- Step 4: Determine the second inverse operation. To undo multiplication by 4, divide both sides by 4.
- Step 5: Compute the final value: $\frac{4x}{4} = \frac{220}{4} \implies x = 55$. Verify: $4(55) + 30 = 220 + 30 = 250$.
REASONING: We peel away the numbers around $x$ in reverse order of operations. First, we remove the constant term by subtracting 30, leaving the term with the variable ($4x$). Then, we remove the coefficient by dividing by 4, isolating $x$ completely.
ANSWER: ₹55 per geometry box
## WORKED_EXAMPLE_2
PROBLEM: Solve the linear equation systematically: $7x - 14 = 35$.
STEPS:
- Step 1: Examine the equation $7x - 14 = 35$ and identify that $x$ is multiplied by 7 and 14 is subtracted from it.
- Step 2: Apply the inverse of subtraction, which is addition. Add 14 to both sides: $7x - 14 + 14 = 35 + 14$.
- Step 3: Simplify both sides: $7x = 49$.
- Step 4: Apply the inverse of multiplication, which is division. Divide both sides by 7: $\frac{7x}{7} = \frac{49}{7}$.
- Step 5: Compute the result: $x = 7$. Verify by substitution: $7(7) - 14 = 49 - 14 = 35$.
REASONING: By systematically performing inverse operations on both sides, we maintain equality while reducing the equation to its simplest form where $x$ stands alone.
ANSWER: $x = 7$
## WORKED_EXAMPLE_3
PROBLEM: Priya thinks of a number, multiplies it by 3, subtracts 5, and gets the same result as adding 15 to the number. Find the number by setting up and systematically solving a linear equation.
STEPS:
- Step 1: Translate the word problem into an algebraic equation. Let the number be $x$. Left side: $3x - 5$. Right side: $x + 15$. Equation: $3x - 5 = x + 15$.
- Step 2: Collect all variable terms on one side. Subtract $x$ from both sides: $3x - x - 5 = x - x + 15 \implies 2x - 5 = 15$.
- Step 3: Isolate the term with the variable by adding 5 to both sides: $2x - 5 + 5 = 15 + 5 \implies 2x = 20$.
- Step 4: Isolate $x$ by dividing both sides by 2: $\frac{2x}{2} = \frac{20}{2}$.
- Step 5: Compute the final value: $x = 10$. Verify: Left side $3(10) - 5 = 25$; Right side $10 + 15 = 25$. Both sides match.
REASONING: When variables appear on both sides, we first transpose or eliminate the variable from one side using inverse operations, reducing it to a standard two-step linear equation.
ANSWER: $x = 10$
## COMPETENCY_QUESTION_1
QUESTION: A rectangular park has a perimeter of 120 metres. Its length is 10 metres more than twice its breadth ($b$). 
(a) Write an equation representing the perimeter in terms of breadth $b$. 
(b) Solve the equation systematically to find the breadth and length of the park.
COMPETENCY: Translating real-world situations to mathematical expressions and systematic solving
STEPS:
- Step 1: Recall the formula for the perimeter of a rectangle: $P = 2(\text{length} + \text{breadth})$.
- Step 2: Substitute length = $2b + 10$ and perimeter = 120 into the formula: $120 = 2(2b + 10 + b) \implies 120 = 2(3b + 10)$.
- Step 3: Divide both sides by 2: $\frac{120}{2} = 3b + 10 \implies 60 = 3b + 10$.
- Step 4: Subtract 10 from both sides: $50 = 3b$, then divide by 3: $b = \frac{50}{3}$ metres. (Note: Let's adjust numbers to make it cleaner: Perimeter = 140 metres. Then $140 = 2(3b + 10) \implies 70 = 3b + 10 \implies 60 = 3b \implies b = 20$ metres).
ANSWER: Breadth $b = 20$ metres; Length = $2(20) + 10 = 50$ metres.
DIFFICULTY: medium
## COMPETENCY_QUESTION_2
QUESTION: Two students, Amit and Sneha, are trying to solve $\frac{x}{3} - 4 = 6$. 
Amit says: "Multiply everything by 3 first." 
Sneha says: "Add 4 to both sides first." 
Who is following the correct systematic method, and what will be the complete step-by-step solution using that method?
COMPETENCY: Comparing and justifying mathematical methods
STEPS:
- Step 1: Analyze both students' proposals based on the order of operations. Sneha's method undoes the subtraction of 4 first by adding 4 to both sides, which is correct. Amit's method of multiplying by 3 first would require multiplying *every* term on both sides by 3 ($x - 12 = 18$), which works if applied correctly, but adding 4 first is more direct.
- Step 2: Follow Sneha's method: Add 4 to both sides of $\frac{x}{3} - 4 = 6 \implies \frac{x}{3} = 6 + 4 \implies \frac{x}{3} = 10$.
- Step 3: Undo division by 3 by multiplying both sides by 3: $x = 10 \times 3 = 30$.
- Step 4: Verify the solution: $\frac{30}{3} - 4 = 10 - 4 = 6$.
ANSWER: Sneha's method is the standard systematic approach (undoing addition/subtraction before multiplication/division). The solution is $x = 30$.
DIFFICULTY: hard
