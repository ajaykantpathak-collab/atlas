The way climate shapes culture and society refers to how long-term weather patterns, temperature ranges, and rainfall distribution directly influence human clothing, housing architecture, food habits, festivals, and social lifestyles across different regions of India.

## COMMON_MISCONCEPTION
Students often believe that cultural practices like food, clothing, and festivals are chosen entirely by random tradition or fashion, failing to recognize that these traditions originated as practical survival strategies shaped by regional climate and physical geography over centuries.
