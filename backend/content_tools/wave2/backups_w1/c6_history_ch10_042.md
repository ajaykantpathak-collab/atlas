## ELI5
The Revolt of 1857 opens in Meerut that May: sepoys refusing cartridges greased against their faith, marching to Delhi to raise the last Mughal, Bahadur Shah Zafar, as their leader. Sepoys lit the fuse, but peasants, artisans and displaced rulers fed the flame - the first countrywide shake of Company rule, remembered as the first war of independence.

## DEFINITION
The Revolt of 1857 was a major, widespread uprising by Indian soldiers (sepoys) and various sections of society against the control and policies of the British East India Company. It is often remembered as the first significant attempt by Indians to challenge British colonial authority.

## WALKTHROUGH
Have you ever felt so frustrated by a rule that you decided to stand up against it with your friends? The Revolt of 1857 started in a similar way, when Indian soldiers in the British army felt their deep-seated religious beliefs were being insulted. They refused to use new rifle cartridges because they heard they were greased with cow and pig fat, which was offensive to both Hindu and Muslim soldiers. This anger spread quickly from the army barracks in Meerut to other parts of India. The soldiers rode to Delhi to show they were serious by asking the last Mughal Emperor, Bahadur Shah Zafar, to lead them. By understanding this, you see that the revolt wasn't just a sudden fight; it was a collective reaction to a lack of respect for Indian traditions.

## COMMON_MISCONCEPTION
Students often think the revolt was purely a "military mutiny" about gun parts. In reality, while the cartridges were the immediate trigger, the soldiers were also frustrated by broader colonial policies. Focusing only on the grease makes it seem like a small accident rather than a planned protest against foreign rule.
