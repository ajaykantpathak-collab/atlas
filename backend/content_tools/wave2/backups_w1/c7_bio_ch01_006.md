## ELI5
One minute your brother is laughing at a video, the next he slams the door over a small remark. Adolescent mood swings are not bad manners; they are chemistry. Hormonal fluctuations act on the developing brain - especially its emotional centres - producing intense feelings, sharp self-consciousness, and a tug-of-war between craving independence and craving acceptance. The feelings are involuntary; the responses can be learned. That distinction is the whole coping toolkit.

## DEFINITION
The psychological and physiological shift during puberty characterized by mood swings, heightened self-consciousness, and a strong desire for independence, driven by hormonal fluctuations acting on the developing brain.
