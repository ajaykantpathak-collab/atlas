## ELI5
Dark clouds in the morning, so you pack an umbrella — you just checked the weather. Planning a Himalayan trip in winter, you pack woolens without looking at any sky — you trusted the climate. Weather is the atmosphere’s mood right now, at this place, this hour; climate is the personality a place builds up over a long period, typically thirty years or more. A snapshot versus an album: today’s rain is weather, ‘it always rains in July here’ is climate. Look out the window for one, look at the record book for the other.

## DEFINITION
Weather is the short-term state of the atmosphere at a specific time and place, while climate is the average pattern of weather conditions in a region observed over a long period (typically 30 years or more).

## WALKTHROUGH
Imagine you are packing your school bag in the morning. If you see dark clouds, you pack an umbrella—that is checking the *weather*. Now, if you are planning a trip to the Himalayas in winter, you pack heavy woolens because you know the region is generally cold—that is considering the *climate*. Begin by observing the sky outside your window right now; that immediate change is weather. Next, think about your hometown's general personality—is it always hot, or does it have a long rainy season? That "personality" is the climate, which is built by adding up thousands of days of weather. The key insight is that weather is a "snapshot" of today, while climate is the "album" of many years.
