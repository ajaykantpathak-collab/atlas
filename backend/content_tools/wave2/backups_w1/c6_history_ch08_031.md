## ELI5
The Sikh kingdom's chapter is a story of joining: after the tenth Guru, Punjab's militant bands - the misls - ran their own corners, until Ranjit Singh gathered the pieces into one sovereign state at the close of the eighteenth century, with Lahore as its capital. Scattered bands in, one empire out; the unification is the headline.

## DEFINITION
The Sikh Kingdom was a powerful regional state in Punjab established by Ranjit Singh, which grew from a collection of decentralized military bands called *misls* into a unified, sovereign empire.

## WALKTHROUGH
Imagine if your school was divided into ten different sports teams, each playing by its own rules and controlling different corners of the playground. What would happen if a single leader convinced all those teams to stop competing against each other and instead work together as one giant, powerful school team? This is exactly how the Sikh Kingdom began. You start by understanding that after the 10th Guru, the Sikh community organized into independent military groups called *misls*. Then, you see how Ranjit Singh acted like the ultimate "team captain," bringing these separate *misls* together to form one strong, united empire centered in Lahore. The key insight is that unity transforms small, scattered groups into a regional power.

**Takeaway:** Ranjit Singh unified Punjab's misls into a centralized Sikh empire with its capital at Lahore. **In the exam:** in Sikh questions, run the sequence - misls divided, Ranjit Singh unifies, Lahore crowned the centre. **Check yourself:** merge the walkthrough's playground teams - one captain, one set of rules is the unification in miniature. **Why students miss it:** students read the misls as kingdoms already - the scattered bands are a no to that; unity is what Ranjit Singh added. ## COMMON_MISCONCEPTION
Students often confuse the Sikh Gurus with the political rulers like Ranjit Singh. They assume that because the religion was founded by a Guru, all subsequent leaders were religious heads. It is crucial to distinguish between the religious leadership of the Gurus and the political leadership of Ranjit Singh who unified the state.
## ON_FAILURE
First, ask the student to draw a line down a page: label one side "Religious Gurus" and the other "Political Leaders." Guide them to place Guru Nanak and Guru Gobind Singh on the religious side. Then, explain that Ranjit Singh was a secular ruler who focused on geography and governance, not on religious doctrine.

## COMPETENCY_QUESTION_1

COMPETENCY: Historical Empathy and Role Identification
STEPS:
- 1: Identify the 'before' boss (a local *misl* commander).
- 2: Identify the 'after' boss (Ranjit Singh or his central state).

## COMPETENCY_QUESTION_2
QUESTION: Compare the role of the 10th Guru in establishing the Khalsa with the role of Ranjit Singh in establishing the Empire. How do they differ?
COMPETENCY: Comparative Analysis
STEPS:
- 1: Define the role of the 10th Guru (spiritual/brotherhood foundation).
- 2: Define the role of Ranjit Singh (political/territorial unification).
- 3: Contrast the two roles.
ANSWER: The 10th Guru created the identity and brotherhood (the Khalsa) that kept the Sikhs united in spirit. Ranjit Singh took that united identity and organized it into a formal, land-based government. One created the "people," the other created the "state."
DIFFICULTY: hard
