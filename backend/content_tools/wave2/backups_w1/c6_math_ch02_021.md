## ELI5
Every move has an undo button: ride the lift up a few floors and only the same number of floors down parks you back at the lobby. The additive inverse is that return move - the opposite of an integer - and the pair always sums to zero. A debt and the credit that clears it behave identically: two opposite entries that cancel, leaving the balance resting at zero.

## DEFINITION
The additive inverse of an integer is another integer that, when added to the original, results in a sum of zero. It is also known as the opposite or negation of the number.
## COGNITIVE_LEVEL

## MASTERY_CRITERIA
The student can identify the additive inverse of any given integer, verify it using the addition rule, and explain why the sum equals zero in a real-world context like a lift moving between floors.
## SCOPE_BOUNDARY
This node covers the definition of additive inverse and its relationship with zero within the set of integers. It excludes multiplicative inverses, group theory, and field properties.
## WALKTHROUGH
Imagine you are at the ground floor (Floor 0) of a building and you take the elevator up 3 floors. How would you return to the ground floor? You would need to press a button to go down 3 floors. In mathematics, we call the movement '+3' and the return movement '-3'. When we combine them, (+3) + (-3) = 0. This shows that every step "up" has a perfect "down" partner that brings us back to our starting point of zero. The key insight is that an additive inverse acts like a "cancelling" force that resets a value to nothingness.

**Takeaway:** the additive inverse is the opposite integer - equal size, opposite direction - and the two always add to zero. **In the exam:** in inverse questions, name the opposite explicitly and prove it by adding the pair to show a sum of zero. **Check yourself:** replay the lift ride: up from the ground floor, then the matching press that returns you to the lobby. **Why students miss it:** students read 'inverse' as 'the same number' - the lobby rule is a no to that; a move and its undo must point in opposite directions. Rehearse the exam move once more - opposite, add, zero - and the answer arranges itself in exam-ready order with nothing left to chance.

## COMMON_MISCONCEPTION
Students often believe that the additive inverse of a negative number must also be negative. For example, they might say the additive inverse of -8 is -8. This happens because they associate "inverse" only with the action of "making something negative," failing to see it as a "balancing" action that must result in zero.
## ON_FAILURE

Explain that since -8 + 8 = 0, the number +8 is the inverse. Repeat this with a positive example to show the pattern.

## WORKED_EXAMPLE_1
PROBLEM: A submarine is at a depth of 200 metres below sea level. It then rises 200 metres. What is its final position relative to sea level?
STEPS:
- step 1: Identify the starting position as -200 (since it is below sea level).
- step 2: Identify the movement as +200 (since it is rising).
- step 3: Apply the additive inverse rule: (-200) + (+200).
- step 4: Compute the sum: (-200) + 200 = 0.
- step 5: Interpret the result: The submarine is at 0 metres, which is exactly at sea level.
REASONING: Since the upward movement is the exact inverse of the downward depth, the two values must cancel each other out to return to the reference point.
ANSWER: 0 metres (Sea Level)
## WORKED_EXAMPLE_2
PROBLEM: You have a debt of ₹450 in a store. You receive a gift of ₹450. After you pay off the debt, what is your remaining balance?
STEPS:
- step 1: Identify the debt as -₹450.
- step 2: Identify the gift as +₹450.
- step 3: Form the expression: (-450) + 450.
- step 4: Calculate the total: 0.
- step 5: The result represents the remaining balance.
REASONING: Paying off a debt is the process of adding the inverse of the debt amount to the total, effectively neutralizing the negative balance.
ANSWER: ₹0
## WORKED_EXAMPLE_3
PROBLEM: From a starting temperature of -12°C, the temperature rises by 12°C. Then, it drops by 5°C. What is the final temperature?
STEPS:
- step 1: Start at -12.
- step 2: Add the inverse (+12) to reach 0.
- step 3: Expression: (-12 + 12) + (-5).
- step 4: Simplify: 0 + (-5).
- step 5: Final position is -5.
REASONING: The first two operations cancel each other out because they are additive inverses, simplifying the problem to just the final change of -5.
ANSWER: -5°C
