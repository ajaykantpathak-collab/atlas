## ELI5
One piece tries to reunite the north: Harshavardhana, ruling 606 to 647 CE from Kannauj. The city sits mid-Gangetic, on the river trade - hence every neighbour's ambition and the era's wars. Two witnesses record him: Bana Bhatta, the court poet whose Harshacharita flatters, and Hiuen Tsang, the Chinese pilgrim who studied under him and reports like a foreign journalist. ## DEFINITION
The rule of Harshavardhana in the 7th century CE (606–647 CE) marked a significant period of political consolidation in Northern India, centered around his capital city of Kannauj, following the decline of the Gupta Empire.

Covers Harshavardhana's rise to power, administration, patronage of art and Buddhism, and the strategic importance of Kannauj. It does NOT cover the later imperial struggles for Kannauj in the Tripartite Struggle of the 8th–10th centuries.

## COMMON_MISCONCEPTION
Students often confuse the political unification achieved by Harshavardhana with the permanent centralization of an empire like the Mauryas or Mughals. They assume that because Harsha controlled Northern India, his empire had a permanent bureaucratic machinery, missing the reality that his rule depended on a network of powerful subordinate allies and regional feudatories (samantas).
## ON_FAILURE
