## ELI5
A polygon is any figure drawn without lifting the pencil: straight segments only, ending where it began. Street signs and floor tiles are a gallery of them. The sides count gives the name, and regularity is a double test - every side the same length and every corner the same size. Fail either and the polygon is honestly irregular, but still a polygon.

## DEFINITION
A polygon is a simple, closed, two-dimensional figure formed by three or more straight line segments. A polygon is "regular" if all its sides are equal in length and all its interior angles are equal; otherwise, it is "irregular."

## WALKTHROUGH
Have you ever noticed the shape of a street sign or the tiles on your floor? To start, imagine drawing any shape on paper using only straight lines without lifting your pencil, finishing exactly where you started. That closed shape is a polygon. If you find that every side you drew is the exact same length and every corner "bend" is the same width, you have drawn a regular polygon. If the sides or corners look different, it is irregular. The key insight is that the "name" of the polygon depends entirely on how many straight paths (sides) you took to close the shape.

**Takeaway:** polygons are closed figures of straight segments; regular means equal sides and equal angles together, otherwise irregular. **In the exam:** in classification questions, check closure and straightness first, then count sides for the name, then run the regularity double-test. **Check yourself:** trace the walkthrough's tiles and signs - closed, straight-sided, and named by the count. **Why students miss it:** students refuse irregular figures the polygon name - the floor tiles are a no to that; unequal sides make it irregular, not illegal. ## COMMON_MISCONCEPTION
Students often believe that "regular" just means "symmetric" or "looks normal." For example, they might call a rectangle a regular quadrilateral because it looks neat and balanced. They miss that "regular" strictly requires all sides to have the same length, which a non-square rectangle lacks.
## ON_FAILURE
Ask the student to take a ruler and measure the sides of their shape. Say: "If you measure every side of a square, you find they are all 3cm. If you measure a rectangle, you find two sides are 3cm and two are 5cm. Does a rectangle have all equal sides?" This forces them to focus on measurement rather than visual intuition.

## WORKED_EXAMPLE_1
PROBLEM: A park is shaped like a polygon with 6 sides of 10 metres each. All its interior angles are equal. Is it a regular or irregular hexagon? Calculate the sum of its interior angles.
STEPS:
- step 1: Identify sides (n=6) and confirm side lengths (all 10m).
- step 2: Confirm angles are equal, so it is a regular hexagon.
- step 3: Apply the sum formula: (n-2) x 180.
- step 4: Substitute n=6: (6-2) x 180 = 4 x 180.
- step 5: Calculate result: 720 degrees.
REASONING: Since all sides are equal and angles are equal, it fits the definition of regular. The formula uses the number of sides to determine the total interior rotation.
ANSWER: Regular hexagon; 720 degrees.
## WORKED_EXAMPLE_2
PROBLEM: An architect draws a 4-sided plot where two sides are 20 metres and two sides are 15 metres. The angles are all 90 degrees. Is it a regular quadrilateral?
STEPS:
- step 1: Count sides (n=4). It is a quadrilateral.
- step 2: Compare sides: 20m, 20m, 15m, 15m.
- step 3: Check criteria: For a regular polygon, all sides must be equal.
- step 4: Conclusion: Since 20 is not equal to 15, the sides are not equal.
- step 5: Determine type: It is an irregular quadrilateral (a rectangle).
REASONING: Regularity is an "all or nothing" property; missing the side-length requirement makes it irregular even if the angles are perfect.
ANSWER: Irregular quadrilateral.
## WORKED_EXAMPLE_3
PROBLEM: A garden is a regular pentagon. If one side is 5 metres, what is the total length of the fence needed? How many degrees is the sum of its interior angles?
STEPS:
- step 1: Identify shape as regular pentagon (n=5).
- step 2: Calculate perimeter: 5 sides x 5 metres = 25 metres.
- step 3: Identify formula for interior angles: (n-2) x 180.
- step 4: Substitute n=5: (5-2) x 180 = 3 x 180.
- step 5: Calculate sum: 540 degrees.
REASONING: Perimeter is the sum of sides, and angle sum is derived from the number of sides. Both rely on the properties of a regular pentagon.
ANSWER: 25 metres; 540 degrees.

## COMPETENCY_QUESTION_2
QUESTION: A carpenter says, "Any polygon with 4 equal sides is a square." Is this statement always true? Use the concept of regular/irregular polygons to justify your answer.
COMPETENCY: Error identification and correction
STEPS:
- step 1: Understand the claim (equal sides = square).
- step 2: Recall definition of square (a regular quadrilateral).
- step 3: Consider a rhombus (4 equal sides, but angles can be different).
- step 4: Conclude that a rhombus is an irregular quadrilateral (unless it's a square).
