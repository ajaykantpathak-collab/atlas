## ELI5
Independence and partition arrived in the same August of 1947: the Union Jack came down on the fifteenth, and the map came apart along with it - the Muslim League under Jinnah insisting Muslims were a separate nation, the Congress accepting the division as freedom's bitter price. Two countries were born where one flag had stood, and the two events share one date.

## DEFINITION
Independence and Partition refer to the events of August 1947, when India became a self-governing nation while simultaneously being divided into two separate countries, India and Pakistan.
## COGNITIVE_LEVEL
understand
## MASTERY_CRITERIA
The student must be able to identify the key parties involved (Congress and Muslim League) and explain that partition was the result of differing political views on nationhood leading to the end of British rule.
## SCOPE_BOUNDARY
This node covers the political transition of 1947 and the creation of two nations; it does not cover the specifics of post-partition resettlement, the princely states, or the drafting of the Constitution.
## WALKTHROUGH
Imagine your school is about to become independent and manage itself, but two groups of students have such different ideas about how to run it that they decide to split into two separate schools instead. To understand 1947, think of it as a house being divided because the people living there could not agree on how to share the space. We start by looking at the goal of freedom from British rule, which everyone wanted. Then, we look at why the Muslim League, led by Jinnah, felt they needed a separate land. Finally, we see why the Congress leaders, Nehru and Patel, accepted this tough choice to ensure the country could finally be free. The key insight is that independence was not just about Britain leaving; it was about defining the borders of the new nations.

## COMMON_MISCONCEPTION
