## ELI5
Taste a jalebi and you are reading history: migrant communities did not just settle in India, they sweetened it. Parsis brought fire-temples and enterprise; Jews and Syrian Christians their ancient prayers; Muslim builders their domes and arches - and Indian artisans answered with lintels and lotus carving. Recipes, scripts, inlaid marble, new dialects: centuries of settlement left the country's everyday richer. Contribution, not conquest, is this chapter's ledger.
