## DEFINITION
This topic explores the monumental art and architecture developed during the Maurya Empire (c. 321–185 BCE), characterized by massive stone sculptures, monolithic polished pillars, rock-cut caves, and early stupas that established early Indian monumental stone traditions. Centered primarily in northern and eastern India under royal patronage—especially during Emperor Ashoka’s reign—Mauryan art reflected imperial power, religious devotion, and political unification.
