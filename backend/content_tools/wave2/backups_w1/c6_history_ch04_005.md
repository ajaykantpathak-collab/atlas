## ELI5
The Great Bath of Mohenjo-daro is the timeline's oldest grand public tank: baked bricks laid tight, sealed with bitumen so the water stayed put, steps dipping in from two sides, and a well standing by to fill it. In the city's story it marks the moment waterworks became architecture - a community pool engineered before most of the world had paved a street.

## DEFINITION
The Great Bath of Mohenjo-daro was a large, rectangular public tank constructed from baked bricks and waterproofed with bitumen, designed to hold water for community use. It represents the advanced engineering and organized urban planning of the Harappan civilization.
