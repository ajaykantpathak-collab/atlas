## ELI5
The Himalayas do not wall India off from Nepal - they handshake. Ranges tower on the physical map, but the borderlands fade seamlessly into open plains, rivers born in the mountains flow south together, and centuries of open movement mixed festivals, pilgrimage and families. India-Nepal is a border that geography softened: the mountains connect here as much as they divide.

## DEFINITION
The deep cultural and geographic ties between India and Nepal are shaped by their shared Himalayan border, interconnected river systems flowing from the mountains to the plains, and centuries of open socio-religious, linguistic, and familial exchanges.
