## ELI5
The Rajput chapter runs from the eighth to the sixteenth century: independent warrior houses across northern and western India, bound not by one crown but by a shared code - honour, valour, loyalty to clan. Prithviraj Chauhan is the era's most famous name, and the houses fought each other as readily as any invader - a chequered map of pride.

## DEFINITION
The Rajput kingdoms were a collection of independent warrior-led Hindu states that emerged in northern and western India between the 8th and 16th centuries, characterized by a strong culture of chivalry and defensive warfare.

## COMMON_MISCONCEPTION
Students often assume that all Rajputs belonged to one single ruling family or dynasty. In reality, "Rajput" was a broad category for many different clans (like the Chauhans, Rathores, and Sisodias) who were rivals as often as they were allies.
