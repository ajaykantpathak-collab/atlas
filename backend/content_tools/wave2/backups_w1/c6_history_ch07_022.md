## ELI5
The Sultanate's three centuries are a tower of five blocks, each a dynasty in turn: Mamluk, Khalji, Tughlaq, Sayyid, Lodi - from 1206 to 1526. Know the order and any date drops into its right block: Razia in the first, the Khaljis around the turn of the fourteenth century, the Lodi line falling last to the Mughals.

## DEFINITION
The Delhi Sultanate refers to the period between 1206 and 1526 CE when five successive dynasties ruled parts of India from their capital, Delhi. These dynasties were the Mamluk (Slave), Khalji, Tughlaq, Sayyid, and Lodi dynasties.

## WALKTHROUGH
Imagine you are building a tower made of five different colored blocks, where each block represents a different ruling family. Just as you need to place the bottom block first, history requires us to know which family ruled first in Delhi. We start with the Slave dynasty, founded by Qutbuddin Aibak, and move forward through the Khalji dynasty. Think of these dynasties as shifts in power where the "boss" of the empire changes, but the capital remains the same. By memorizing the order of these five families, you gain a map of 300 years of Delhi's history.

**Takeaway:** five dynasties ruled Delhi in order - Mamluk, Khalji, Tughlaq, Sayyid, Lodi - and the order is the timeline's map. **In the exam:** in dynasty questions, place the date in the right block of the five-block tower before answering. **Check yourself:** stack the walkthrough's coloured blocks - each family is one block, bottom to top, 1206 to 1526. **Why students miss it:** students merge the blocks into one house - the five-crest sequence is a no to that; the tower changes colour five times. ## COMMON_MISCONCEPTION
Students often think the Delhi Sultanate was a single "kingdom" ruled by one king for 300 years. They fail to realize that a "dynasty" change often meant a violent or political shift where one royal family was replaced by another. This leads to confusion when they see multiple names like "Aibak" and "Khalji" as if they were related.
