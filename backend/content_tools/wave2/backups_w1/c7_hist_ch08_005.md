## ELI5
Christianity reached Kerala, tradition says, while the Roman empire was young: St. Thomas the Apostle is held to have landed on the Malabar coast in 52 CE, centuries before any European power appeared. Sea lanes to ports like Muziris carried the first congregations; their churches grew wooden, lamp-lit, built like the temples next door. Faith arrived as a traveller and dressed like a neighbour - integration long before colonisation.
