## ELI5
Which is warmer, minus five degrees or minus two? Minus two - and the elevator explains why: ground is zero, minus two is one flight up from minus five. Comparing integers is pure number-line position: right is greater, so zero beats every negative and minus one beats minus one hundred. Bigger-looking digits in the negative zone mean deeper, not greater.

## DEFINITION
Comparing and ordering integers involves determining the relative size of whole numbers and their negative counterparts by their position on the number line, where numbers to the right are always greater than those to the left.
## COGNITIVE_LEVEL
understand
## MASTERY_CRITERIA
The student must be able to identify which of two integers is greater by visualizing their position on a number line or using the building floor analogy (e.g., higher floors are greater than lower floors).
## SCOPE_BOUNDARY
This node covers ordering positive integers, negative integers, and zero on a number line. It does not cover operations like addition or subtraction, nor does it include rational numbers like fractions or decimals.
## WALKTHROUGH
Imagine you are in an elevator in a building with a basement. If you are standing at the "Welcome Hall" (Ground Floor or 0), and you go down two floors, you reach -2. If your friend goes down five floors, they reach -5. Which one of you is standing on a "higher" floor relative to the other? Since -2 is closer to the ground level than -5, -2 is actually the higher position. On a number line, this means -2 is to the right of -5, so -2 > -5. The key insight is that for negative numbers, the one that looks "smaller" (closer to zero) is actually the greater one.

**Takeaway:** integer comparison is pure position - right on the line is greater, so zero outranks every negative and closer-to-zero wins among them. **In the exam:** in comparison questions, convert each number to a floor, then read the lift button order bottom to top. **Check yourself:** stand in the walkthrough's basement and climb toward zero, naming each stop as it grows. **Why students miss it:** students let the bigger digit win - one hundred below ground is a no to that; depth is not height, position is. ## COMMON_MISCONCEPTION
Students often treat negative numbers like positive ones, assuming that because 10 is larger than 5, -10 must be larger than -5. This happens because they see the "10" and "5" and ignore the negative sign, which acts as a "direction" indicator on the number line.
## ON_FAILURE
Draw a vertical number line (like a thermometer or building floors). Ask the student to place 0 in the middle. Ask them to point to -5 and -10. Ask, "If you are standing at -5, do you need to go up or down to reach -10?" Since they must go down, -10 is lower (smaller) than -5.

## WORKED_EXAMPLE_1
PROBLEM: A thermometer in Leh reads -7°C at night and -2°C in the afternoon. Which temperature is higher?
STEPS:
- step 1: Identify the two values: -7 and -2.
- step 2: Visualize the number line where 0 is the freezing point.
- step 3: Locate -2 and -7 to the left of 0.
- step 4: Identify that -2 is closer to 0 than -7.
- step 5: Conclude that -2 is to the right of -7.
REASONING: Since -2 is closer to the reference point (0) than -7, it represents a warmer (higher) temperature.
ANSWER: -2°C is higher.
## WORKED_EXAMPLE_2
PROBLEM: Two bank accounts show balances of -₹500 and -₹200. Which account has a higher balance?
STEPS:
- step 1: Identify the two values: -500 and -200.
- step 2: Compare the absolute values (500 and 200).
- step 3: Apply the rule that for negative numbers, the one with the smaller absolute value is greater.
- step 4: Since 200 < 500, then -200 > -500.
REASONING: A debt of ₹200 is less severe than a debt of ₹500, meaning the -200 account is "closer" to zero.
ANSWER: The account with -₹200 has a higher balance.
## WORKED_EXAMPLE_3
PROBLEM: Arrange the following depths (in metres below sea level) from shallowest to deepest: -15m, -5m, -20m, -2m.
STEPS:
- step 1: List the numbers: -15, -5, -20, -2.
- step 2: Shallowest means closest to sea level (0), which is the greatest integer.
- step 3: Deepest means furthest from 0 to the left, which is the smallest integer.
- step 4: Order them: -2 > -5 > -15 > -20.
- step 5: Shallowest is -2, then -5, -15, -20.
REASONING: In depths, a smaller negative number indicates a position closer to the surface (0), making it "shallower" than a larger negative number.
ANSWER: -2m, -5m, -15m, -20m.

## COMPETENCY_QUESTION_2
QUESTION: Compare the following pairs using > or <: 
a) -15 ___ -12 
b) -9 ___ 0 
c) -1 ___ -100 
Justify your answers.
COMPETENCY: Comparing and justifying
STEPS:
- step 1: Evaluate -15 vs -12; -12 is to the right of -15, so -15 < -12.
- step 2: Evaluate -9 vs 0; 0 is to the right of all negative numbers, so -9 < 0.
- step 3: Evaluate -1 vs -100; -1 is closer to 0, so -1 > -100.
- step 4: Verify against the rule: numbers to the right are always greater.
ANSWER: a) -15 < -12, b) -9 < 0, c) -1 > -100. Justification: On the number line, the number positioned further to the right is always the greater value.
