## ELI5
Look out of a Mumbai window on a July morning - heavy clouds, street puddles - and you are reading weather: today's mood. Average thirty years of such mornings and you hold climate: the city's personality. Weather is the day-to-day atmospheric condition of one place; climate is the long-period pattern the weather keeps returning to. Mumbai's July mood is monsoon; Mumbai's climate is coastal wetness, decade after decade.

## DEFINITION
Weather refers to the day-to-day atmospheric conditions of a specific place, whereas climate represents the average weather pattern of a region calculated over a long period, typically 30 years or more.
## COGNITIVE_LEVEL

## MASTERY_CRITERIA

## COMMON_MISCONCEPTION
Students often think that if a particular summer is unusually cool or rainy, the permanent climate of that region has changed. For example, a student might experience heavy unseasonal rainfall in Rajasthan in March and conclude that Rajasthan is no longer a desert climate, confusing a temporary weather anomaly with permanent climatic zones.
