## ELI5
A broken streetlight, a heap of uncollected garbage, a clogged drain — no single neighbour fixes these; the city has a team for it: the municipality, the town's own elected local government. Villages have Panchayats; cities have this. Its job list is the unglamorous backbone of urban life — streetlights burning, drains flowing, garbage moving, water running. Size decides the name: a huge city elects a Corporation, a smaller town a Council, but the work is the same — elected representatives, met on your own street, organising the civic services that keep a crowded place clean, safe and liveable. The city runs because its local government shows up daily.

## DEFINITION
Municipalities are the urban local self-government institutions responsible for providing essential civic services and managing the daily affairs of towns and cities. They function through elected representatives to ensure clean, safe, and organized living conditions for city dwellers.

PROBLEM: A city of 5 million people needs to manage a major sewage crisis. Who handles this and why?
STEPS:
- Identify if the area is rural or urban: It is a city (Urban).
- Identify the correct institution: Large cities use a Municipal Corporation.
- Identify the leader: The Mayor heads this body.
- Connect to function: Municipalities are responsible for sewage and drainage.
REASONING: Since the scale is a "large city," the administrative body must be a Corporation, which has the power to manage large-scale civic infrastructure.
ANSWER: The Municipal Corporation, led by a Mayor, handles this because sewage management is a core duty of urban local government.

## COMPETENCY_QUESTION_2
QUESTION: Why is the 'elected' nature of the Municipal Council important for citizens?
COMPETENCY: Analytical Reasoning
STEPS:
- Define 'elected': Citizens choose the person.
- Analyze the benefit: Elected members are accountable to the voters.
- Consider the alternative: If members were appointed, they might not listen to citizens.
