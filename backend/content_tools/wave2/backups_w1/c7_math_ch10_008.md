## ELI5
The market shopkeeper's ledger shows a 200-rupee loss spread evenly over 4 days - a negative divided by a positive, and each day's share is a fresh loss. Division inherits multiplication's sign rules: like signs give a positive quotient, unlike signs a negative one. The best habit is checking by multiplying back - quotient times divisor must rebuild the dividend, signs included. Share the sizes first, decide the sign second, and the ledger always balances.

## DEFINITION
The division of integers follows specific sign rules: dividing two integers with like signs (both positive or both negative) yields a positive quotient, while dividing two integers with unlike signs (one positive and one negative) yields a negative quotient. This is expressed mathematically as $(+) \div (+) = (+)$, $(-)\div (-) = (+)$, $(+) \div (-) = (-)$, and $(-)\div (+) = (-)$, provided the divisor is not zero.

## COMMON_MISCONCEPTION
Students often apply addition rules to division, mistakenly believing that two negative signs combine to make the result more negative (e.g., thinking $(-24) \div (-3) = -8$ because they treat it like adding $-24$ and $-3$). Another frequent error is ignoring one of the negative signs in a multi-step division or expression, treating the operation as if it were multiplying or dividing whole numbers without tracking the negative polarity of both the dividend and divisor.

## WORKED_EXAMPLE_1
PROBLEM: A submarine is descending into the Indian Ocean. Over 5 equal time intervals, its total change in depth is −350 metres. What is the integer representing its change in depth per interval?
STEPS:
- step 1: Identify the total change in depth (dividend) as −350 metres and the number of intervals (divisor) as $+5$.
- step 2: Set up the integer division expression: $(-350) \div (+5)$.
- step 3: Apply the sign rule for unlike signs (negative divided by positive), which states the quotient will be negative: $(-)\div(+) = (-)$.
- step 4: Divide the absolute values: $350 \div 5 = 70$.
- step 5: Combine the sign and absolute value to get the final result with units: $-70$ metres per interval.
REASONING: Since the total depth change is negative (descent) and is distributed equally over a positive number of time intervals, each interval must represent an equal negative displacement, requiring the division of a negative integer by a positive integer.
ANSWER: −70 metres per interval
## WORKED_EXAMPLE_2
PROBLEM: In a science experiment, a student records a total temperature drop of −48°C inside a freezer chamber over 6 hours. If the temperature dropped by an equal amount every hour, calculate the hourly temperature change integer.
STEPS:
- step 1: Identify the total temperature drop as −48 and the number of hours as $+6$.
- step 2: Set up the division expression: $(-48) \div (+6)$.
- step 3: Determine the sign of the quotient by noting that a negative integer divided by a positive integer yields a negative sign.
- step 4: Compute the division of the whole number parts: $48 \div 6 = 8$.
- step 5: Write the final signed integer with its unit: $-8$°C per hour.
REASONING: Distributing a total drop in temperature over elapsed time is modeled by division, where the negative total divided by the positive time yields the negative rate of change per hour.
ANSWER: −8°C per hour
## WORKED_EXAMPLE_3
PROBLEM: Simplify the complex integer expression involving multiple sign rules: $[(-144) \div (-12)] \div [(-36) \div 9]$.
STEPS:
- step 1: Evaluate the first bracket by dividing two negative integers: $(-144) \div (-12) = +12$ because like signs give a positive quotient.
- step 2: Evaluate the second bracket by dividing a negative integer by a positive integer: $(-36) \div 9 = -4$ because unlike signs give a negative quotient.
- step 3: Substitute the bracket results back into the main expression: $(+12) \div (-4)$.
- step 4: Apply the division sign rule for the remaining expression: a positive integer divided by a negative integer yields a negative quotient $(+) \div (-) = (-)$.
- step 5: Divide the absolute values: $12 \div 4 = 3$, attaching the negative sign to get $-3$.
REASONING: Order of operations requires evaluating parenthetical bracket expressions first using their respective sign rules, then applying the final sign rule to the resulting simplified integers.
ANSWER: −3
