## ELI5
Chronology of an army: in the Vedic age, soldiers went home after the raiding season; by the mahajanapada centuries, around 600 to 300 BCE, kings kept soldiers on the payroll all year. Standing armies needed standing money, so taxation regularised itself to pay salaries, iron armour and war elephants. Stone walls like Rajgir's rose to guard the treasury that paid it all. Pay, steel and walls arrived together - the fiscal state in armour.

## DEFINITION
The development of organized military structures, standing armies, and defensive fortifications by early kingdoms and Mahajanapadas (c. 600–300 BCE) in the Gangetic plains to protect territories, expand borders, and collect regular taxes.
