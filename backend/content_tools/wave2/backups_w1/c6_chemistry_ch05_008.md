## ELI5
Snap a pencil and the change is instant; leave an iron nail outside and the change needs days; watch a river and it needs thousands of years to carve its valley. Nature runs at both speeds. Slow natural changes — rusting, weathering, erosion — are always working, yet so gradually that no single moment shows progress: the gate looks the same each morning yet is a little weaker each year. That is the trick to catch: compare the before and after across a long duration, and changes invisible in real time stand out clearly. Mountains crumble, iron greys, rivers deepen — patience is the only lens that sees them move.

## DEFINITION
Slow natural changes are gradual transformations in the environment, such as weathering, erosion, and rusting, that occur over long periods and are often imperceptible in real-time. These changes represent permanent alterations to the structure or chemical composition of materials.
