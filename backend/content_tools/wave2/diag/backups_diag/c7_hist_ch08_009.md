## DEFINITION
Vasudhaiva Kutumbakam — 'the world is one family' — is an ancient Indian idea, drawn from the Maha Upanishad, that treats all humanity as a single household. It is an ethic of welcome for the guest, restraint towards the stranger, and peace as the default between communities and kingdoms; Indian rulers cited it, and modern India placed it at the heart of its diplomacy, from Non-Aligned Movement platforms to the inscriptions in its parliament house. As a philosophy it argues that narrow loyalty to 'my people first' shrinks the mind, while the wise live at ease with the whole world as kin.
