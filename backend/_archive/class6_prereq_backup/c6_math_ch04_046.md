## DEFINITION
Addition and subtraction of decimals is the process of combining or finding the difference between numbers containing a decimal point, performed by aligning the decimal points vertically to ensure digits of the same place value are added or subtracted together.
## COGNITIVE_LEVEL
apply
## MASTERY_CRITERIA
The student must be able to correctly align decimal points, pad with placeholder zeros where necessary, and perform the arithmetic operations to reach the correct result.
## SCOPE_BOUNDARY
This node covers addition and subtraction of decimals with consistent place values. It does not cover multiplication, division, algebraic expressions, or significant figures.
## WALKTHROUGH
Imagine you are at the market and you buy two items: a notebook for ₹15.50 and a pen for ₹4.75. To find the total, you cannot just add 15.50 and 4.75 as if they were whole numbers because the decimal point marks the boundary between rupees and paise. You must write them one above the other so that the decimal points are in a perfectly straight line, like a column. If one number has fewer digits after the point (like 4.7), add a zero (making it 4.70) so the columns match perfectly. Finally, add or subtract as you would with normal whole numbers, then drop the decimal point straight down into your answer.
## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_1
What is the most important rule to follow when setting up a problem to add 2.45 and 12.3?
## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_1
The decimal points must be aligned vertically. A wrong answer like "adding the last digits first" reveals the student is treating decimals like whole numbers and ignoring place value.
## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_2
Why is it helpful to add a zero to 1.5 when you are subtracting it from 2.64?
## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_2
Adding a zero (making it 1.50) gives both numbers the same number of decimal places, making it easier to subtract column by column. A wrong answer suggests the student does not understand that 1.5 is the same as 1.50, which is essential for place value alignment.
## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_3
Calculate 8.4 - 2.15.
## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_3
Step 1: Align points: 8.40 and 2.15. Step 2: Borrow from 4 to make 10-5=5. Step 3: Subtract columns to get 6.25. A wrong answer usually comes from calculating 8.4-2.15 as 8.4-2.1=6.3 and ignoring the 5, or misaligning the place values.
## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_4
If you have 5.0 and 5.000, are they the same value? Explain why this matters for addition.
## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_4
Yes, they are equal because adding zeros to the right of a decimal does not change its value. This matters because it allows us to match the number of digits in different numbers before adding or subtracting.
## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_5
Is 0.7 + 0.3 equal to 0.10? Why or why not?
## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_5
No, it is equal to 1.0. The sum 0.7 + 0.3 = 1.0; a student saying 0.10 is incorrectly treating the decimals like whole numbers (7+3=10) without considering the place value of tenths.
## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_6
Create a problem where you need to subtract 1.25 from 3.0. Show how you would write it down to solve it.
## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_6
The student should write 3.00 - 1.25. A poor answer would be writing 3.0 - 1.25 without the extra zero, which makes the subtraction confusing for the tenths column.
## COMMON_MISCONCEPTION
Students often align numbers to the right (like whole numbers) instead of aligning the decimal point. For example, calculating 2.5 + 1.23 as 3.73 (correct) vs 2.5 + 1.23 = 1.48 (by adding 2 to 1 and 5 to 23). This happens because they forget that 2.5 is actually 2.50.
## ON_FAILURE
If a student fails, have them draw a vertical line through the decimal points of both numbers. Tell them: "The decimal point is like a button on a shirt; if you don't line up the buttons, the shirt won't close correctly." Then, show them how to fill empty columns with '0' as a placeholder until the columns look even.

## WORKED_EXAMPLE_1
PROBLEM: Rahul bought a bottle of milk for ₹45.50 and a packet of biscuits for ₹12.75. What is the total amount he spent?
STEPS:
- Step 1: Identify amounts: 45.50 and 12.75.
- Step 2: Align the decimal points vertically: 45.50 under 12.75.
- Step 3: Add the hundredths column: 0 + 5 = 5.
- Step 4: Add the tenths column: 5 + 7 = 12 (write 2, carry over 1 to the ones place).
- Step 5: Add the ones and tens columns: 5 + 2 + 1 (carry) = 8; 4 + 1 = 5.
REASONING: By aligning the decimal points, we ensure that paise are added to paise and rupees to rupees. Carrying over the 1 ensures the place value rules are respected.
ANSWER: ₹58.25
## WORKED_EXAMPLE_2
PROBLEM: A tailor had a piece of cloth 8.5 metres long. He used 3.25 metres for a shirt. How much cloth is left?
STEPS:
- Step 1: Identify total length (8.5m) and used length (3.25m).
- Step 2: Align points: 8.50 - 3.25.
- Step 3: Subtract hundredths: 0 - 5 (borrow 1 from tenths) = 10 - 5 = 5.
- Step 4: Subtract tenths: 4 - 2 = 2.
- Step 5: Subtract ones: 8 - 3 = 5.
REASONING: We must add the zero to 8.5 to make it 8.50 so that the columns match. Subtracting then follows standard borrowing rules.
ANSWER: 5.25 metres
## WORKED_EXAMPLE_3
PROBLEM: A bucket contains 10 litres of water. If we remove 2.5 litres and then add 1.75 litres, how much water is in the bucket?
STEPS:
- Step 1: Start with 10.00.
- Step 2: Subtract 2.50: 10.00 - 2.50 = 7.50.
- Step 3: Add 1.75: 7.50 + 1.75.
- Step 4: Align columns: 7.50 + 1.75.
- Step 5: Add hundredths: 0 + 5 = 5. Add tenths: 5 + 7 = 12 (carry 1). Add ones: 7 + 1 + 1 = 9.
REASONING: Operations must be done in sequence. We use placeholders (0) to ensure the decimal points remain aligned throughout the multi-step calculation.
ANSWER: 9.25 litres
## COMPETENCY_QUESTION_1
QUESTION: Priya says that 0.4 + 0.25 = 0.29 because she added 4+25. Explain why she is wrong and what the correct answer is.
COMPETENCY: Error identification and correction
STEPS:

- Step 2: Convert 0.4 to 0.40.
- Step 3: Align and add: 0.40 + 0.25.

ANSWER: Priya is wrong because she treated 0.4 as 0.04. The correct way is 0.40 + 0.25 = 0.65.

## COMPETENCY_QUESTION_2
QUESTION: You have three lengths of ribbon: 1.2m, 0.55m, and 2.1m. If you join them all together, what is the total length? Show your alignment.
COMPETENCY: Translating real-world situations to mathematical expressions
STEPS:
- Step 1: Convert all to same decimal places: 1.20, 0.55, 2.10.
- Step 2: Align vertically with decimal points in a line.
- Step 3: Add hundredths: 0+5+0 = 5.
- Step 4: Add tenths: 2+5+1 = 8.
- Step 5: Add ones: 1+0+2 = 3.
ANSWER: The total length is 3.85m. Alignment: 1.20 + 0.55 + 2.10 = 3.85.
