## DEFINITION
A letter is a written message sent from one person to another. An informal letter is written to friends or family in a conversational tone, while a formal letter is written to people in positions of authority (like a school principal or a government official) to request information or express a concern.

## WALKTHROUGH
Imagine you want to tell your cousin about the amazing Dhokra art you saw in class, and then imagine you need to write to your teacher to ask for more information about the National War Memorial. Do you think you would use the same words? To begin, we look at the 'receiver'—if it's a friend, we use 'Dear' and casual language; if it's an official, we use a polite title and structured sentences. We start by choosing our purpose, then pick the appropriate tone, and finally follow the layout. The key insight is that our relationship with the reader determines the "voice" of our writing.
## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_1
What is the main difference between the purpose of a formal letter and an informal letter?
## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_1
An informal letter is for personal communication with friends/family, while a formal letter is for professional or official requests. A wrong answer suggests the student does not distinguish between social and institutional relationships.
## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_2
If you are writing to a friend about a trip to a memorial, should you use complex, strict language or warm, casual language? Why?
## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_2
You should use warm, casual language because the goal is to share personal feelings. A wrong answer shows confusion about the purpose of tone in letter writing.
## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_3
You are writing a request to the head of a local museum for information. How would you start your letter?
## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_3
Start with "Respected Sir/Madam." A wrong answer like "Hi" shows the student is applying an informal greeting to a formal situation.
## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_4
Analyze this sentence: "Hey, give me the info on the memorial right now!" Is this appropriate for a formal letter? Why or why not?
## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_4
No, it is not appropriate because it is rude and demanding. A formal letter should be polite and clear, not bossy.
## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_5
Evaluate which closing is better for an informal letter: "Yours faithfully" or "With love"?
## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_5
"With love" is better for an informal letter. "Yours faithfully" is formal; using it with a friend sounds stiff and strange.
## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_6
Write the opening line for a formal letter requesting details about the National War Memorial.
## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_6
"I am writing to request information regarding the National War Memorial." A poor answer would be too vague or use slang.
## COMMON_MISCONCEPTION
Students often mix up "salutations" (greetings) and "closings." For example, they might write "Dear Principal" (formal) but close with "See you later, bye!" (informal). This happens because they view the letter as one big block rather than a structured piece of communication where the tone must remain consistent from start to finish.
## ON_FAILURE
If the student mixes tones, ask them to read their letter out loud. Ask: "If you were speaking to the Principal, would you really say 'See you later'?" Then, provide a "Formal" vs. "Informal" word bank (e.g., 'Dear' vs 'Respected', 'With love' vs 'Yours sincerely') and have them sort the phrases.

## WORKED_EXAMPLE_1
PROBLEM: You need to write a letter to your friend about your visit to the National War Memorial.
STEPS:
- 1: Identify the receiver (Friend = Informal).
- 2: Choose a warm greeting (e.g., "Dear Ananya").
- 3: Write the reason for writing (e.g., "I visited the memorial today").
- 4: Share a personal feeling (e.g., "It made me feel so proud").
- 5: Choose a warm closing (e.g., "With love").
REASONING: By following the informal structure, the letter maintains the correct friendly tone expected of a personal note.
ANSWER: Dear Ananya, I am writing to tell you about my trip to the National War Memorial. It was an amazing experience to see the eternal flame. It made me feel very proud of our soldiers. With love, [Your Name].
## WORKED_EXAMPLE_2
PROBLEM: Write a request to a local official asking for information about a local memorial.
STEPS:
- 1: Identify the receiver (Official = Formal).
- 2: Use a formal title (e.g., "Respected Sir").
- 3: Clearly state the request (e.g., "I wish to learn more about our local memorial").
- 4: Use polite language (e.g., "Could you please provide...").
- 5: Use a formal closing (e.g., "Yours faithfully").
REASONING: The formal structure respects the official status of the receiver and ensures the request is taken seriously.
ANSWER: Respected Sir, I am a student writing to request information about the history of our local memorial. Could you please provide some details? Yours faithfully, [Your Name].
## WORKED_EXAMPLE_3
PROBLEM: Identify and fix the tone errors in this sentence: "Dear Principal, I want to know about the war, tell me now!"
STEPS:
- 1: Analyze the tone: Informal greeting used with demanding language.
- 2: Identify the audience: Principal (Formal).
- 3: Change "Dear" to "Respected".
- 4: Change "I want to know/tell me now" to "I am writing to request information".
- 5: Ensure the closing is formal (e.g., "Yours sincerely").
REASONING: The original was too aggressive and informal for a school authority.
ANSWER: Respected Principal, I am writing to request information about the history of the war. Yours sincerely, [Your Name].
## COMPETENCY_QUESTION_1
QUESTION: You are writing to your brother who lives in another state. Which greeting and closing pair is correct? A) Respected Sir / Yours faithfully B) Dear Brother / With love.
COMPETENCY: Tone identification
STEPS:
- 1: Identify the relationship (Family).
- 2: Identify the tone required (Informal).
- 3: Compare options against the tone.
- 4: Select the informal match.
ANSWER: B. "Dear Brother / With love" is appropriate for family, whereas A is for official correspondence.
DIFFICULTY: easy
## COMPETENCY_QUESTION_2
QUESTION: Why is it important to use formal language when writing to a government official about a park's condition?
COMPETENCY: Reasoning and application
STEPS:
- 1: Define the role of a government official.
- 2: Explain the purpose of formal language (clarity, respect).
- 3: Explain what happens if informal language is used (it might look unprofessional or be ignored).
- 4: Conclude that formal language increases the chances of a helpful response.
ANSWER: It is important because official requests require respect and clarity to ensure they are processed correctly. Informal language might seem unprofessional or dismissive, which could lead to the official not taking the request seriously.
DIFFICULTY: hard
