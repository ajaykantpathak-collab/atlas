## DEFINITION
Prepositions are position words — they place things in space (in, on, under, behind, between), in time (at, on, in, since, for), and in direction (to, into, towards). 'The cat is ON the mat' — change the preposition and the whole picture changes.

## COGNITIVE_LEVEL
remember

## MASTERY_CRITERIA
The student uses space prepositions correctly (in/on/under/between/among/behind), time prepositions by the calendar rule (at+clock, on+day/date, in+month/year), and between-vs-among.

## SCOPE_BOUNDARY
Covers common space and time prepositions. Does NOT cover phrasal verbs or prepositional phrases in depth (class 8+).

## WALKTHROUGH
The calendar rule is the anchor: at = exact point (at 5 o'clock), on = day/date (on Monday, on 15 August), in = longer stretches (in July, in 2024, in the morning). Space: in = inside, on = touching surface, between = two things, among = a crowd.

## COMMON_MISCONCEPTION
Students translate from Hindi — 'in the bus' feels like 'inside' so they write 'in the bus' where English says 'on the bus' (public transport takes 'on'). They also swap at/on/in for time randomly.

## ON_FAILURE
Step 1: Time? Apply the calendar: at-clock, on-day, in-month. Step 2: Space? Picture it — inside=in, surface=on, surrounded=middle words. Step 3: Public transport (bus/train/plane) = 'on', cars = 'in'.

## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_1
Fill: 'The meeting is ___ Monday ___ 3 o'clock ___ July.'

## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_1
on Monday, at 3 o'clock, in July — the calendar rule end to end. Wrong answers mixing the three show the rule isn't internalized as a set.

## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_2
'She travels ___ bus daily.' in or on — and why?

## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_2
'on' — public transport takes 'on' (on the bus/train/plane); 'in' is for cars and taxis. A wrong answer 'in' reveals Hindi-inside translation instead of the English convention.

## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_3
Between or among: 'The secret is ___ you and me' / 'She stood ___ the crowd.'

## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_3
between you and me (two), among the crowd (many). A wrong answer swapping them shows the two-vs-many split isn't yet clear.
