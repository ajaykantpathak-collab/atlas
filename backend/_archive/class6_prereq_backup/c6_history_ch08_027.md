## DEFINITION
The Vijayanagara Empire was a powerful medieval Indian kingdom that controlled southern India between 1336 and 1646, with its capital city, Vijayanagara, serving as a symbol of victory. It was established by the brothers Harihara and Bukka and reached the height of its influence under the rule of King Krishnadevaraya.

## COMMON_MISCONCEPTION
Students often mistakenly think that the Vijayanagara Empire was the only kingdom in India at the time. This happens because textbooks often isolate chapters; in reality, India was home to many diverse regional kingdoms existing simultaneously, as explored in the "Many in the One" theme of this chapter.
