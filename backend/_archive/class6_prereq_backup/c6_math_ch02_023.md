## DEFINITION
The division of integers is the process of splitting an integer into equal parts, where the sign of the result is determined by the signs of the dividend and divisor: same signs yield a positive result, while different signs yield a negative result. Division by zero is undefined as it does not result in a specific number.
## COGNITIVE_LEVEL

## MASTERY_CRITERIA
The student must be able to correctly calculate the quotient of any two given integers and explain the sign rule (e.g., why a negative divided by a positive is negative) using the logic of inverse multiplication.
## SCOPE_BOUNDARY
This node covers the division of integers and the sign rules applied to quotients. It excludes algebraic division (variables), properties of division (e.g., commutativity), and modular arithmetic.
## WALKTHROUGH
Imagine you are at a game show where you earn points for correct answers and lose points for wrong ones. If you lost 20 points over 4 rounds by losing the same number of points each round, how many points did you lose per round? To solve this, we divide the negative total (-20) by the number of rounds (4). Just like in multiplication, we divide the numbers first (20 / 4 = 5) and then check the signs: since one is negative and one is positive, our answer must be negative (-5). The key insight is that division is the "undoing" of multiplication; if (-5) x 4 = -20, then -20 / 4 must be -5.
## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_1
What is the rule for the sign of the quotient when you divide two integers with different signs?
## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_1
The quotient is always negative. A wrong answer like "positive" suggests the student is confusing division with the rule for multiplying two negatives, or simply guessing.
## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_2
If you divide 0 by -5, what is the result, and why is it possible to do this, unlike dividing -5 by 0?
## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_2
The result is 0 because 0 multiplied by -5 is 0. A student who says "undefined" for 0 / -5 is confusing the dividend (0) with the divisor (0); division by zero is only undefined when zero is the divisor.
## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_3
A submarine is at sea level (0 meters) and descends to -120 meters in 6 equal stages. What integer represents the change in depth for each stage?
## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_3
-120 / 6 = -20. The change is -20 meters per stage. A student who answers +20 has ignored the negative sign representing descent.
## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_4
Compare the calculation of (-30) / (-5) and (-30) / 5. Which is larger and why?
## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_4
(-30) / (-5) = 6, while (-30) / 5 = -6. Since 6 is greater than -6, the first result is larger. An error here shows a failure to apply sign rules correctly to the final comparison.
## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_5
A student claims that (-15) / (-3) = -5. Is this correct? Explain using multiplication.
## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_5
No, it is incorrect because (-3) x (-5) = +15, not -15. The correct answer is +5 because (-3) x (+5) = -15. A wrong answer here shows the student does not understand division as the inverse of multiplication.
## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_6
Create a division problem using integers that results in -4 and explain your reasoning.
## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_6
Any problem like (-12) / 3 or 12 / (-3) is acceptable. The student must show they know the signs must be different to result in a negative quotient.
## COMMON_MISCONCEPTION
Students often apply the "two negatives make a positive" rule to addition instead of multiplication and division. For example, they might solve (-10) / (-2) correctly as 5, but then think (-10) + (-2) is also 5. This happens because they memorize "negative + negative = positive" as a blanket rule for all operations.
## ON_FAILURE
If a student confuses sign rules, ask them to write the division as a multiplication sentence. For (-10) / (-2) = ?, ask: "What number multiplied by -2 gives -10?" They will see it must be +5. For addition, use the "Building of Fun" lift analogy: "If you go down 10 floors and then down 2 more, are you higher or lower than where you started?"

## WORKED_EXAMPLE_1
PROBLEM: A company lost ₹1800 over 9 months, losing an equal amount each month. What was the integer representing the monthly profit/loss?
STEPS:
- Step 1: Total loss is -1800, time is 9 months.
- Step 2: Use division: total / time = monthly change.
- Step 3: Expression: (-1800) / 9.
- Step 4: Divide absolute values: 1800 / 9 = 200.
- Step 5: Apply sign: Negative / Positive = Negative. Result is -200.
REASONING: We use a negative integer for the total loss. Dividing by the number of months gives the average change, and the sign rule ensures the loss is represented as a negative value.
ANSWER: -200 rupees
## WORKED_EXAMPLE_2
PROBLEM: A diver goes down in 4 stages to reach a total depth of -80 meters. What was the depth change per stage?
STEPS:
- Step 1: Total depth is -80 meters, number of stages is 4.
- Step 2: Formula: total depth / number of stages.
- Step 3: Expression: (-80) / 4.
- Step 4: Divide absolute values: 80 / 4 = 20.
- Step 5: Apply sign: Negative / Positive = Negative. Result is -20.
REASONING: The diver is moving below sea level, so the total displacement is negative. Dividing this into equal parts gives the average change per stage, which remains negative.
ANSWER: -20 meters
## WORKED_EXAMPLE_3
PROBLEM: A bank account balance changed by -₹500 due to 5 equal debit transactions. What was the value of each transaction?
STEPS:
- Step 1: Total change = -500, number of transactions = 5.
- Step 2: Each transaction = (-500) / 5.
- Step 3: Divide 500 by 5 to get 100.
- Step 4: Apply sign rules: Negative / Positive = Negative.
- Step 5: Result is -100.
- Step 6: Interpret: Each debit is ₹100, represented as -100.
REASONING: Debits reduce the balance, so they are negative. Dividing the total reduction by the number of transactions shows the magnitude of each individual reduction.
ANSWER: -100 rupees
## COMPETENCY_QUESTION_1
QUESTION: If x / (-4) = 10, what is the value of x? Explain your reasoning using the relationship between division and multiplication.
COMPETENCY: Translating real-world situations to mathematical expressions
STEPS:
- Step 1: Understand that if a number divided by -4 is 10, then the number must be the product of 10 and -4.
- Step 2: Write the multiplication sentence: x = 10 * (-4).
- Step 3: Multiply absolute values: 10 * 4 = 40.
- Step 4: Apply sign rule: Positive * Negative = Negative.
ANSWER: x = -40. Reasoning: Division is the inverse of multiplication; if we divide x by -4 to get 10, multiplying 10 by -4 must return us to x. Since 10 is positive and -4 is negative, the product is negative.
DIFFICULTY: medium
## COMPETENCY_QUESTION_2
QUESTION: Two students, A and B, are dividing integers. Student A says (-50) / (-5) = -10. Student B says (-50) / (-5) = 10. Who is correct and why? Justify your answer using the sign rules.
COMPETENCY: Error identification and correction
STEPS:
- Step 1: Analyze Student A's answer: (-50) / (-5) = -10. Check: -10 * -5 = 50. (Incorrect)
- Step 2: Analyze Student B's answer: (-50) / (-5) = 10. Check: 10 * -5 = -50. (Correct)
- Step 3: Apply the sign rule: Negative / Negative = Positive.
- Step 4: Compare results.
ANSWER: Student B is correct. The rule states that dividing two integers with the same sign results in a positive quotient. Since both -50 and -5 are negative, the quotient must be positive (+10).
DIFFICULTY: hard
