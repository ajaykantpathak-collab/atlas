## DEFINITION
Fractions and decimals are two different ways of writing the same value; a fraction represents a part of a whole using a numerator and denominator, while a decimal represents that same part using place value powers of ten. Converting between them allows us to switch between these two formats based on which is more convenient for our calculations.
## COGNITIVE_LEVEL
apply
## MASTERY_CRITERIA
The student must be able to convert any simple fraction into a decimal through division and convert a decimal into its simplest fractional form using place value names (tenths, hundredths, etc.).
## SCOPE_BOUNDARY
This node covers basic conversion between terminating fractions and decimals. It excludes repeating decimals analysis, irrational numbers, and binary conversions.
## WALKTHROUGH
Imagine you have half a chocolate bar; you can call it 1/2, but if you look at a digital scale, it shows 0.5. To convert a fraction like 3/4 to a decimal, think of it as a division problem: 3 divided by 4, which equals 0.75. To go the other way, take 0.25 and look at the last digit's place value—it is in the hundredths place, so you write 25/100 and then simplify it to 1/4 by dividing both numbers by 25. The key insight is that the "bar" in a fraction is just a shorthand symbol for the division sign.
## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_1
What is the standard method for converting a fraction like 5/8 into a decimal?
## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_1
The method is to divide the numerator (5) by the denominator (8). If a student answers "you just add a decimal point," they likely do not understand the relationship between the fraction bar and the division operation.
## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_2
If you have the decimal 0.4, why do you write it as 4/10 instead of 4/100?
## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_2
Because the digit 4 is in the tenths place, not the hundredths place. A wrong answer suggests the student confuses the decimal place value names, which is critical for identifying the denominator.
## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_3
Convert 3/5 into a decimal. Show your steps.
## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_3
Step 1: Divide 3 by 5. Step 2: 3 is smaller than 5, so write it as 3.0. Step 3: 5 goes into 30 six times, so the result is 0.6. A wrong answer usually occurs if the student tries to divide the denominator by the numerator instead.
## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_4
Compare 0.75 and 3/4. Are they the same value? How do you know?
## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_4
Yes, they are the same. Converting 3/4 to 0.75 or 0.75 to 75/100 (which simplifies to 3/4) proves they are identical. A student saying "no" fails to see that fractions and decimals are just different representations of the same amount.
## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_5
Is it easier to represent 1/3 as a decimal? Why or why not?
## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_5
It is difficult because 1/3 results in a repeating decimal (0.333...), which never ends. A student who says "it's easy because it's just 0.3" misses the fact that 0.3 is only an approximation, not the exact value of 1/3.
## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_6
Create a fraction that, when converted to a decimal, results in exactly 0.2.
## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_6
The fraction is 2/10, which simplifies to 1/5. If the student provides a different fraction that equals 0.2 (like 20/100), it is also correct; if they provide something like 2/100, they misunderstand place value.
## COMMON_MISCONCEPTION
Students often divide the larger number by the smaller number regardless of whether it is the numerator or the denominator. For example, when asked to convert 1/4, they calculate 4 ÷ 1 = 4 instead of 1 ÷ 4 = 0.25.
## ON_FAILURE
Remind the student that the numerator is the "part" and the denominator is the "whole." Tell them: "The fraction bar means 'divided by,' so always read it as top-number divided by bottom-number." Have them practice writing "Numerator ÷ Denominator" on their paper before picking up the calculator or starting long division.

## WORKED_EXAMPLE_1
PROBLEM: You bought 3/4 of a kilogram of apples. How much is this in decimal form?
STEPS:
- step 1: Identify numerator (3) and denominator (4).
- step 2: Set up the division 3 ÷ 4.
- step 3: Add a decimal point: 3.00 ÷ 4.
- step 4: 30 ÷ 4 = 7 with remainder 2; 20 ÷ 4 = 5.
- step 5: Result is 0.75 kg.
REASONING: Since a fraction represents part of a whole, dividing the part by the whole gives the decimal representation.
ANSWER: 0.75 kg
## WORKED_EXAMPLE_2
PROBLEM: A ribbon is 0.8 meters long. Write this as a fraction in its simplest form.
STEPS:
- step 1: Identify the place value of the last digit (8 is in the tenths place).
- step 2: Write as a fraction: 8/10.
- step 3: Find the greatest common divisor (GCD) of 8 and 10, which is 2.
- step 4: Divide both numerator and denominator by 2.
- step 5: 8÷2 = 4 and 10÷2 = 5.
REASONING: Using the place value helps translate the decimal to a fraction, and simplifying makes the fraction standard.
ANSWER: 4/5 meters
## WORKED_EXAMPLE_3
PROBLEM: You have 1/2 liter of milk in one jar and 0.25 liters in another. Which is more?
STEPS:
- step 1: Convert 1/2 to decimal: 1 ÷ 2 = 0.5.
- step 2: Compare 0.5 and 0.25.
- step 3: Align decimal points: 0.50 and 0.25.
- step 4: Observe that 50 hundredths is greater than 25 hundredths.
- step 5: Conclude 0.5 > 0.25.
REASONING: To compare different formats, we must convert them to the same format first.
ANSWER: 1/2 liter is more.
## COMPETENCY_QUESTION_1
QUESTION: If you have 0.75 liters of water, can you pour it into a bottle that only holds 3/4 of a liter? Explain your reasoning.
COMPETENCY: Translating real-world situations to mathematical expressions.
STEPS:
- step 1: Recognize that 0.75 is the decimal form of 3/4.
- step 2: Calculate 3 ÷ 4 = 0.75.
- step 3: Compare the quantities.
- step 4: Conclude they are equal.
ANSWER: Yes, they are equal. Since 0.75 is exactly the same as 3/4, the water will fit perfectly.
DIFFICULTY: medium
## COMPETENCY_QUESTION_2
QUESTION: Your friend says that 0.5 is smaller than 0.125 because 5 is smaller than 125. Is your friend correct? Why or why not?
COMPETENCY: Error identification and correction.
STEPS:
- step 1: Identify the error (comparing digits instead of place values).
- step 2: Convert both to fractions: 0.5 = 5/10 (or 500/1000) and 0.125 = 125/1000.
- step 3: Compare 500/1000 and 125/1000.
- step 4: Explain that 0.5 is 5 tenths, while 0.125 is 1 tenth and 2 hundredths and 5 thousandths.
ANSWER: The friend is incorrect. 0.5 is equal to 0.500, which is much larger than 0.125, because the 5 is in the tenths place, giving it a much higher value than the 1 in the tenths place of 0.125.
DIFFICULTY: hard
