## DEFINITION
Decimals are a way of writing fractions that have denominators of 10, 100, 1000, and so on, using a decimal point to separate the whole number from the fractional part. The position of each digit to the right of the decimal point represents tenths, hundredths, or thousandths, extending the base-10 place-value system.
## COGNITIVE_LEVEL
understand
## MASTERY_CRITERIA
The student must be able to convert a fraction with a denominator of 10 or 100 into its decimal form and identify the place value of digits to the right of the decimal point.
## SCOPE_BOUNDARY
This node covers the introduction to decimals and their place-value representation, including tenths and hundredths. It excludes repeating decimals, operations (addition/subtraction), and scientific notation.
## WALKTHROUGH
Have you ever looked at a ruler and seen the small lines between the centimetres? If you have 3 full centimetres and 4 of those tiny millimetre marks, how do you write that down? Instead of writing "3 and 4 tenths of a centimetre," we use a decimal point: 3.4 cm. The decimal point acts like a "divider" that separates the whole number from the parts that are smaller than one. Think of the decimal point as the anchor of the place-value system; everything to the left is a whole, and everything to the right is a fraction of a whole.
## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_1
In the number 5.7, which digit is in the tenths place?
## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_1
The digit 7 is in the tenths place. If a student answers "5," they are confusing the whole number place value with the fractional place value.
## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_2
Explain why 0.5 is the same as the fraction 5/10.
## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_2
0.5 means 5 tenths, which is written as the fraction 5/10. A student who says "they are not the same" likely doesn't understand that the decimal point denotes a base-10 fractional part.
## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_3
Convert the fraction 24/100 into a decimal.
## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_3
The answer is 0.24. This is found by moving the decimal point two places to the left for a denominator of 100. If a student writes 2.4, they are likely miscounting the number of decimal places needed.
## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_4
Compare 0.3 and 0.03. Which is larger and why?
## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_4
0.3 is larger because it represents 3 tenths (30/100), whereas 0.03 represents only 3 hundredths (3/100). A student who thinks 0.03 is larger is likely ignoring the place value and just looking at the number of zeros.
## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_5
If you have 8 rupees and 50 paise, how would you write this as a decimal in terms of rupees?
## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_5
It is 8.50 rupees because 50 paise is 50/100 of a rupee. A student who writes 8.05 is failing to recognize that 50 paise is 5 tenths or 50 hundredths of a rupee.
## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_6
Write your own decimal number that is greater than 0.1 but less than 0.2.
## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_6
Any number like 0.11, 0.15, or 0.19 is correct. A student who struggles to provide an answer does not understand that there are many values between tenths.
## COMMON_MISCONCEPTION
Students often think that 0.4 is smaller than 0.25 because they see "4" and "25" and assume 25 is bigger. They treat the digits after the decimal point as whole numbers rather than parts of a whole (tenths/hundredths).
## ON_FAILURE
Ask the student to draw a square divided into 100 small equal blocks. Shade 4 columns (40 blocks) and ask them to count how many are shaded (40/100 = 0.40). Then shade 25 blocks and ask which area is larger. Visually demonstrating that 4 tenths covers more space than 25 hundredths fixes the place-value confusion.

## WORKED_EXAMPLE_1
PROBLEM: A tailor uses 1.4 metres of cloth for a shirt. Represent this as a fraction.
STEPS:
- Step 1: Identify the whole number part (1) and the decimal part (0.4).
- Step 2: Convert the decimal part 0.4 into a fraction (4/10).
- Step 3: Combine them: 1 + 4/10.
- Step 4: Write as a mixed fraction: 1 4/10 or an improper fraction 14/10.
REASONING: The decimal point indicates that 4 is in the tenths place, meaning it is 4 parts of 10. Converting directly to a fraction makes it easier to measure cloth using a standard tape.
ANSWER: 14/10 metres
## WORKED_EXAMPLE_2
PROBLEM: A student has 0.75 litres of milk. Express this as a fraction with a denominator of 100.
STEPS:
- Step 1: Look at the digits after the decimal point (7 and 5).
- Step 2: Place the number 75 over the denominator 100 because the last digit is in the hundredths place.
- Step 3: Simplify the fraction if needed, but 75/100 is requested.
- Step 4: Confirm 0.75 represents 75 hundredths.
REASONING: Because the 5 occupies the second position after the decimal, we must use 100 as the denominator. This represents the total number of hundredth-parts in the whole litre.
ANSWER: 75/100 litres
## WORKED_EXAMPLE_3
PROBLEM: A gold coin weighs 0.08 grams. Write this as a fraction and explain its place value.
STEPS:
- Step 1: Identify that the 8 is in the second decimal position.
- Step 2: The second position corresponds to the hundredths place.
- Step 3: Place 8 over 100 to get 8/100.
- Step 4: The 0 in the tenths place signifies there are no tenths.
REASONING: Decimals work like a ladder; the first step is tenths, the second is hundredths. By correctly identifying the position of the 8, we correctly assign the denominator.
ANSWER: 8/100 grams
## COMPETENCY_QUESTION_1
QUESTION: If you have 0.2 rupees and 0.02 rupees, which is worth more? Explain using the concept of place value.
COMPETENCY: Comparing and justifying
STEPS:
- Step 1: Write both as fractions with the same denominator.
- Step 2: Convert 0.2 to 20/100.
- Step 3: Convert 0.02 to 2/100.
- Step 4: Compare the numerators (20 > 2).
ANSWER: 0.2 rupees is worth more. 0.2 represents 20/100, while 0.02 represents only 2/100, making 0.2 ten times larger.
DIFFICULTY: medium
## COMPETENCY_QUESTION_2
QUESTION: A bottle holds 0.5 litres of water. A cup holds 0.45 litres of juice. Which container holds more liquid? How would you explain this to someone who thinks 0.45 is larger because 45 > 5?
COMPETENCY: Error identification and correction
STEPS:
- Step 1: Standardize the decimal places (0.5 becomes 0.50).
- Step 2: Compare the tenths place (5 vs 4).
- Step 3: Explain that 5 tenths is 50 hundredths.
- Step 4: Conclude 0.50 > 0.45.
ANSWER: The bottle holds more. Even though 45 is larger than 5, the 5 in 0.5 is in the tenths place (worth 0.50), while the 4 in 0.45 is in the tenths place (worth only 0.40). Adding a zero makes the comparison clear.
