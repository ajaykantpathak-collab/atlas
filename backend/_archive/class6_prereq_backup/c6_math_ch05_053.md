## DEFINITION
An algebraic expression is a mathematical phrase containing variables (letters representing unknown numbers), constants (fixed numbers), and operations (like addition or subtraction). Terms are the parts of the expression separated by plus (+) or minus (-) signs, and the coefficient is the numerical factor multiplying the variable within a term.
## COGNITIVE_LEVEL
understand
## MASTERY_CRITERIA
The student must be able to decompose a given expression into its individual terms, identify the coefficient and variable part for each term, and classify a term as a constant or variable term.
## SCOPE_BOUNDARY
This node covers identifying terms, coefficients, and constant terms in basic expressions. It excludes polynomial arithmetic, factoring, and rational expressions.
## WALKTHROUGH
Imagine you are at a fruit stall where an apple costs ₹10 and a banana costs ₹5. If you buy 'x' apples and 'y' bananas, the total cost can be written as 10x + 5y. To understand this, look at the plus sign: it acts like a wall, separating the expression into two "terms": 10x and 5y. The number 10 is the coefficient because it tells us how many apples we have, and 'x' is the variable because the number of apples could change. If we add a flat delivery fee of ₹20, the expression becomes 10x + 5y + 20, where 20 is a "constant term" because it doesn't change regardless of how many fruits you buy.
## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_1
In the expression 4a + 7b - 3, what are the three separate terms?
## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_1
The terms are 4a, 7b, and -3. A wrong answer (like listing 4, a, 7, b, 3) shows the student confuses individual factors within a term with the terms themselves, which are defined by the operation signs.
## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_2
Why is the number 5 in the term 5y called a "coefficient"?
## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_2
It is called a coefficient because it represents the multiplier of the variable 'y'. A wrong answer suggests the student thinks it is just a "number" or "constant," missing the specific relationship that a coefficient exists only to modify a variable.
## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_3
Identify the coefficient of the term 'm' in the expression 9n + m - 12.
## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_3
The coefficient is 1. Students often answer 0 because they see no number written before 'm'; explaining that 'm' is the same as '1 × m' corrects this.
## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_4
Analyze the expression 6x - 2y + 8. How does the negative sign affect the term containing 'y'?
## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_4
The negative sign belongs to the term, making it -2y. A wrong answer (identifying the term as 2y) ignores that the sign preceding a term is part of that term's identity.
## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_5
Evaluate the expression 3x + 10 if x = 2. Is 10 a constant term or a coefficient? Justify.
## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_5
10 is a constant term because it does not have a variable attached to it and its value remains fixed regardless of the value of 'x'. Answering that it is a coefficient reveals a misunderstanding of the definition of constant vs. variable terms.
## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_6
Create an expression that has three terms, including one constant term, one term with a coefficient of 5, and one term with a coefficient of -2.
## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_6
A correct example is 5a - 2b + 10. A poor answer (like 5a - 2b + 10c) indicates the student forgot to include a constant term (a number without a variable).
## COMMON_MISCONCEPTION
Students often mistakenly include the plus or minus sign as a separate "operation" rather than part of the term itself, or they treat the variable as part of the coefficient. For example, in 5x - 3y, they might identify the coefficient of the second term as 3 instead of -3, or identify 'x' as part of the coefficient 5.
## ON_FAILURE
Ask the student to circle every term including the sign directly in front of it. Say, "The sign is like a backpack the term carries; you cannot leave it behind." Then, have them underline the number (the coefficient) and put a box around the letter (the variable) to visually separate them.

## WORKED_EXAMPLE_1
PROBLEM: A notebook costs ₹20 (let the number of notebooks be 'n') and a pen costs ₹10 (let the number of pens be 'p'). If there is a fixed bag price of ₹50, write the expression for the total cost and identify the terms, coefficients, and constant.
STEPS:
- step 1: Translate the context: 20 times n plus 10 times p plus 50.
- step 2: Write the expression: 20n + 10p + 50.
- step 3: Separate terms by operations: 20n, 10p, 50.
- step 4: Identify components: 20 and 10 are coefficients, 'n' and 'p' are variables, 50 is a constant.
REASONING: Translating words to expressions helps organize data; identifying terms helps distinguish between items that vary (pens/books) and items that are fixed (bag).
ANSWER: Expression: 20n + 10p + 50; Terms: 20n, 10p, 50; Coefficients: 20, 10; Constant: 50.
## WORKED_EXAMPLE_2
PROBLEM: An expression is given as 15x - 4y - 9. Identify the terms and the coefficient of each variable term.
STEPS:
- step 1: Identify the separators (minus signs).
- step 2: Write terms with their signs: 15x, -4y, -9.
- step 3: Extract variable terms: 15x and -4y.
- step 4: State coefficients: The coefficient of x is 15; the coefficient of y is -4.
REASONING: Recognizing that the sign belongs to the term is crucial for correctly identifying negative coefficients.
ANSWER: Terms: 15x, -4y, -9; Coefficients: 15 and -4.
## WORKED_EXAMPLE_3
PROBLEM: A gardener has a fixed collection of 5 tools. He buys 'b' buckets at ₹40 each and 's' shovels at ₹30 each. Write the expression. If he has 2 buckets and 3 shovels, calculate the total value of items, ignoring the fixed tools.
STEPS:
- step 1: Write expression: 40b + 30s.
- step 2: Identify variable terms: 40b and 30s.
- step 3: Substitute given values: 40(2) + 30(3).
- step 4: Perform arithmetic: 80 + 90.
- step 5: Sum total: 170.
REASONING: By treating terms as separate units, we can calculate the value of each item type independently before summing them.
ANSWER: Total value is ₹170.
## COMPETENCY_QUESTION_1
QUESTION: You have two expressions: A = 5x + 3 and B = 3x + 5. Are the coefficients of x the same in both? Are the constants the same? Explain.
COMPETENCY: Comparing and justifying
STEPS:
- step 1: Analyze expression A: term 5x has coefficient 5, constant is 3.
- step 2: Analyze expression B: term 3x has coefficient 3, constant is 5.
- step 3: Compare coefficients: 5 is not equal to 3.
- step 4: Compare constants: 3 is not equal to 5.
ANSWER: No, the coefficients are different (5 and 3) and the constants are different (3 and 5). This shows that the position of the number changes its meaning—a coefficient multiplies the variable, while a constant does not.
DIFFICULTY: medium
## COMPETENCY_QUESTION_2
QUESTION: A student claims that in the expression 7 - 2z, the coefficient of z is 2. Is this student correct? If not, what is the correct coefficient?
COMPETENCY: Error identification and correction
STEPS:
- step 1: Identify the terms: 7 and -2z.
- step 2: Look at the term containing 'z': -2z.
- step 3: Determine the multiplier of 'z': -2.
- step 4: Formulate correction: The student ignored the subtraction sign.
ANSWER: The student is incorrect. The coefficient of z is -2, not 2, because the subtraction sign preceding the variable term must be included as part of the term itself.
DIFFICULTY: hard
