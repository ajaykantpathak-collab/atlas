## DEFINITION
Subtraction of integers is the mathematical operation of finding the difference between two integers, which is equivalent to adding the additive inverse (the opposite) of the number being subtracted.
## COGNITIVE_LEVEL
apply
## MASTERY_CRITERIA
The student must be able to convert any subtraction expression into an addition expression using the rule "a - b = a + (-b)" and solve it accurately using a number line or token model.
## SCOPE_BOUNDARY
Covers subtraction of integers, including positive and negative numbers and zero. Excludes algebraic variable manipulation, absolute value proofs, or complex properties of integers.
## WALKTHROUGH
Imagine you are in a building with a lift that goes both above and below the ground floor (0). If you are on the +2 floor and you want to go to the -3 floor, you need to think: "How many floors down do I have to press?" Subtraction is just a way of finding the "movement" needed to get from your starting point to your target point. Instead of just taking away, think of it as "adding the opposite." If you see a subtraction sign, change the sign of the next number and add it instead; for example, subtracting a negative 3 is the same as moving up 3. The key insight is that every subtraction problem is actually an addition problem in disguise.
## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_1
What is the rule for subtracting an integer?
## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_1
Subtracting an integer is the same as adding its opposite. A student who says "you just make it smaller" or "you always take away" is failing to recognize that subtracting a negative number actually increases the value.
## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_2
Why does the expression 5 - (-3) result in 8 instead of 2?
## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_2
Because subtracting a negative is equivalent to adding a positive (5 + 3 = 8). A student who answers 2 is likely ignoring the "double negative" rule and treating the subtraction sign as a simple removal of value rather than an inverse operation.
## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_3
If you are at Floor +4 and you want to reach Floor -2, what movement is needed?
## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_3
You need to move -6. You calculate this by Target - Start, which is (-2) - (+4) = -6. A student who answers +6 is confusing the direction of movement or the order of subtraction.
## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_4
Compare the expressions 10 - 2 and 10 + (-2). Are they the same? Why?
## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_4
Yes, they are the same because subtracting 2 is identical to adding the inverse of 2. A student who claims they are different does not understand that subtraction is simply the addition of an additive inverse.
## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_5
A bank account has a balance of -₹50 (a debt). You pay off a debt of ₹20. How do you represent this with integers?
## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_5
(-50) - (-20) = -30. The student must recognize that "paying off" a debt is a subtraction of a negative amount, which improves the balance. A wrong answer like -70 shows the student thinks paying a debt decreases the balance.
## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_6
Create a real-world scenario where subtracting a negative integer is the only way to describe the situation.
## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_6
A good answer involves temperature or altitude, like "The temperature rose from -10°C to -2°C." A poor answer uses only positive numbers, showing the student has not mastered the concept of numbers below zero.
## COMMON_MISCONCEPTION
Students often treat the subtraction sign as a "remove" instruction only, failing to realize that subtracting a negative number acts like adding. They often calculate 3 - (-2) as 1 because they subtract the 2 from the 3, ignoring the negative sign in the middle.
## ON_FAILURE
If a student fails, have them draw a "Building of Fun" number line. Ask them: "If you are on -2 and the operator removes a negative (a red token), what happens to your total?" Show them that removing a "debt" or "red token" is the same as gaining a positive value.

## WORKED_EXAMPLE_1
PROBLEM: A submarine is at 40 metres below sea level (-40m). It descends another 30 metres. What is its new position?
STEPS:
- step 1: Identify starting position (-40) and movement (down 30 means -30).
- step 2: The expression is (-40) - (+30).
- step 3: Apply the rule: (-40) + (-30).
- step 4: Add the two negative values: -(40 + 30) = -70.
- step 5: The new position is 70 metres below sea level.
REASONING: Since the submarine is already below sea level, descending further is moving deeper into the negative range, which is addition of two negatives.
ANSWER: -70 metres
## WORKED_EXAMPLE_2
PROBLEM: A thermometer shows -5°C. The temperature rises to 2°C. What is the difference in temperature?
STEPS:
- step 1: Identify start (-5) and target (2).
- step 2: Use the rule: Target - Start = Movement (2 - (-5)).
- step 3: Convert to addition: 2 + (+5).
- step 4: Calculate the sum: 7.
- step 5: The temperature rose by 7 degrees.
REASONING: The distance between two points on a number line is found by subtracting the start from the target; the double negative creates a positive movement.
ANSWER: 7°C
## WORKED_EXAMPLE_3
PROBLEM: You have a bank balance of -₹100. You deposit ₹150. What is your new balance?
STEPS:
- step 1: Current balance is -100. Deposit is a positive gain of +150.
- step 2: Expression: -100 + 150.
- step 3: Identify the larger magnitude (150) and smaller magnitude (100).
- step 4: Subtract 100 from 150 to get 50.
- step 5: Apply the sign of the larger magnitude (+).
REASONING: When adding a negative and a positive, you find the difference and keep the sign of the larger value, reflecting the transition from debt to credit.
ANSWER: +₹50
## COMPETENCY_QUESTION_1
QUESTION: A student claims that (-5) - (-2) is equal to -7. Explain why this is incorrect and provide the correct result using the number line or token model.
COMPETENCY: Error identification and correction
STEPS:
- step 1: Analyze the student's error (they likely added the absolute values).
- step 2: Apply the rule: (-5) + (+2).
- step 3: Visualize: Start at -5, move 2 steps to the right.
- step 4: Arrive at -3.
ANSWER: The student is incorrect because they did not account for the double negative. Subtracting -2 is the same as adding 2, so the correct result is -3.
DIFFICULTY: medium
## COMPETENCY_QUESTION_2
QUESTION: You are playing a game where you start at 0. You draw two cards: the first is -10, the second is -5. If you subtract the second card from the first, where do you end up? If you add them, where do you end up? Compare the two results.
COMPETENCY: Translating real-world situations to mathematical expressions
STEPS:
- step 1: Define expressions: (-10) - (-5) and (-10) + (-5).
- step 2: Solve subtraction: -10 + 5 = -5.
- step 3: Solve addition: -10 - 5 = -15.
- step 4: Compare: -5 is greater than -15.
ANSWER: Subtracting the cards results in -5, while adding them results in -15. Subtracting a negative creates a higher (more positive) value than adding a negative.
