## DEFINITION
Punctuation is traffic control: commas separate but never join clauses; semicolons join related independent clauses without a conjunction; colons introduce lists/explanations; apostrophes mark possession and contraction — never plurals.

## COGNITIVE_LEVEL
analyze

## MASTERY_CRITERIA
The student punctuates complex sentences correctly — comma splices fixed, semicolons for balanced clauses, colons before lists, apostrophes confined to possession/contraction.

## SCOPE_BOUNDARY
Covers the board-tested punctuation set. Does NOT cover quotation punctuation in depth or dashes/parentheses.

## WALKTHROUGH
The comma's limits: 'It rained, we stayed home' is a splice — the comma cannot do a conjunction's job. Fixes: add 'so', split with a full stop, or upgrade to a semicolon. The apostrophe's borders: 'its' = possession, 'it's' = it is; 'the 1990s' not 'the 1990's' — plurals take no apostrophe.

## COMMON_MISCONCEPTION
Students sprinkle commas as pauses ('wherever a breath fits'), use semicolons as super-commas, and apostrophize plurals ('apple's for sale').

## ON_FAILURE
Step 1: Check each clause-join — comma alone = splice; add a conjunction or upgrade to semicolon. Step 2: Colon only when what follows explains/lists what precedes. Step 3: Apostrophe = possession or contraction; test 'its' by expanding to 'it is'.

## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_1
Fix: 'The concert was cancelled, many fans were disappointed.'

## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_1
Comma splice — fix as 'cancelled; many fans' or 'cancelled, so many fans' or a full stop. A wrong answer keeping the comma shows comma-power belief.

## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_2
Semicolon or comma: 'The exam was hard ___ it was fair.'

## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_2
Semicolon — two independent clauses, closely related, no conjunction. A wrong answer choosing comma creates a splice; 'and' + comma is the other legal fix.

## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_3
'The students book's were stolen' — find both errors.

## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_3
'students'' (possessive apostrophe after s) and 'books' (plural takes no apostrophe). A wrong answer fixing only one shows partial apostrophe rules.
