## DEFINITION
Subordinate clauses are classified by the JOB they do in the main clause: a noun clause works as subject/object/complement ('What he said was true'), an adjective (relative) clause describes a noun ('the book that I bought'), an adverb clause modifies the verb for time/reason/condition ('when she arrived').

## COGNITIVE_LEVEL
analyze

## MASTERY_CRITERIA
The student identifies the clause type by its grammatical function — not by its connector — and distinguishes 'that'-noun-clauses from 'that'-relative-clauses.

## SCOPE_BOUNDARY
Covers the three subordinate clause types and their tests. Does NOT cover reduced clauses or non-finite clauses.

## WALKTHROUGH
The substitution tests: noun clause — replace it with 'something/it' and the sentence survives ('What he said' → 'It was true'). Adjective clause — it answers 'which one?' about a noun and can swap 'that' for 'which/who'. Adverb clause — it answers when/why/how/under-what-condition of the main verb.

## COMMON_MISCONCEPTION
Students classify clauses by their introductory word — assuming every 'that'-clause is a noun clause and every 'when'-clause is adverbial — instead of asking what job the clause performs in its sentence.

## ON_FAILURE
Step 1: Locate the subordinate clause boundaries. Step 2: Ask what it does — subject/object of the main verb (noun), describes a noun (adjective), or modifies the verb's circumstances (adverb). Step 3: Apply the swap tests to confirm.

## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_1
'What he promised was never delivered.' — clause type?

## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_1
Noun clause — 'what he promised' is the subject; swap in 'It' and the sentence stands. A wrong answer 'adjective clause' shows connector-based classification instead of function-based.

## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_2
'The house that Jack built is old.' — is 'that Jack built' a noun clause?

## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_2
No — adjective/relative clause; it describes 'house' (which house? that Jack built). 'That' here replaces 'which' and refers to the noun — not a content clause. The same connector, different job.

## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_3
'She left because the meeting ended.' — clause type and its question?

## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_3
Adverb clause of reason — it answers 'why did she leave?' about the main verb. A wrong answer 'noun clause' shows the student didn't test what the clause modifies.
