## DEFINITION
Senior reported speech handles the leftovers: exclamations → 'exclaimed that' with the exclamation flattened ('What a shot!' → 'exclaimed that it was a great shot'); questions embedded inside statements ('Do you know where he lives?' — the wh-part keeps statement order); and mixed reporting verbs chosen for force.

## COGNITIVE_LEVEL
analyze

## MASTERY_CRITERIA
The student reports exclamations and embedded questions correctly, preserves statement order inside noun-clause questions, and selects reporting verbs by the original's force.

## SCOPE_BOUNDARY
Covers exclamations, embedded/indirect questions, and verb choice. Does NOT cover basic statement/question/command frames (class 9-10).

## WALKTHROUGH
Embedded questions are the trap: 'Do you know where does he live?' is WRONG — inside the noun clause, question-order dies even though the sentence asks a question. 'Do you know where he lives?' Exclamations flatten: the emotion converts to the reporting verb ('exclaimed in surprise') and the content goes to a that-clause.

## COMMON_MISCONCEPTION
Students keep inversion inside embedded questions ('I don't know where is he') and report exclamations by quoting them — both kill the reported frame.

## ON_FAILURE
Step 1: Embedded question → the wh-word heads a noun clause; statement order inside. Step 2: Exclamation → exclaimed + that + flattened statement. Step 3: Reporting verb carries the force — asked/told/ordered/requested/warned/exclaimed.

## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_1
Fix: 'Do you know where does he live?'

## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_1
'Do you know where he lives?' — the embedded wh-question is a noun clause; statement order inside despite the sentence being a question. The single most-tested senior structure.

## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_2
Report: 'What a wonderful performance!' she said.

## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_2
'She exclaimed that it was a wonderful performance' — the exclamation flattens into a that-clause; 'what a' dissolves into the reporting verb.

## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_3
'I don't know where ___ .' is she / she is?

## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_3
'she is' — noun-clause order after 'where'. 'Where is she' is question order, illegal inside 'I don't know…' frames.
