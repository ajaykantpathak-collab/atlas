## DEFINITION
The class 9 notice: boxed, ~50 words, institution name → NOTICE → date → heading → body answering What/When/Where/Whom-to-contact → signature with designation. Third person always — 'All students are informed', never 'I'.

## COGNITIVE_LEVEL
apply

## MASTERY_CRITERIA
The student drafts a compliant notice with all elements, third-person clipped body, and under-limit word count, and audits defective notices for register and skeleton faults.

## SCOPE_BOUNDARY
Covers school notices at board standard. Does NOT cover classified ads or tenders.

## WALKTHROUGH
The notice is the most mechanical format: box it, stack the four-line header, write the body as clipped third-person announcement sentences answering the 4Ws, sign as '[Name], [Designation]'. Fifty words — enthusiasm gets cut first.

## COMMON_MISCONCEPTION
Students write invitation-tone notices ('Please do come!'), use first person, exceed 50 words, and forget the issuing-authority line or the contact/deadline.

## ON_FAILURE
Step 1: Box + header stack (org, NOTICE, date, heading). Step 2: Body — What/When/Where in clipped third-person sentences. Step 3: Contact line ('contact the undersigned by [date]'). Step 4: Signature + designation. Step 5: ≤50 words.

## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_1
What order do a notice's header elements follow?

## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_1
Institution name → NOTICE → date → heading. A wrong answer leading with the heading treats the notice like an essay title — authority leads the format.

## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_2
Which body sentence is notice-legal: 'You should all come and have fun!' or 'Interested students may register with the undersigned by 8 May'?

## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_2
The second — third person, action + deadline + contact. The first is invitation-register with first/second person — notices inform, never invite.

## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_3
A notice is 70 words including 'a very exciting and wonderful celebration' — first cut?

## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_3
The enthusiasm-phrase — adjectives performing excitement are the first fat to trim. The 4W facts are untouchable; decoration is expendable.
