## DEFINITION
Subordinating conjunctions — because, although, when, while, if, since, unless, until — create dependent clauses that lean on a main clause. Each carries a specific logic: because=reason, although=concession, when/while=time, if/unless=condition, until=time-limit.

## COGNITIVE_LEVEL
apply

## MASTERY_CRITERIA
The student selects the subordinator matching the intended logic, places the dependent clause correctly (front position needs a comma), and distinguishes since-time from since-reason.

## SCOPE_BOUNDARY
Covers the eight common subordinators and clause placement. Does NOT cover correlative pairs or advanced clause types.

## WALKTHROUGH
The leaning-words map: although = 'yes, BUT anyway' (concession); unless = 'if not' (negative condition); until = 'up to the moment' (limit). Position rule: dependent clause first → comma follows ('Although it rained, we played'). Main clause first → no comma needed ('We played although it rained').

## COMMON_MISCONCEPTION
Students use 'because' for every logic ('because it rained, we stayed home' is right; but 'because it rained, we still played' is a logic-flip — needs 'although'). They also misplace or omit the comma after fronted clauses.

## ON_FAILURE
Step 1: Name the logic between the clauses — reason, contrast, time, condition. Step 2: Pick the subordinator whose job is that logic. Step 3: Clause first → comma; clause second → usually no comma.

## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_1
'___ it rained heavily, the match continued.' because or although?

## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_1
'although' — the match continuing DESPITE rain is concession, not causation. A wrong answer 'because' makes rain the reason the match continued — a logic-flip.

## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_2
Fix the punctuation: 'When the bell rang the students rushed out.'

## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_2
'When the bell rang, the students rushed out' — a fronted dependent clause needs its comma. A wrong answer adding it after 'students' breaks the wrong place.

## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_3
'I will wait ___ you return.' — until or unless?

## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_3
'until' — waiting up to the moment of return (time-limit). 'Unless' would mean 'if you don't return' — a wrong answer swaps condition for time-limit.
