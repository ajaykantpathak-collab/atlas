## DEFINITION
A formal letter has a fixed skeleton worth dedicated format marks: sender's address → date → receiver's designation+address → subject line → salutation (Dear Sir/Madam) → body (purpose → details → request) → subscription (Yours faithfully) → name. Every missing element is a mark thrown away before content is even read.

## COGNITIVE_LEVEL
apply

## MASTERY_CRITERIA
The student produces the complete 8-element skeleton in correct order for any formal letter task (complaint, inquiry, order, letter-to-editor), and can audit a defective letter listing each missing or misplaced element.

## SCOPE_BOUNDARY
Covers layout and the function of each block. Does NOT cover register/word choice inside the body (90_002) or informal letters (chapter 91).

## WALKTHROUGH
Draw the skeleton as a labeled diagram. The logic: the top block tells the receiver WHO is writing and HOW to reply; subject is a one-line headline; the three body paragraphs obey purpose→details→request; subscription pairs with salutation — 'Dear Sir/Madam' locks to 'Yours faithfully'. Examiners tick format marks left-to-right down the page.

## COMMON_MISCONCEPTION
Students merge sender's and receiver's addresses, drop the subject line (the single most-lost format mark), sign off 'Yours sincerely' to 'Dear Sir' (the pairing rule — faithfully pairs with Dear Sir/Madam when the name is unknown), or put the date after the salutation.

## ON_FAILURE
Step 1: Write the 8-element checklist before drafting. Step 2: Fill each element — never skip subject. Step 3: Match the pair — Dear Sir/Madam ↔ Yours faithfully; Dear Mr Sharma ↔ Yours sincerely. Step 4: Audit the draft top-down against the checklist.

## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_1
Order these blocks: salutation, subject, receiver's address, sender's address, date.

## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_1
sender's address → date → receiver's address → subject → salutation. A wrong answer placing subject before receiver's address reveals the student knows the pieces but not the legal order — format marks are positional.

## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_2
What's missing: [letter with sender's address, date, salutation 'Dear Sir', body, 'Yours sincerely', name].

## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_2
Two faults: no receiver's address and no subject line; plus the pairing error — 'Dear Sir' requires 'Yours faithfully', not 'sincerely'. A wrong answer catching only the missing elements reveals the student knows the skeleton but not the salutation↔subscription contract.

## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_3
Write the subject line for: a complaint to MCD about garbage not collected for two weeks.

## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_3
Something like 'Non-collection of garbage in Ward 12 for two weeks' — noun-phrase, one line, states the issue. A wrong answer writing a full sentence ('I want to complain that garbage…') reveals the student doesn't know the subject is a headline, not a sentence.
