## DEFINITION
A school notice is a clipped public announcement: institution name, NOTICE, date, heading, and a body answering What/When/Where/Whom — in third person, inside a box, in about 50 words.

## COGNITIVE_LEVEL
apply

## MASTERY_CRITERIA
The student drafts a complete class-7 notice with all elements, third-person voice, and the 4W body, and audits a defective notice for missing blocks and register slips.

## SCOPE_BOUNDARY
Covers basic school notices (events, lost/found). Does NOT cover the formal word-limit enforcement or senior formats.

## WALKTHROUGH
The box IS the format — draw it first. Stack: SCHOOL NAME / NOTICE / date / heading. Body in clipped third person: 'A football match will be held on 12 March at 4 pm in the main ground. Interested students may contact the undersigned.' No 'I', no excitement, just facts.

## COMMON_MISCONCEPTION
Students write notices like invitations ('Please do come!'), use first person, and forget the issuing authority and contact line.

## ON_FAILURE
Step 1: Box first. Step 2: Header stack — org, NOTICE, date, heading. Step 3: Body = What + When + Where + contact, third person. Step 4: Sign with designation.

## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_1
What is wrong: 'NOTICE — Hi friends! We are having a super fun book fair next week, please come all!!!'

## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_1
Nearly everything: first person, exclamation, no org name/date/heading/contact, invitation-tone. A wrong answer listing only 'no date' shows single-fault audits — check skeleton AND register.

## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_2
Which body fits a notice: 'You should come!' or 'Interested students may register with Mrs Das by Friday'?

## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_2
The second — third person, action + deadline + contact. The first performs enthusiasm, which is not what notices do.

## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_3
Where does 'ABC School' go in a notice?

## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_3
At the very top, above NOTICE — the issuing authority leads because authority IS the format. A wrong answer putting it at the bottom shows the signature-block confusion.
