## DEFINITION
Class 12 reports add precision: the lead compresses What/When/Where/Who in ~25 words; body paragraphs deploy passive constructions for objectivity ('the prizes were distributed'); and quotes must carry attribution. Word limit ~125-150.

## COGNITIVE_LEVEL
create

## MASTERY_CRITERIA
The student writes a board-standard report with compressed lead, passive-voice objectivity, attributed quotes, and correct length — no narrative or opinion drift.

## SCOPE_BOUNDARY
Covers reports at class 12 standard. Does NOT cover analytical paragraphs or feature articles.

## WALKTHROUGH
Objectivity mechanics: passive voice removes the reporter — 'the winners were felicitated' not 'we felicitated the winners'. The quote slot: one attributed reaction ('The response was overwhelming, said the convenor'). Length is part of the format — a 200-word report loses marks like a missing headline.

## COMMON_MISCONCEPTION
Students write active first-person reporting ('we organized', 'I saw'), pad beyond the word limit, and invent quotes without attribution tags.

## ON_FAILURE
Step 1: Lead = 4W in one breath (~25 words). Step 2: Body = how it unfolded, passivized ('was inaugurated', 'were awarded'). Step 3: One attributed quote. Step 4: Outcome close. Step 5: Audit — no 'I/we enjoyed', inside the limit.

## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_1
Fix for report register: 'We organized a great event and everyone loved it.'

## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_1
'The event drew participation from 12 schools' — passivized, quantified, no 'we'. A wrong answer keeping 'we organized' shows the institutional-vs-personal stance confusion.

## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_2
Which lead is board-grade: 'On 5 August, the school hosted its annual blood donation camp in the auditorium, where 120 donors participated' or 'It was a proud day when we all gathered for a noble cause'?

## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_2
The first — compressed 4W + scale. The second is a narrative opening performing pride instead of reporting facts.

## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_3
A report reads 'the principal was very happy' — what improves it?

## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_3
Convert to an attributed quote — 'The participation was heartening, said the Principal' — certification through quotation beats adjective-assertion.
