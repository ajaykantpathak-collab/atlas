## DEFINITION
Subject–verb agreement is decided by the HEAD of the subject phrase, never by the noun nearest the verb. 'The box of chocolates is empty' — 'box' is the head, 'chocolates' is a prepositional decoy. Boards plant decoys between subject and verb precisely to catch students who agree with the last word they read.

## COGNITIVE_LEVEL
analyze

## MASTERY_CRITERIA
The student can identify the head noun in a subject phrase of any length, strip intervening phrases (of-, with-, along with-, as well as-), and choose the correct verb form in 9/10 items including inverted and there-sentences.

## SCOPE_BOUNDARY
Covers head-noun identification and the decoy structures (prepositional phrases, parentheticals, 'as well as/along with/together with' which do NOT make the subject plural). Does NOT cover indefinite pronouns (91_002) or pluralia tantum (91_003).

## WALKTHROUGH
Take 'The leader, along with his followers, ___ (is/are) here.' Cover everything after the comma — the subject is just 'the leader'. Then the 'there' trap: 'There ___ (is/are) three books on the desk' — 'there' is a dummy; the real subject sits AFTER the verb. Train the two-step: find the real subject, then agree.

## COMMON_MISCONCEPTION
Students agree the verb with the nearest noun ('the box of chocolates are') — proximity agreement — and treat 'as well as / along with' like 'and', wrongly pluralizing the subject.

## ON_FAILURE
Step 1: Find the verb. Step 2: Ask 'who/what + verb?' — that phrase is the subject. Step 3: Cross out prepositional phrases and as-well-as inserts inside it. Step 4: Agree with what survives. Step 5: In 'there is/are', look past 'there' to the noun after the verb.

## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_1
'The quality of these mangoes ___ (was/were) poor.'

## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_1
'was' — head noun is 'quality', singular; 'of these mangoes' is a decoy phrase. A wrong answer 'were' reveals proximity agreement — the student matched the verb to 'mangoes', the noun physically closest, instead of the head.

## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_2
'The captain, as well as the players, ___ (was/were) congratulated.'

## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_2
'was' — 'as well as' is a parenthetical insert, not a coordinator; subject stays singular. A wrong answer 'were' reveals the student believes as-well-as = and, the exact misconception this node exists to kill.

## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_3
'There ___ (is/are) a boy and two girls waiting outside.'

## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_3
'are' — compound subject 'a boy and two girls' follows the verb. (Some accept proximity 'is'; boards expect 'are'.) A wrong answer 'is' chosen because 'a boy' is singular shows the student applied proximity logic to the one construction where it is explicitly wrong.
