## DEFINITION
Quantifiers encode attitude: 'few' = almost none (negative), 'a few' = some (positive); 'little' = almost none, 'a little' = some. Countability governs the family: few/fewer/many for countables, little/less/much for uncountables, and 'the few/the little' means 'the small amount that exists'.

## COGNITIVE_LEVEL
apply

## MASTERY_CRITERIA
The student selects few/a few/little/a little by intended attitude and countability, uses fewer/less correctly, and distinguishes 'the few' (all of the scarce group) from 'a few'.

## SCOPE_BOUNDARY
Covers the four attitude quantifiers + count/mass pairing + fewer/less. Does NOT cover articles (94_001).

## WALKTHROUGH
Two glasses on the desk: 'There is little water' — bad news, nearly empty. 'There is a little water' — good news, enough for a sip. Same quantity, opposite mood: the 'a' flips scarcity into sufficiency. Then countability: 'few friends' (countable) vs 'little time' (uncountable) — mismatch 'few time' is the classic error.

## COMMON_MISCONCEPTION
Students think few/a few differ only in style, not attitude — missing that 'few' is pessimistic. They also write 'less marks/students' (countables need 'fewer') — the most common board determiner error.

## ON_FAILURE
Step 1: Countable or not? → picks the family (few/many vs little/much). Step 2: Is the news good or bad? → 'a-' forms mean 'enough to matter', bare forms mean 'almost none'. Step 3: Comparatives — fewer for countables, less for uncountables.

## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_1
'He has ___ friends, so he is always lonely.' few or a few?

## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_1
'few' — the loneliness clue signals the negative reading (almost none). A wrong answer 'a few' reveals the student reads few/a few as interchangeable spellings — missing the entire attitudinal payload the 'a' carries.

## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_2
Correct: 'There were less students today.'

## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_2
'fewer students' — students are countable. A wrong answer accepting 'less' shows the student doesn't partition English nouns into count/mass — the prerequisite for half the determiner system.

## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_3
'___ knowledge he had saved him.' the few / a little / the little?

## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_3
'the little' — the small amount of knowledge that existed was sufficient; 'the little' = all of a scarce supply. A wrong answer 'a little' misses that 'the' + little/few collects the whole scarce set — a distinction boards test precisely because it looks trivial.
