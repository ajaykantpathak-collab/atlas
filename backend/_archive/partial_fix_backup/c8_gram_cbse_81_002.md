## DEFINITION
Some verbs cannot go passive and some sentences should not: intransitive verbs (sleep, arrive, happen) have no object to promote — 'The baby was slept' is impossible. And passives earn their keep only when the receiver or result matters more than the doer.

## COGNITIVE_LEVEL
analyze

## MASTERY_CRITERIA
The student refuses passive for intransitive verbs, identifies when passive is the better choice (result-focus, unknown doer, formal/scientific register), and defends keeping active when the doer is the point.

## SCOPE_BOUNDARY
Covers passive legality and rhetorical choice. Does NOT cover the mechanics of conversion (81_001).

## WALKTHROUGH
Legality first: 'The accident happened' cannot invert — there is no object to move; 'was happened' is a classic wrong build. Choice second: 'My grandmother knitted this sweater' stays active — the doer is the heart of the sentence. 'Rice is grown in Punjab' goes passive — who grows it is obvious and unimportant.

## COMMON_MISCONCEPTION
Students passivize mechanically — including intransitives ('was happened', 'was slept') — and flip sentences where the doer carries the meaning, burying the point.

## ON_FAILURE
Step 1: Find the object. No object → passive is illegal. Step 2: Ask what the sentence is ABOUT — if the doer is the point, stay active. Step 3: Formal/scientific contexts prefer passive ('The experiment was conducted').

## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_1
Can this go passive? 'The sun rises in the east.'

## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_1
No — 'rises' is intransitive; nothing receives the action. A wrong answer 'The east is risen by the sun' shows the student moves words without checking transitivity.

## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_2
Better active or passive: 'The storm destroyed the crops.'

## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_2
Either works, but passive 'The crops were destroyed' is better when the damage is the news — the storm is already known. The defensible answer matters more than the choice.

## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_3
Why is 'was happened' always wrong?

## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_3
'Happen' is intransitive — no object exists to promote to subject, so there is no legal passive. A wrong answer accepting it shows the transitivity check was skipped.
