## DEFINITION
A letter to the editor is a formal letter with a public voice: full skeleton, then a body that states a civic issue with facts, its impact, and a constructive suggestion — through the columns of the paper, not to the editor personally. Measured outrage, not a rant.

## COGNITIVE_LEVEL
create

## MASTERY_CRITERIA
The student writes a letter-to-editor with correct formal skeleton, fact-based issue statement, impact paragraph, and a specific constructive suggestion — in measured public register.

## SCOPE_BOUNDARY
Covers the letter-to-editor format. Does NOT cover complaint letters to officials (similar skeleton, different addressee logic).

## WALKTHROUGH
The three-beat body: (1) state the issue with specifics — 'the broken pavement on Mall Road has caused three falls this month'; (2) show impact on the public; (3) suggest a concrete fix or appeal to authorities. The frame opener: 'Through the columns of your esteemed daily, I wish to highlight…'

## COMMON_MISCONCEPTION
Students write rants (emotive, accusation-heavy, no facts) or pure complaints with no suggestion — both lose the content marks this format reserves for impact + suggestion.

## ON_FAILURE
Step 1: Formal skeleton complete (editor's address, subject). Step 2: Issue stated with facts/figures, not adjectives. Step 3: Public impact paragraph. Step 4: Constructive suggestion — what should be done, by whom. Step 5: 'Yours sincerely' + name (named recipient pairing).

## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_1
Which opener fits: 'Through the columns of your esteemed daily, I wish to draw attention to…' or 'Hey Editor, your city is falling apart!'

## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_1
The first — public-formal register. The second is rant-register; letters to editors persuade through facts, not volume.

## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_2
A body reads 'The traffic is very bad and everyone is angry. Something must be done!!!' — what's missing?

## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_2
Facts (which road, what delay, what incidents), impact detail, and a specific suggestion — plus the exclamations must go. A wrong answer 'nothing' shows the student can't audit for content-completeness.

## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_3
Which close pairs correctly with 'To the Editor'?

## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_3
'Yours sincerely' — the editor is a named-role recipient; 'faithfully' is for Dear Sir/Madam-unnamed pairs. Boards accept sincerely here.
