## DEFINITION
Collective nouns (team, committee, jury, family) take singular when the group acts as one unit and plural when members act individually — 'The jury has reached a verdict' vs 'The jury are divided'. Pluralia tantum (scissors, trousers, news, physics, measles) have fixed forms that ignore their appearance: 'news is', 'scissors are'.

## COGNITIVE_LEVEL
apply

## MASTERY_CRITERIA
The student chooses singular vs plural for collectives by asking 'unit or members?', and correctly handles fixed-form nouns (news singular, scissors plural) plus 'a pair of' constructions.

## SCOPE_BOUNDARY
Covers collectives with unit/member contrast and the board-favourite fixed-form nouns. Does NOT cover indefinites (91_002).

## WALKTHROUGH
Act it out: 'The class is quiet' — one silent blob = singular. 'The class are arguing among themselves' — members doing different things = plural. Then list the fakes: words ending in -s that are singular (news, physics, measles, billiards) and plural words with no singular (scissors, trousers, spectacles, earnings).

## COMMON_MISCONCEPTION
Students mark by spelling — anything ending in 's' gets a plural verb ('the news are'), and collectives get memorized as always-singular, so 'the committee are quarrelling' looks wrong to them even when members act separately.

## ON_FAILURE
Step 1: For collectives ask 'acting as one unit, or as separate members?' Step 2: For -s words, check the fixed-form list first. Step 3: For 'a pair of scissors/trousers', agree with 'pair' (singular).

## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_1
'The committee ___ (has/have) submitted its report.'

## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_1
'has' — the committee acts as one unit submitting one report. A wrong answer 'have' suggests the student pluralizes all collectives reflexively, or over-applies the member-acting rule without checking for the unit reading.

## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_2
'The news ___ (is/are) encouraging.'

## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_2
'is' — 'news' ends in -s but is singular by convention. A wrong answer 'are' is pure spelling-agreement — the terminal -s hijacked the student's grammar.

## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_3
'A pair of scissors ___ (is/are) on the table.'

## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_3
'is' — the head is 'pair', singular; 'scissors' alone would take 'are'. A wrong answer 'are' shows the student agreed with the salient noun rather than the head — same failure as the decoy-phrase node, different costume.
