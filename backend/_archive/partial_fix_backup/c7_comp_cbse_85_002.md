## DEFINITION
An informal letter to a friend or relative keeps the light skeleton — address, date, Dear ___, warm body, loving sign-off — and the body must DO something: share news, ask questions back, invite, thank, or apologize. A letter that only reports is a postcard, not a letter.

## COGNITIVE_LEVEL
create

## MASTERY_CRITERIA
The student writes a complete informal letter with all blocks and a body that carries a clear purpose (news + questions + a forward-looking close), sustaining warm register throughout.

## SCOPE_BOUNDARY
Covers purpose-driven informal letters (news, invitation, thanks, apology). Does NOT cover formal letters or email format.

## WALKTHROUGH
The purpose test: why are you writing? To share (news + reactions), to ask (questions to them), to invite (details + enthusiasm). A good body does all three lightly: 'I joined the football team! How is your new school? Come visit during Diwali.' The close looks forward: 'Write back soon.'

## COMMON_MISCONCEPTION
Students write a flat news-report body with no interaction — no questions to the reader, no forward close — and lose the warmth marks while keeping perfect skeletons.

## ON_FAILURE
Step 1: Skeleton check — address, date, Dear, body, loving close. Step 2: Body does three jobs — your news, questions about them, a forward look (invite/plan/write-back). Step 3: Read aloud — does it sound like you?

## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_1
What three jobs does an informal letter body do?

## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_1
Share your news, ask about them, close with something forward-looking (invite/plan/write-back). A wrong answer 'just tell them things' produces the flat postcard-body this node exists to fix.

## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_2
Fix this cold line for a letter to a friend: 'I am writing to inform you of my selection in the school team.'

## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_2
'You will not believe it — I made the school team!' A wrong answer that only drops 'inform' keeps the reporting stance — the fix is excitement, not vocabulary.

## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_3
Which close earns the warmth mark: 'Yours faithfully' / 'Write back soon, your friend Amit' / 'The end'?

## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_3
The middle — forward-looking and personal. 'Faithfully' is for strangers; 'The end' isn't a letter close at all.
