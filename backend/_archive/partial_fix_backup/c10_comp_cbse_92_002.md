## DEFINITION
The analytical paragraph describes a chart/table in ~100–120 words: identify what the data shows, report the trend and the extremes, compare — never opine. Banned: 'I think', reasons, suggestions. Required: report verbs (shows/indicates/reveals), comparatives, and data citation.

## COGNITIVE_LEVEL
apply

## MASTERY_CRITERIA
Given a chart, the student writes a 3-part analytical paragraph (what the data is → main trend → key comparison) with zero opinion, correct report verbs, and at least two cited figures.

## SCOPE_BOUNDARY
Covers bar/line/pie description for CBSE. Does NOT cover event reports (92_001) or essay argumentation (93_001).

## WALKTHROUGH
Teach the three-part skeleton: (1) 'The chart shows ___ over ___' (frame). (2) 'Overall, ___ rose/fell sharply while ___' (trend). (3) 'X peaked at 62% in 2019, compared to Y at 31%' (extremes + comparison). Then the golden rule: the data is the star — the writer is invisible. 'I think the trend is worrying' loses the mark; 'The trend indicates a decline' earns it.

## COMMON_MISCONCEPTION
Students editorialize — adding causes ('because of poor facilities'), opinions ('which is very good'), and advice ('the government should') — because they can't believe description alone is the task.

## ON_FAILURE
Step 1: Sentence 1 names what the chart measures. Step 2: Sentence 2 states the dominant trend. Step 3: Cite 2+ figures with comparatives. Step 4: Delete every opinion word — think, feel, should, because, unfortunately.

## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_1
Chart shows city pollution rising 2015–2024. Which sentence belongs: 'Pollution rose steadily from 45 to 78 AQI' or 'This is because people buy too many cars'?

## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_1
The first — reports the figure; the second speculates a cause, which is banned. A wrong answer reveals the student thinks analysis means explaining WHY — in this genre it means reporting WHAT, precisely.

## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_2
Rewrite for the genre: 'I think the graph shows very bad pollution.'

## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_2
'The graph indicates a rise in pollution from X to Y' — report verb + cited data. A wrong answer keeping 'I think' or 'very bad' shows the student hasn't adopted the impersonal-observer stance the paragraph requires.

## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_3
What goes in sentence 1 of an analytical paragraph?

## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_3
The frame — what the data shows and its scope ('The bar chart depicts literacy rates in four states between 2011 and 2021'). A wrong answer jumping straight to a figure shows the student doesn't know the reader needs the map before the territory.
