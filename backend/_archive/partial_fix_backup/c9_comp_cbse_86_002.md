## DEFINITION
Formal register bans contractions, slang, exclamations, vague words (stuff, a lot, really), and emotional statements — it converts feelings into facts: 'smells gross' → 'poses a health hazard'. Courtesy phrases (I would be grateful, kindly) do the commanding.

## COGNITIVE_LEVEL
apply

## MASTERY_CRITERIA
The student rewrites informal markers into formal equivalents, maintains third-person objectivity in complaint bodies, and closes requests with courteous fixed phrases.

## SCOPE_BOUNDARY
Covers register rules for formal letters. Does NOT cover the skeleton (86_001).

## WALKTHROUGH
The banned list hunt: contractions (can't→cannot), slang (gross→unhygienic), intensifiers (really bad→a matter of serious concern), exclamations (delete), vagueness (stuff→specific noun). The courtesy rule: 'I would be grateful if you could…' outranks 'You must…' in formal power.

## COMMON_MISCONCEPTION
Students think formal = fancy vocabulary — they add big words but leave contractions and slang in place. The real rules are deletions and conversions, not decoration.

## ON_FAILURE
Step 1: Scan for the banned list — contractions, slang, !!, vague intensifiers. Step 2: Convert emotional claims to factual ones. Step 3: Close requests with 'I would be grateful if you could + verb'.

## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_1
Fix: 'I'm writing coz the streetlight hasn't worked for like two weeks and it's super annoying.'

## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_1
'I am writing to report that the streetlight near my residence has been non-functional for two weeks.' A wrong answer fixing only the contraction leaves emotive residue ('very annoying') — every marker must convert.

## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_2
Is 'do the needful' good formal register?

## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_2
No — it is a dated Indianism boards mark down; say what is actually needed ('arrange daily collection'). A wrong answer shows the student mistakes officialese for formality.

## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_3
Which request close earns the mark: 'Fix this fast!!!' or 'I would be grateful if you could look into the matter at the earliest'?

## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_3
The second — courteous indirection carries the command in formal register. The first performs urgency instead of requesting action.
