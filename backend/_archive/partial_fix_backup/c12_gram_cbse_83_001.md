## DEFINITION
Sentence-reordering items test cohesion logic: sentences link through pronoun references, article flow (a→the on second mention), time/logic sequence, and connector chains (first, then, however, therefore). The opener carries no backward references.

## COGNITIVE_LEVEL
analyze

## MASTERY_CRITERIA
The student reconstructs paragraph order by tracing pronoun antecedents, a→the article flow, logical sequence, and connector dependencies.

## SCOPE_BOUNDARY
Covers paragraph reordering strategy. Does NOT cover within-sentence jumbled words (class 6) or cloze.

## WALKTHROUGH
The chain rules: (1) the opener introduces new entities with 'a' and contains no pronouns pointing backward; (2) 'the', 'this', 'he/it/they' refer backward — their sentence cannot open; (3) connectors ('however', 'then', 'therefore') demand their logic-predecessor; (4) the closer resolves the sequence.

## COMMON_MISCONCEPTION
Students order by topic-feel instead of reference-chains, and place 'the X' sentences before 'a X' introductions — breaking the article flow.

## ON_FAILURE
Step 1: Find the opener — new entities, no backward pronouns, 'a' articles. Step 2: Chain the references — each 'the/he/this' must land after its introducer. Step 3: Check connector logic. Step 4: Read the assembled paragraph — every link must point backward cleanly.

## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_1
Which sentence opens a jumbled paragraph: 'The machine then stopped working' or 'A small factory bought a new machine'?

## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_1
The second — 'a machine' introduces the entity; 'the machine' requires it to already exist. A wrong answer shows the student ignores the a→the chain.

## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_2
'He failed the test.' / 'Ravi never studied.' — which comes first and why?

## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_2
'Ravi never studied' first — 'He' needs its antecedent 'Ravi' established. Pronoun-before-noun is the reordering giveaway.

## DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTION_3
Order: 'Therefore, the project was cancelled' / 'Funds ran out midway' / 'The project began in 2020'.

## DIAGNOSTIC_ANSWER_3
began (setup) → funds ran out (problem) → therefore cancelled (result). 'Therefore' demands its cause first — connector logic fixes the tail.
