## explain
The brain sits protected inside the skull and is split into three working zones. The forebrain (cerebrum, thalamus, hypothalamus) does the thinking and hormone control; the midbrain relays signals; the hindbrain (cerebellum, pons, medulla) runs balance and the automatic life functions like heartbeat. The spinal cord continues the signal path down into the body.
