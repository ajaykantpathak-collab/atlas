## explain
The codon table is the cell's dictionary: find the first base down the left, the second across the top, the third down the right — the box gives the amino acid. AUG starts every protein; three codons (UAA, UAG, UGA) are full stops. The code is degenerate — 61 codons for 20 amino acids — and nearly universal across life.
