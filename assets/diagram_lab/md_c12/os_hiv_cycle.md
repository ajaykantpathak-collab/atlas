## explain
HIV runs a seven-step hijack: gp120 locks onto the CD4 receptor; the viral core fuses in; reverse transcriptase converts the RNA genome into DNA; integrase splices it into the host chromosome — now it's permanent. The cell's own machinery transcribes viral genes, new particles assemble at the membrane and bud off, each wrapped in stolen membrane — thousands per infected T-cell.
