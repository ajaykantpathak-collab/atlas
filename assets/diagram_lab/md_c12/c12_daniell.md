## explain
Two half-cells, one circuit: zinc oxidises and dissolves, pumping electrons through the wire to the copper side where Cu2+ plates out as fresh copper. The salt bridge keeps both solutions neutral by drifting ions — remove it and the cell dies instantly. Zinc anode to copper cathode, 1.1 volts: the first practical battery.
