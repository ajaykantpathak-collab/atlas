## explain
Two growth curves side by side: the J (exponential, dN/dt = rN) climbs forever when resources are unlimited; the S (logistic, dN/dt = rN(K−N)/K) brakes as the population nears K — the carrying capacity line it flattens against. Real populations start like the left curve and end like the right.

With unlimited resources, every individual adds to the breeding pool, so growth accelerates with size: dN/dt = rN. The curve starts nearly flat, then bends upward forever — the J-shape of exponential growth. Real populations can't do this for long; it's what bacteria do in a fresh petri dish before food runs out.
