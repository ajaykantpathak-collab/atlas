## explain
The cyclotron solves acceleration with timing: a magnetic field bends particles into circles inside two D-shaped chambers, and every time they cross the gap an RF voltage kicks them faster. Each kick widens the circle — the same period every lap — so the RF flips in perfect sync until the beam exits at the rim at relativistic speed.
