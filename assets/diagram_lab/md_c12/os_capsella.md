## explain
The dicot embryo's choreography: the zygote divides into a globular ball riding its suspensor stalk, bulges into the heart-shaped stage as cotyledon bumps form, then folds into the mature embryo — cotyledons curled over the axis, plumule pointing up, radicle pointing down, all set for the seed coat to close around it.
