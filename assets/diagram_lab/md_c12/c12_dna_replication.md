## explain
Replication runs on teamwork at the fork: helicase unzips the helix, primase lays RNA starters, and polymerase builds new strands — continuously on the leading strand, in backward Okazaki fragments on the lagging strand. Ligase seals the fragments, and each daughter molecule keeps one old strand — semiconservative, exactly as the double helix predicted.
