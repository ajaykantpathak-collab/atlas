## explain
The hypothalamus pulses GnRH; FSH recruits a squad of follicles; the growing follicle pours estrogen, rebuilding the endometrium; high estrogen triggers the LH surge — ovulation; the burst follicle becomes corpus luteum pumping progesterone to stabilise the lining. No implantation → hormones crash → the lining sheds and the cycle restarts. Four hormones, two organs, 28 days.
