## explain
The anatropous ovule — the commonest angiosperm form — is inverted: the body bends 180° over its funiculus stalk, bringing the micropyle next to the stalk. Integuments wrap the nucellus, leaving a gap (micropyle) for the pollen tube; inside sits the embryo sac where double fertilisation happens. After fertilisation: integuments → seed coat, ovule → seed.
