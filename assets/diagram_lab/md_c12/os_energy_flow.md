## explain
Energy enters once (sunlight → GPP), shrinks at every step (respiration to heat), and never returns — a one-way river, not a cycle. Only ~10% crosses each trophic level; NPP is what's banked for the next level. Contrast with matter, which loops forever — this figure is the argument for why ecosystems need the sun forever.
