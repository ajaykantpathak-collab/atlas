## explain
Spermatogenesis is a numbers game: diploid spermatogonia divide by mitosis (self-renewal), one primary spermatocyte enters meiosis I and yields two haploid secondary spermatocytes; meiosis II doubles that to four spermatids; spermiogenesis reshapes each into a tailed spermatozoon. One diploid cell in → four haploid sperm out — continuous production from puberty.
