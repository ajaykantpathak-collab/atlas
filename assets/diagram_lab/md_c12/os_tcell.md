## explain
A naive helper T cell needs a proper briefing: its receptor must fit the antigen and its CD4 must grip the MHC II — only then do cytokines drive it to clone into an army of matching helpers, which go on to activate B cells, cytotoxic T cells and macrophages. The helper is the immune system's commander — nothing starts without it.
