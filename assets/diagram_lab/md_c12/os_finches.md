## explain
From one mainland ancestor, Galapagos finches radiated into species each with a beak shaped for its food — probing warbler-finch, grasping tree finch, seed-crushing ground finch, flower-reaching cactus finch. Natural selection sculpted the tool to fit the job: adaptive radiation in 13 species on a few islands.
