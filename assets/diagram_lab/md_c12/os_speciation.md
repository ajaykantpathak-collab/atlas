## explain
Two tempos for making species: gradualism — the lineage drifts slowly and continuously until populations can't interbreed; punctuated equilibrium — species sit in stasis for ages, then a short explosive burst of change splits them. Both end at reproductive isolation; the fossil record shows both tempos.
