## explain
Human arm, bat wing, whale flipper, cat leg — wildly different jobs, identical bone plan: one humerus, radius and ulna, carpals, then digits. Homologous structures are evolution's signature — one ancestral blueprint remixed for flying, swimming, running, grasping. Divergent evolution in four bones.
