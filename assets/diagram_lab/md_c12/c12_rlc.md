## explain
Wire R, L and C in series across AC and each fights differently: the resistor stays in phase, the inductor's voltage leads, the capacitor's lags. Total opposition is impedance — R² plus (XL − XC)². When frequency makes XL = XC, they cancel, impedance bottoms out and current peaks — resonance, the principle tuning radios.
