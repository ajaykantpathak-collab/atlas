## explain
The lac operon is biology's cleanest gene switch. No lactose: the repressor sits on the operator, blocking RNA polymerase — genes OFF. Lactose arrives: it binds the repressor, the repressor falls off, polymerase reads lacZ-Y-A, and the cell makes the enzymes to digest its lunch. Genes turned on exactly when needed — no waste.
