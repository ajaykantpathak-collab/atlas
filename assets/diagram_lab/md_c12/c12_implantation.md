## explain
Implantation is a three-step docking: the day-6/7 blastocyst first *apposes* (loose contact), then *adheres* (trophoblast binds the luminal epithelium), then *invades* — sinking into the endometrium until fully buried by day 10-11. The trophoblast will build the placenta; the inner cell mass builds the embryo.
