## explain
Count species against area and a law emerges: S = CA^Z — species rise with area as a power law. On log-log axes it draws a straight line with slope Z (~0.2-0.4; islands steeper). The reason half the world's species live on 2.3% of land, and the math behind why cutting a forest in half loses a quarter of its species.
