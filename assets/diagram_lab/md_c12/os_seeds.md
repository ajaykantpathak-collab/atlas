## explain
Two seed blueprints side by side: the bean packs everything into two fat cotyledons wrapped in a seed coat — plumule up top, hypocotyl axis in the middle, root tip below. The maize grain keeps a separate endosperm food bank; its single cotyledon — the scutellum — sits at the interface, while coleoptile and coleorhiza sheath the shoot and root tips like launch tubes.
