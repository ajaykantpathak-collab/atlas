## explain
Delete one nucleotide and the ribosome's three-letter reading frame slips: everything downstream of the gap re-groups into different codons — Ser stays, but Val-Pro-Tyr becomes Ala-Leu-Leu. A single letter lost, and the protein is garbled from that point on.
