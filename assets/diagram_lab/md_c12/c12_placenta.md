## explain
The placenta is two tissues in one: the fetal side (chorionic plate + villi trees dangling into the intervillous space) and the maternal side (decidua basalis). Maternal blood floods the space around the villi — oxygen and nutrients diffuse across the villus walls into fetal blood, which the umbilical cord carries back. Two bloodstreams, never mixing, one organ.
