## explain
Agrobacterium is a natural gene engineer: attracted to plant wounds, it transfers a segment of its Ti plasmid — the T-DNA — straight into the plant's genome, causing crown gall tumours. Biotech hijacks the trick: swap the tumour genes for a useful gene, and the bacterium delivers your payload into the plant's DNA. The backbone of crop engineering.
