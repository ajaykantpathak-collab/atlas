## explain
Carbon loops: photosynthesis pulls CO₂ into plants, eating moves it up the food chain, respiration and decomposition return it to air, and the ocean swaps it in and out. Fossil fuels are ancient carbon parked underground — combustion burns them back to the atmosphere on our schedule, not geology's. That's the imbalance behind climate change.
