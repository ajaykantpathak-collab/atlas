## explain
Of hundreds of millions, one sperm wins the race to the egg — its acrosome enzymes digest a path through the zona pellucida, the membranes fuse, and the egg instantly hardens its coat against the runners-up (polyspermy block). One head inside, the tail drops away — the zygote's two pronuclei are on their way.
