## explain
An age pyramid stacks a population by age and sex — males left, females right, three tiers (pre-reproductive, reproductive, post-reproductive). The shape predicts the future: a wide base means more children than parents — explosive growth ahead; even columns mean slow growth; a narrow base means decline. India's pyramid is still broad-based — that's the demographic dividend.
