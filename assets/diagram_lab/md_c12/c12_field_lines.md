## explain
A dipole's field is a map in two layers: field lines stream from the positive to the negative charge — dense between them where the pull is strongest — while equipotential loops cross them at right angles. Moving along a field line costs energy; along an equipotential, it costs nothing. The two grids never meet except at 90°.
