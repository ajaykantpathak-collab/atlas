## explain
A catastrophe kills most of a population at random — not the weak, just the unlucky. The survivors rebuild the numbers, but the gene pool stays thin: alleles that vanished don't come back. Bottleneck = genetic drift at its harshest — cheetahs and northern elephant seals carry the scar.
