## explain
X-linked recessive moves criss-cross: a carrier mother passes the affected X to half her sons (who show it — no second X to cover it) and half her daughters (who carry silently, covered by father's clean X). Sons can't be carriers — one bad X means the trait. That's why haemophilia and colour blindness cluster in males.
