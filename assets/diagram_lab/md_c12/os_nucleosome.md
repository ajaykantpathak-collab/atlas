## explain
Two metres of DNA fit a cell because of staged folding: 147 base pairs wrap twice around an 8-histone spool (the nucleosome), H1 seals each wrap, and the beads-on-string 10 nm fibre coils into a 30 nm solenoid — the first ~7× compaction of many before chromosome territory. The packaging problem solved one bead at a time.
