## explain
Morgan's fly cross on a grid: the red-eyed father's X carries the dominant allele, the white-eyed mother's X's are both recessive. Line up her eggs against his sperm and the square predicts it — every daughter gets dad's red-eyed X (carriers all), every son gets mum's white-eyed X and shows it, since the Y offers no cover.
