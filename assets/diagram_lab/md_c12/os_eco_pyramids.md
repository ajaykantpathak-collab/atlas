## explain
Three ways to stack an ecosystem: count heads (numbers), weigh it (biomass), or meter the flow (energy). Numbers and biomass can invert — a pond's tiny phytoplankton crop supports huge zooplankton because turnover is fast — but the energy pyramid never flips: the 10% rule is thermodynamics, not opinion.
