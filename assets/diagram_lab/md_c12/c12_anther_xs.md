## explain
The micrograph proves the schematic: two lobes joined by a thin connective bridge, each carrying two pollen sacs packed with dark-staining pollen grains — all wrapped in the layered anther wall. Four chambers, one product. This is the transverse section a lab practical would show you under the microscope.
