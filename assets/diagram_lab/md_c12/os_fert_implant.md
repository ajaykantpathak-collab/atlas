## explain
Day 0: ovulation sends the egg into the tube where fertilisation happens in the ampulla. The zygote divides as it travels — morula by day 3-4, hollow blastocyst by day 5-6 as it reaches the uterus. By day 6-7 the blastocyst hatches and implants in the endometrium — a full week from release to docking.
