## explain
One bird species lands on empty islands and, generation by generation, splits into a dozen — each with a beak shaped by the food it chases: nectar sippers, insect probers, seed crushers, fruit tearers. Adaptive radiation: one ancestral stock → many forms filling many niches — the same engine that built Darwin's finches.
