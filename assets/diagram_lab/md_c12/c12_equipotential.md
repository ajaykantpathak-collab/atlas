## explain
An equipotential surface joins every point at the same potential — moving a charge along it costs zero work. Around a point charge they're concentric spheres (circles in 2-D), closer together where the field is stronger. Field lines always cut equipotentials at right angles — the steepest downhill direction of the potential landscape.
