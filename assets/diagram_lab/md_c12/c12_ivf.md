## explain
IVF moves fertilisation outside the body: hormones ripen a batch of eggs, they're retrieved, mixed with sperm in a dish, grown to blastocyst in the incubator, then a chosen embryo is transferred into the uterus. Two weeks later the pregnancy test answers the question — the process behind millions of births.
