## explain
The ribosome is a two-piece machine: the small subunit holds the mRNA while the large subunit assembles protein. tRNAs shuttle through three docks — A (arrive with the next amino acid), P (transfer the growing chain), E (exit empty). The chain threads out the top, codon by codon, ~15 amino acids a second.
