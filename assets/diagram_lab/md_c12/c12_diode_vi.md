## explain
A p-n junction diode is a one-way valve for charge. In forward bias the current stays near zero until the knee voltage (~0.7 V silicon), then rises steeply — the depletion barrier is beaten. In reverse bias only a tiny leakage flows, until the breakdown voltage Ubr where current surges (and usually destroys the junction unless limited). That asymmetry makes diodes the working part of rectifiers.
