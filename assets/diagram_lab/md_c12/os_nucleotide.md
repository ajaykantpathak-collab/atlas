## explain
A nucleotide is a three-part monomer: a nitrogenous base (two-ring purine or one-ring pyrimidine), a pentose sugar, and a phosphate cap. DNA uses deoxyribose — missing the 2' oxygen that ribose carries — and chains are built 5' to 3', phosphate to sugar, rung by rung.
