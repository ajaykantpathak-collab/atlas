## explain
One tube, two deliveries: the pollen tube grows down the style — vegetative nucleus steering, two male gametes aboard — and enters the ovule through the micropyle, guided by a synergid's filiform tips. The first gamete fuses with the egg to make the diploid zygote; the second fuses with the two polar nuclei inside the central cell to make triploid endosperm — the embryo's packed lunch.
