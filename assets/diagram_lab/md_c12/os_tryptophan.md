## explain
A self-regulating factory: when tryptophan is abundant it boards the repressor, the pair parks on the operator and RNA polymerase is blocked — no enzymes made. When tryptophan runs scarce the repressor sits idle, polymerase cruises past the operator, and the five trp genes build the enzymes that make more.
