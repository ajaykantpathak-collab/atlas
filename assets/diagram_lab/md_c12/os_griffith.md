## explain
Griffith injected mice with four combinations: live S (smooth, capsule) killed the mouse; live R (rough, no capsule) and heat-killed S left it healthy — but dead S plus live R killed it, and live S cells were recovered from the body. Something in the dead S cells transformed live R into killers — the "transforming principle," later proven to be DNA.
