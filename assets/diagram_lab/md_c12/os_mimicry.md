## explain
Batesian mimicry is evolutionary identity theft: a harmless species evolves the warning colours of a toxic one. Predators learn 'that pattern means pain' once — and avoid both. The mimic's lie only works because the model's truth keeps it credible; if mimics ever outnumber models, predators learn to ignore the signal.
