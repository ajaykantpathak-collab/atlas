## explain
Light through two slits becomes two coherent wave-trains. Where the paths differ by a whole wavelength, crests land on crests — a bright fringe. Where they differ by a half, crest meets trough — darkness. The alternating fringe pattern on the screen is the classic proof that light is a wave.
