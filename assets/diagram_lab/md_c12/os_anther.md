## explain
The anther cross-section is a four-sack factory: each microsporangium packs sporogenous tissue whose mother cells undergo meiosis into pollen. The sac walls stack four deep — tapetum feeding the grains inside, middle layers and endothecium bracing it, epidermis on the skin — while the connective joins the lobes. At maturity the sacs burst and pollen grains pour out.
