## explain
Monosaccharides get classified two ways at once: by where the carbonyl sits (end = aldose, middle = ketose) and by how many carbons the chain carries (triose 3, pentose 5, hexose 6). Glucose is an aldohexose — aldehyde at the end, six carbons long — and both facts are visible in its Fischer projection.
