## explain
The binding-energy curve is the map of nuclear power: it rises steeply from hydrogen, peaks at iron-56, then gently declines toward uranium. Anything moving toward the peak releases energy — fusion pushes light nuclei up the left slope (stars' fuel), fission slides heavy nuclei up the right slope (reactors' fuel). Iron sits at the top: the universe's dead end for energy.
