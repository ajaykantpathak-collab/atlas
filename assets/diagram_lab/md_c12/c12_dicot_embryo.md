## explain
After fertilisation the zygote divides into a proembryo on its suspensor stalk; the embryo proper passes through octant and globular stages, then the heart-shaped stage as two cotyledon lobes push out, finally maturing into a bent-over embryo with radicle and plumule — while the suspensor's job (pushing it into the nutritive endosperm) is done.
