## explain
A sperm is stripped-down engineering: head carrying the haploid nucleus under an acrosome enzyme cap (drills through the egg's zona), neck centriole as the hinge, midpiece packed with mitochondria to power the tail's whip — the whole cell is a delivery vehicle for 23 chromosomes.
