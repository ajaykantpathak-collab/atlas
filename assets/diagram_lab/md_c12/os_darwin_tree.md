## explain
Darwin's one sketch — "I think" — captures the whole theory: founding species at the base throw up diverging branches; most twigs end early (extinction) while a few reach the top (living descendants). The elephant family proves it real: from Palaeomastodon's root the tree fans out — mammoths and mastodons ended, Elephas and Loxodonta live on.
