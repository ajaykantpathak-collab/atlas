## explain
The loop that runs the cycle: hypothalamus pulses GnRH → pituitary releases FSH and LH → ovary grows the follicle (estrogen) and later the corpus luteum (progesterone) → those hormones feed back up to the brain, dialling the next stage — while the uterus thickens and sheds its lining at the bottom.
