## explain
The first infection builds slowly — a 5-day lag while B cells multiply, then a modest antibody peak. But memory cells persist; the second time the same antigen arrives, the response launches in 1-2 days and peaks ~100× higher, mostly IgG. That asymmetry is the whole point of vaccination: the first hit comes from a safe shot, not the disease.
