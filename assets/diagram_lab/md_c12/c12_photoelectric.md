## explain
Light comes in packets, and each photon pays a fixed toll: the metal's work function. Photon energy above the fee → the electron escapes with the change as kinetic energy. Below it → nothing escapes, no matter how bright the beam. That's why dim ultraviolet ejects electrons but blinding red light can't — energy lives in frequency, not intensity. Einstein's proof that light is a particle.
