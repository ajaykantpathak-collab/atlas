## explain
Sperm is made in the testes (hung outside the body — cooler, as spermatogenesis needs), matures in the epididymis, travels the vas deferens up and around the bladder, picks up fuel from the seminal vesicles and alkaline protection from the prostate, and exits through the urethra. Each gland adds to semen — the sperm is only ~5% of it.
