## explain
Nitrogen is everywhere but locked: N₂'s triple bond resists all but specialist bacteria. Fixation makes ammonia, nitrification turns it to nitrate, plants assimilate it into protein, ammonification returns dead matter to ammonia, and denitrification vents N₂ back to the sky. Every step is bacterial chemistry — no microbes, no cycle.
