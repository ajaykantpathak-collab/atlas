## explain
A cut triggers mast cells to dump histamine: nearby capillaries dilate and leak, flooding the tissue with blood and fluid — the redness, heat and swelling of inflammation. The fluid carries phagocytes to the scene to eat invaders. It looks like damage but it's the body's emergency plumbing — local traffic rerouted for defense.
