## explain
The cycle in one figure: the follicle strip matures left to right; the uterus runs menstrual → proliferative → secretory underneath; estrogen climbs to the LH spike at day 14 (ovulation), then progesterone dominates the luteal phase — and crashes if no implantation, restarting the period.
