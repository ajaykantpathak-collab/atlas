## explain
The adaptive immune system's opening move: a macrophage engulfs a bacterium, digests it, keeps antigen fragments, and presents them on MHC II molecules at its surface. A helper T cell with the matching receptor locks on — and activates, launching the coordinated attack. Presentation turns a random meal into an intelligence briefing.
