## explain
Light through a narrow slit does not stay in a line — it fans out into a central bright band flanked by weaker fringes. The narrower the slit, the wider the spread. The intensity plot shows the sinc² pattern: a dominant central maximum holding most of the energy, with minima at regular intervals and rapidly dying side lobes.
