## explain
A human arm, whale flipper, bat wing and horse leg look nothing alike — yet every one is humerus + radius/ulna + wrist + digits in the same order. Homology: same structure inherited from a shared ancestor, reshaped by different selective pressures. It's the strongest anatomical evidence for evolution.
