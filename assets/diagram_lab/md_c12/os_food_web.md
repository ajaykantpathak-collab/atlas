## explain
A food chain is a lie told for clarity — real ecosystems are webs: every species eats several things and is eaten by several. Arrows point mouth-to-mouth (energy's direction), producers anchor the base, and the apex sits where no arrows leave. Webs explain why removing one species ripples everywhere — pull a thread and the whole mesh shifts.
