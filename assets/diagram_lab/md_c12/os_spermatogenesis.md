## explain
One spermatogonium's full journey: mitosis keeps the stem pool topped up; the primary spermatocyte runs meiosis I into two haploid secondary spermatocytes; meiosis II yields four spermatids; differentiation grows the head and tail — four sperm from one diploid cell, continuously from puberty.
