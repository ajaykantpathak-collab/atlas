## explain
Cross-pollination is a three-step relay: pollen from the donor flower's anther clings to a visiting bee; the bee flies to another plant of the same species; its body brushes the receiver's stigma and the grains transfer. Genetic mixing between different plants — variation that self-pollination can't deliver.
