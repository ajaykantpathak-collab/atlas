## explain
Each cycle, a squad of primary follicles starts maturing — granulosa cells multiply, fluid vesicles appear and merge into the antrum — but usually only one reaches the Graafian stage and ovulates, releasing the secondary oocyte wrapped in its corona radiata. The emptied follicle transforms into the corpus luteum pumping progesterone; if no pregnancy follows, it scars over as the corpus albicans.
