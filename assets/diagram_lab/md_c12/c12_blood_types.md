## explain
Blood typing is pattern matching with antibodies: drop the sample into anti-A, anti-B, anti-A+B and anti-Rh serums. Wherever it clumps — agglutinates — that antigen is on the donor's red cells. Clump in anti-A and Rh but not B? You're A positive. All smooth? O negative — the universal donor.

## exam
- Tap the serum checking the rhesus factor
- Tap the clumps meaning antigen present
- Tap the sample staying smooth — antigen absent
