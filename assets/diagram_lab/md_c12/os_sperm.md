## explain
A delivery vehicle stripped to essentials: the head carries the nucleus under an enzyme cap — the acrosome that drills through the egg's coats. Behind the neck, the middle piece spirals with mitochondria feeding the tail — a flagellum that whips the whole cell upstream toward the ovum.
