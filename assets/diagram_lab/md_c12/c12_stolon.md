## explain
A stolon is a stem running along the ground that turns nodes into new plants: at each node, adventitious roots dive into the soil and a plantlet shoots up — genetically identical to the parent. Strawberries do it visibly; once the daughter plants establish, the connecting runner can die off and the clones live independently. Vegetative propagation for free.
