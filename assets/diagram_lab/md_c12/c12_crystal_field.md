## explain
In a free ion, all five d-orbitals share one energy. Surround the ion octahedrally with ligands and they split: eg orbitals point straight at the ligands and get repelled upward; t2g orbitals slip between the axes and drop. The gap Δo decides everything — whether electrons pair or spread (high/low spin) and which light gets absorbed (the complex's colour).
