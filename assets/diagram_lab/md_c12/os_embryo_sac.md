## explain
Seven cells, eight nuclei: the egg waits at the micropyle end flanked by two synergid guides whose filiform tips reel in the pollen tube; two polar nuclei share the giant central cell — a sperm fuses with them to make triploid endosperm; three antipodals anchor the far end and fade. One small chamber running the entire fertilisation event.
