## explain
An antibody is a Y built from two heavy chains and two light chains riveted by disulfide bonds. The arm tips carry variable regions — custom-matched grooves for one specific antigen — while the constant Fc stem is the handle immune cells grab. Millions of different variable tips on the same Y frame: that's how the immune system covers every enemy it might ever meet.
