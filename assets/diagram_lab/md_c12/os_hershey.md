## explain
Hershey and Chase tagged phage DNA with radioactive phosphorus (P32) and protein with sulfur (S35), infected bacteria, then blended and spun. P32 ended up inside the bacterial pellet — the DNA went in; S35 stayed in the liquid — the coat stayed out. DNA, not protein, is the genetic material.
