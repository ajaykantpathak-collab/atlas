## explain
Binary fission is prokaryotic reproduction stripped to the essentials: the chromosome replicates, the cell elongates pushing the two copies to opposite ends, the membrane pinches inward at the waist, and cytokinesis completes the split. No gametes, no variation — two clones in about twenty minutes under good conditions. That's why bacterial colonies explode.
