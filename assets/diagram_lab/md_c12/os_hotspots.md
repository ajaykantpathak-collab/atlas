## explain
34 biodiversity hotspots pack half the world's species onto 2.3% of its land — most cluster in the tropics. India holds four (Western Ghats, Himalaya, Indo-Burma, Sundaland-Nicobar). The map is also a triage list: hotspot species face double jeopardy — extraordinary endemism plus extraordinary habitat loss.
