## explain
Four chains — two heavy, two light — welded by disulfide bonds into a Y. The arm tips (Fab) carry variable regions shaped for exactly one antigen; the stem (Fc) is standard across antibodies of a class and summons the cleanup crew. Millions of Ys, one target each — the specificity comes entirely from those variable tips.
