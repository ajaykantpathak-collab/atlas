## explain
A B cell isn't useful until activated: its receptor catches the matching antigen, the cell swallows and displays it on MHC II, a helper T cell confirms the match — and the B cell explodes into clones: plasma cells pumping out thousands of antibodies per second plus memory cells for the rematch. Clonal selection — only the right soldier multiplies.
