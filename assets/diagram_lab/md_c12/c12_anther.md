## explain
A mature anther is dithecous — two lobes joined by connective tissue, each lobe carrying two pollen sacs (four total). The sac walls are layered: epidermis outside, endothecium for spring-loaded splitting, tapetum inside feeding the developing grains. When the sacs dehydrate and split — dehiscence — the pollen pours out, ready for transfer.
