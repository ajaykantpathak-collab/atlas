## explain
Oogenesis is stop-start by design: oogonia form before birth and arrest in prophase I for years; one resumes each cycle, meiosis I yields a secondary oocyte plus a polar body, and the oocyte arrests again in metaphase II — meiosis only finishes if a sperm enters. One big egg, three discarded polar bodies, almost all the cytoplasm kept for the embryo.
