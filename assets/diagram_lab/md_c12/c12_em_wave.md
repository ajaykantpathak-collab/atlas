## explain
An electromagnetic wave carries E and B fields that are perpendicular to each other and both perpendicular to the direction of travel — the ray points along E × B. The wavefront is the surface joining points in phase; the wave-normal is perpendicular to it. Energy travels at the speed of light, self-sustaining: changing E creates B, changing B creates E.
