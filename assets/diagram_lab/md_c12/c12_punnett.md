## explain
The Punnett square turns allele segregation into arithmetic: each parent's two alleles split into gametes, the grid shows every combination, and the classic monohybrid cross yields 1 RR : 2 Rr : 1 rr genetically — 3 dominant-looking to 1 recessive-looking. The recessive trait that vanished in F1 returns in exactly one quarter of F2 — Mendel's proof that traits come in particles, not blends.
