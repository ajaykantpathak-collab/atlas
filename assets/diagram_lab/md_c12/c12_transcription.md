## explain
The central dogma is biology's information flow: DNA's stored instruction is transcribed into mRNA in the nucleus, the messenger travels to a ribosome, and translation reads its three-letter codons into an amino-acid chain — the protein. Information moves one direction: DNA makes RNA makes protein. The gene is the recipe; the protein is the dish.
