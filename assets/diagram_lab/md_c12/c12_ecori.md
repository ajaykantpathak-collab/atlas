## explain
EcoRI reads DNA like a molecular barcode scanner: it finds GAATTC, cuts each strand between G and A — offset, not flush — leaving single-stranded AATT overhangs called sticky ends. Any two molecules cut by EcoRI can anneal their matching ends — that's how foreign DNA gets spliced into plasmids.
