## explain
Gause's classic experiment: two paramecium species each thrive alone — put them in the same dish with the same food and aurelia outcompetes caudatum to extinction. Competitive exclusion: two species can't permanently share one niche. The rule that explains why species partition habitats — and why invaders are dangerous.
