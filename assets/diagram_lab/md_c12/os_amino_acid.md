## explain
Every amino acid shares the same skeleton: a central alpha carbon bonded to an amino group, a carboxyl group, a hydrogen, and a side chain R. The R group is the only variable — twenty different R groups make the twenty amino acids, and it is R that decides whether a protein's building block is charged, polar or hydrophobic.
