## explain
Four whorls in order: sepals guard the bud (calyx), petals advertise (corolla), stamens make pollen (androecium — anther on filament), and the central pistil receives it — stigma on top, style as corridor, ovary holding the ovules at the base. The architecture every pollination story plays out on.
