## explain
Two lenses multiply: the objective — tiny focal length — magnifies the specimen into a real inverted image inside the tube; the eyepiece then magnifies that image like a simple magnifier, producing a giant virtual image your eye sees. Total magnification = objective power × eyepiece power — that's how a cell becomes screen-sized.
