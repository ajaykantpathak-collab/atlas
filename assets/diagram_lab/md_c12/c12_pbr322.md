## explain
pBR322 is the classic cloning vector — a small circular DNA carrying an origin of replication plus two antibiotic-resistance genes. Insert foreign DNA inside tetR (say at BamHI), and that resistance dies while ampR survives — so bacteria on ampicillin live, on tetracycline die, and you instantly spot the recombinants. Elegant negative screening built into one molecule.
