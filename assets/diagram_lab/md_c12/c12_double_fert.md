## explain
Flowering plants run two fertilizations at once: the pollen tube delivers two sperm nuclei — one fuses with the egg to make the zygote (2n), the other fuses with the twin polar nuclei to make endosperm (3n), the embryo's packed lunch. One pollen grain, two products: the plant and its pantry.
