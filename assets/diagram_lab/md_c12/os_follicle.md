## explain
The ovary runs a conveyor belt: primary follicles wait in the cortex, mature through fluid-filled tertiary stages, and one Graafian follicle ruptures to release the secondary oocyte — leaving the spent follicle to remodel into the hormone-secreting corpus luteum.
