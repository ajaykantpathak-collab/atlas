## explain
Elongation is a conveyor: a tRNA docks at the A site when its anticodon matches the codon; the ribosome passes the peptide chain from the P-site tRNA onto the new amino acid; the emptied tRNA slides to E and exits; the ribosome ratchets one codon forward — repeat until a stop codon. Every three bases, one amino acid.
