## explain
Four hormones draw the cycle: FSH humps early to start the follicle race; estrogen climbs through the follicular phase; the LH spike at day 14 is the ovulation trigger; then progesterone dominates the luteal phase — a mountain that collapses if no embryo arrives, sending the cycle back to day 1.
