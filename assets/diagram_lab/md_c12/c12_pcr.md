## explain
PCR is a thermal loop: 94°C unzips the DNA, ~55°C lets primers lock onto the ends, 72°C lets Taq polymerase extend new strands — then repeat. Each cycle doubles the target, so 30 cycles turn one molecule into a billion — the machine that made DNA fingerprinting, COVID tests and gene cloning routine.
