## explain
Water never stops moving: evaporation lifts it from the ocean (transpiration adds the forest's breath), condensation gathers it into clouds, precipitation drops it back, and runoff/groundwater return it to the sea — which holds 97% of everything. The cycle that feeds every river, crop and aquifer is the same one dinosaurs drank from.
