## explain
Every hydrogen line comes from an electron falling between levels: energy gaps are fixed, so photon energies are fixed — the atom's barcode. Falls to n=1 give ultraviolet Lyman; to n=2 visible Balmer; to n=3, 4, 5 infrared series. Beyond 0 eV the electron escapes completely — ionisation. Bohr's model predicted these numbers; the spectrum confirmed them.
