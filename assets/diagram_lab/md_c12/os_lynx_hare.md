## explain
Ninety years of Hudson Bay fur records draw biology's most famous cycle: hares boom, lynx follow a beat later, then hares crash and lynx starve — repeat every ~10 years. Predator and prey drive each other's numbers in a coupled oscillation — the textbook case of population interdependence.
