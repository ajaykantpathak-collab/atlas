## explain
DNA is negatively charged, so apply a current and it crawls toward the positive pole — through a gel mesh that slows big fragments more than small ones. Result: DNA sorted by size into bands, glowing under UV thanks to ethidium bromide, measured against a known-size ladder in the marker lane. Forensics, paternity tests, cloning checks — all run on this.
