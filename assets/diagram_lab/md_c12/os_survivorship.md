## explain
Three survival strategies graphed: Type I (humans, elephants) — few offspring, heavy care, nearly all live long then die together; Type II (birds, squirrels) — steady death risk at every age; Type III (fish, oysters, plants) — thousands of offspring, no care, most die at birth. K-selected species cluster Type I, r-selected Type III — same ecology, two chapters back.
