## explain
Each month an ovary releases an egg; fimbriae sweep it into the fallopian tube where fertilisation happens; the fertilised egg travels to the uterus and implants in the endometrium — the lining rebuilt every cycle and shed if no embryo arrives. The cervix gates the uterus; the vagina is the canal.
