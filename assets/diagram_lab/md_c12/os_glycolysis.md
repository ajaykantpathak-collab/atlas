## explain
Glycolysis splits one glucose into two pyruvates in the cytoplasm — no oxygen needed. The books balance like this: two ATP spent in the investment phase, four ATP and two NADH earned in the payoff phase — net gain of two ATP plus the electron carriers that cash in downstream.
