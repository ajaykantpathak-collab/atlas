## explain
This diagram shows the cross-sectional structure of skeletal muscle, highlighting how it is organised into smaller and smaller units. Large muscles are made of bundles called fascicles, which in turn contain many individual muscle cells known as muscle fibres. Rich networks of blood capillaries run between these fibres to supply the constant oxygen needed for movement.
