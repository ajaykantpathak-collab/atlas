# Synapse — the junction between two nerve cells

> source: Figure 18.3 Diagram showing axon terminal and synapse
> subject: biology

- id: axon | label: Axon | x: 0.350 | y: 0.150 | r: 0.050 | fn: nerve fibre that carries electrical signals downward
- id: axon_terminal | label: Axon terminal | x: 0.440 | y: 0.260 | r: 0.040 | fn: swollen end of the nerve fibre where signals are passed

- id: receptors | label: Receptors | x: 0.600 | y: 0.790 | r: 0.035 | fn: protein spots that catch chemical messengers on the receiving cell

## explain
This diagram shows a chemical synapse, where two nerve cells communicate without touching. When an electrical signal reaches the axon terminal, tiny sacs release chemical messengers across the gap to trigger a new signal in the next cell.
