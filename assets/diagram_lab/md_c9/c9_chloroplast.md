## explain
Inside a leaf's chloroplast, stacks of green thylakoid discs catch sunlight and split water — releasing the oxygen we breathe. The energy harvested flows into the surrounding stroma, where CO2 is turned into sugar. Spare sugar gets stored as starch granules.
