## explain
Rutherford fired alpha particles at gold foil and expected them all to pass through — they mostly did, but a few bounced back. That was impossible under the plum-pudding model. His conclusion: the atom's mass and positive charge sit in a tiny dense nucleus, and the rest is empty space.
