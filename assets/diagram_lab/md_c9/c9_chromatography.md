## explain
A drop of ink on paper seems black — until solvent climbs through it. Each dye dissolves and travels at its own speed: lightest climbs highest, heaviest stays near the baseline. One ink splits into its hidden rainbow — that is how labs check what a dye, food colour or medicine is really made of.
