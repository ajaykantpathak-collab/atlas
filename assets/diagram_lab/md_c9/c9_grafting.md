## explain
Grafting splices a shoot (scion) onto another plant's root base (stock). Cut surfaces are matched, bound tight, and the cambium layers heal together — the stock feeds water and minerals, the scion grows the desired flowers or fruit. Mango orchards and rose nurseries run on this trick.
