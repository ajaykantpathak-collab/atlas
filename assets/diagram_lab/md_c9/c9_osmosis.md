## explain
Water always flows across a membrane from fresher to saltier — that is osmosis. Put a blood cell in salty water and it shrivels as water leaves; in pure water it swells until it may burst; in matching-strength water it stays normal. That is why IV drips must match your blood's saltiness exactly.
