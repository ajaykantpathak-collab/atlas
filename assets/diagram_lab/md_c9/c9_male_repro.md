## explain
Sperm are made in the testes — kept outside the body because they need cooler temperatures. They mature in the coiled epididymis, travel up the vas deferens, pick up nourishing fluid from the seminal vesicles and prostate, and exit through the urethra. One channel, two jobs — the valves keep the paths separate.
