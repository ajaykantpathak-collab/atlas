## explain
Mitosis is a cell's way to photocopy itself. DNA is duplicated in interphase, chromosomes condense in prophase, line up at the equator in metaphase, split apart in anaphase, get wrapped in new membranes in telophase — and the cell pinches in two. Result: two daughter cells, identical to the parent.
