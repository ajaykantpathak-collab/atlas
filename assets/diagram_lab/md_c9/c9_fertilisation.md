## explain
The cycle: pollen lands on the stigma (pollination), germinates a tube down the style, and delivers two sperm cells to the ovule — one makes the embryo, the other makes its food supply (double fertilization, unique to flowering plants). The ovule becomes the seed, the ovary becomes the fruit, and germination starts the loop again.
