## explain
This diagram illustrates the global carbon cycle, showing how carbon circulates between the atmosphere, land, oceans, and living organisms. Reservoirs store carbon in different forms, while arrows indicate fluxes or the rate of transfer between them. Maintaining this balance is crucial for regulating Earth's climate and supporting life.
