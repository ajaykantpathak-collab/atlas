## explain
Sound travels down the ear canal and vibrates the eardrum. The three tiny ossicle bones — hammer, anvil, stirrup — amplify the vibration and press it into the cochlea, where fluid waves become nerve signals sent to the brain. The semicircular canals beside it handle your sense of balance, and the Eustachian tube equalises pressure when you yawn or swallow.
