## explain
The nucleus guards the DNA. A double envelope wraps it, pierced by pores that control what enters and leaves. Inside, chromatin carries the genes — loose euchromatin is active, packed heterochromatin is silent — and the dark nucleolus builds the ribosomes that later leave through the pores to make proteins.
