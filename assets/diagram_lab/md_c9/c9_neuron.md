## explain
A neuron is built to send one message fast. Dendrites collect signals, the soma sums them, and the axon fires the message onward. Fatty myelin wraps the axon in segments — the signal leaps node to node at up to 100 metres a second. At the terminal, chemical vesicles carry the word across the gap to the next cell.
