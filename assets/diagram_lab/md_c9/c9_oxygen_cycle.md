## explain
Oxygen cycles between sky, life, rock and ocean. Photosynthesis pumps in ~5000 units a year; respiration, fires and rusting minerals drain almost the same back. Buried carbon is the edge — it skips respiration, leaving a slow oxygen surplus. Even the atmosphere leaks a little hydrogen to space.
