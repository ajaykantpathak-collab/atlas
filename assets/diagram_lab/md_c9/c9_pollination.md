## explain
Pollination is pollen's trip from anther to stigma — by wind, insect, or bird. On the flower: petals attract, the anther makes pollen, the filament holds it up; the stigma catches the grain, the style carries the pollen tube down, and the ovary's ovules become seeds once fertilized.
