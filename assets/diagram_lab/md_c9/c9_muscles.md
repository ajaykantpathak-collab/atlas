## explain
Three muscle types, three jobs. Skeletal muscle — striped, many-nuclei, voluntary — pulls your bones. Smooth muscle — spindle-shaped, stripeless — squeezes your gut and blood vessels without you asking. Cardiac muscle — striped but branched — beats your heart nonstop, its disc junctions keeping every cell firing together.
