## explain
The cycle runs on two hormone waves. FSH grows follicles in the first half while estrogen rebuilds the lining; a sharp LH surge triggers ovulation around day 14; the leftover follicle then makes progesterone to keep the lining ready. No fertilization means progesterone drops, the lining sheds — and the cycle resets.
