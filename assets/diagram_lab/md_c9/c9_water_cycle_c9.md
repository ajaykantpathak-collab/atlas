## explain
The sun lifts ocean water into vapour; up high it cools into clouds; wind pushes the clouds over land where rain or snow falls; the water collects in rivers and flows home to the sea. The same water has cycled through dinosaurs, glaciers and your drink — Earth recycles every drop.
