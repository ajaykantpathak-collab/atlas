## explain
The ER is the cell's factory floor — a folded membrane maze spreading out from the nucleus. Rough ER is studded with ribosomes building proteins; smooth ER makes fats and detoxifies. Finished goods pinch off in vesicles that ship them to the Golgi, and lysosomes recycle the waste.
