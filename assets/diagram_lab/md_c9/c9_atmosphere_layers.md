## explain
This diagram shows the five major layers of Earth's atmosphere arranged by altitude. Each layer has unique conditions, from weather systems in the lowest troposphere to satellites and auroras in the thermosphere and exosphere. Together, these layers protect life on Earth and trap warmth.
