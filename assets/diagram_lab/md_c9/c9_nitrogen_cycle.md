## explain
Nitrogen fills 78% of the air but plants cannot touch it until bacteria fix it into ammonium, nitrites, then nitrates. Roots absorb the nitrates and build proteins; animals eat the plants; decomposers return dead matter to ammonium; denitrifying bacteria finally release N2 back to the sky. No bacteria, no life.
