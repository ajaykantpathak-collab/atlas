## explain
All light is the same thing — electric and magnetic fields waving — only the wavelength changes. Radio waves span kilometres; gamma rays are smaller than atoms. Between them sits the narrow rainbow your eyes can detect. Shorter wavelength means more energy, which is why gamma rays harm but radio waves pass by harmlessly.
