## explain
Sonar is underwater radar for sound. A ship fires a sound pulse; it races through the water, hits a submarine or seabed, and bounces back. Timing the echo gives the distance — sound travels about 1500 m/s in water. Bats use the same trick in air; dolphins do it with clicks.
