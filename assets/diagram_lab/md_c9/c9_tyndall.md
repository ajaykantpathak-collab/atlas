## explain
A beam of light is invisible until something scatters it. Dust, mist and smoke particles bounce the light sideways — suddenly you can see the beam itself. That is the Tyndall effect: colloids and suspensions scatter light, true solutions do not. Sunbeams through trees and car headlights in fog are the same physics.
