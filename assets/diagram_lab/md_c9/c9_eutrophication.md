## explain
Fertilizer runoff pours nutrients into a lake; algae feast and carpet the surface; the carpet blocks sunlight so lower plants die; the algae die too and rain down; bacteria decomposing all that dead matter burn up the oxygen; fish suffocate and the bottom becomes a dead zone. Feeding a lake too well is how you kill it.
