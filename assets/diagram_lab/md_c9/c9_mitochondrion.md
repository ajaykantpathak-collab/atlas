## explain
The mitochondrion burns sugar into usable energy (ATP). Its outer membrane wraps a folded inner membrane — those folds, the cristae, pack in the machinery that makes ATP. The matrix inside holds fuel-breaking enzymes, granules, and its own DNA — evidence that mitochondria were once free-living bacteria.
