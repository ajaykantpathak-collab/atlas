## explain
Meiosis makes sex cells — sperm and eggs — with half the DNA of a body cell. Division I separates chromosome pairs (with a segment swap called crossing over for variety); division II splits the copies apart like mitosis. Four unique cells result — that shuffling is why siblings differ.
