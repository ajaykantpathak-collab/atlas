## explain
Calcium has 20 electrons arranged 2-8-8-2: the inner shells fill first, and the outer shell's 2 electrons are the valence electrons — the ones calcium sheds to form Ca²⁺ in every bond it makes. Shell number and valence count explain an element's whole personality.
