## explain
Where two bones meet, the synovial joint makes movement smooth: cartilage caps the bone ends so nothing grinds, synovial fluid oils the gap, a membrane produces that fluid, and a capsule plus ligaments hold the whole joint sealed and stable. Knees, elbows and shoulders all work this way.
