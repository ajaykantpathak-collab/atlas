## explain
The heart is two pumps side by side. The right side collects oxygen-poor blood from the body (vena cava → right atrium → right ventricle) and pushes it to the lungs. The left side receives oxygen-rich blood back (pulmonary vein → left atrium → left ventricle) and fires it round the body through the aorta. Valves keep every beat one-directional; the septum keeps the two sides separate.
