## explain
Mendel picked peas for seven clean either-or traits: seed shape and colour, flower colour and position, pod shape and colour, plant height. Each had a dominant form that showed in the first generation and a recessive form that hid, then reappeared in a 3:1 ratio — the pattern that founded genetics.
