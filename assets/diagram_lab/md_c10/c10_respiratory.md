## explain
Air travels nose → larynx → trachea → bronchi → bronchioles → alveoli — branching like an upside-down tree. At the tips, 300 million air sacs wrapped in capillaries swap oxygen for carbon dioxide. The diaphragm below is the real pump — it drops, the chest expands, air rushes in.
