## explain
Each kidney packs a million nephrons, each a tiny filter factory. Blood is squeezed into Bowman's capsule; the filtrate winds through the proximal tubule, dives down the Loop of Henle to concentrate, climbs back through the distal tubule for fine-tuning, and exits the collecting duct as urine. Water, salt and glucose are reclaimed along the way — only waste leaves.
