## explain
Energy enters at the bottom — plants catch sunlight (10,000 kcal) — and climbs the pyramid in hops of a tenth: herbivores get 1,000, carnivores 100, top predators a mere 10. The other 90% is lost as heat each step, which is why eagles are rare and grass is everywhere. Decomposers sit outside the pyramid, recycling everything back.
