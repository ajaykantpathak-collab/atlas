## explain
A karyotype is a chromosome portrait — the 23 pairs laid out in order, biggest first. Pairs 1–22 are autosomes, identical in everyone. The 23rd pair decides biological sex: XY male, XX female — the Y is tiny but decisive. Doctors read karyotypes to spot extra or missing chromosomes.
