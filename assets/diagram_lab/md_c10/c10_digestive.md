## explain
Food travels a nine-metre factory line. The mouth and saliva start the breakdown, the oesophagus pushes it to the acid churn of the stomach, the liver and pancreas drip enzymes in, the coiled small intestine finishes digestion and absorbs the nutrients, and the large intestine wrings out the water before waste exits.
