## explain
Sound travels three zones: the pinna catches it, the canal pipes it to the eardrum, and three tiny bones (malleus, incus, stapes) amplify the vibration into the cochlea — the snail-shaped coil that converts it to nerve signals. The semicircular canals next door run your balance, and the Eustachian tube equalises pressure — that's why ears pop on flights.
