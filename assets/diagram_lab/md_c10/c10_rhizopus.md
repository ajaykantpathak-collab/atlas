## explain
Bread mould spreads by spores, not seeds. Root-like rhizoids anchor into the food, a stolon crawls along the surface, and upright stalks (sporangiophores) lift the round sporangia packed with sporangiospores. The heads burst, spores ride the air, and a new patch of mould lands on the next slice.
