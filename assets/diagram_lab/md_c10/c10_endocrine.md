## explain
Glands are the body's chemical mail system — they drip hormones straight into the blood, no ducts needed. The pituitary bosses the rest from the head; the thyroid sets your metabolic speed; the adrenals fire adrenaline in emergencies; the pancreas manages blood sugar; and the sex glands drive puberty and reproduction.
