## explain
Three zones run your life. The cerebrum — the folded dome — thinks, remembers, sees and decides. The cerebellum at the back balances and coordinates every movement. The brainstem at the base runs heartbeat and breathing without asking. Deep inside, the thalamus relays signals, the hypothalamus keeps body conditions steady, and the pea-sized pituitary bosses the other glands.
