## explain
Touch something hot and your hand jerks back before you feel the burn — the reflex arc skips the brain. The receptor fires, the sensory neuron races the signal to the spinal cord, a relay neuron hands it to the motor neuron, and the muscle contracts — all in a split second. The brain finds out afterwards.
