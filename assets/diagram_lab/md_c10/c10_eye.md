## explain
The eye works like a camera: the cornea bends light first, the iris controls how much enters through the pupil, the lens fine-tunes focus onto the retina — where receptors flip the image into nerve signals that race down the optic nerve to the brain. The jelly inside holds the shape; the sclera is the protective coat.
