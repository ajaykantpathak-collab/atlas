## explain
A concave mirror dishes inward like a torch reflector — parallel rays bounce off and converge at the focus. Where the object sits decides the image: beyond the centre it forms a real, inverted, shrunken image; close up it magnifies. Shaving mirrors, headlights and telescopes all run on this geometry.
