## explain
Germination is a relay race. Water wakes the seed and splits its coat. The radicle dives first to anchor the plant and drink; the plumule climbs next, carrying the cotyledons up with it. Once the first true leaves open, the seedling makes its own food and the empty cotyledons fall away.
