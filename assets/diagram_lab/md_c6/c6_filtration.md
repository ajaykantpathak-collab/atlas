## explain
Filtration separates by size, not by magic. The mixture lands on a membrane full of tiny holes. Small particles squeeze through the pores and become the filtrate; big ones cannot fit and stay behind as the retentate. The same idea works for coffee filters, sieve jugs and air purifiers.
