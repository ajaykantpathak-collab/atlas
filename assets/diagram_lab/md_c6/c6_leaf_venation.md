## explain
Veins are a leaf's supply pipes — xylem brings water in, phloem ships sugar out. This leaf shows reticulate venation: one strong midrib with branches that split into a fine net. Grasses do it differently — parallel veins running straight. The pattern helps you tell dicots from monocots at a glance.
