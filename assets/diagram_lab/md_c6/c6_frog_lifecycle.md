## explain
A frog's life runs in a circle. Eggs hatch into tadpoles that swim and breathe underwater like fish. Legs sprout, the tail shrinks away, and lungs replace gills — a froglet hops onto land. The adult frog returns to water to lay eggs, starting the cycle again. This shape-shifting is called metamorphosis.
