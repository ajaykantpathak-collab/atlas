## explain
Decantation is the simplest separation — let a mixture settle, then pour the clear liquid off gently, leaving the sediment behind. The guiding rod is the lab trick: the liquid runs down it smoothly instead of splashing and stirring the sediment back up. Rice water and muddy water both work this way at home.
