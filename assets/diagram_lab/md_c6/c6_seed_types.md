## explain
Bean (dicot) seeds split into two big cotyledons that store all the food — no separate endosperm. Maize (monocot) grains keep their food in a large endosperm, with one thin cotyledon (scutellum) pressed against it. Both have the same embryo parts: plumule becomes shoot, radicle becomes root.
