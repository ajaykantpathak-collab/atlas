## explain
This diagram shows the four stages of the mosquito life cycle, moving from eggs laid on water through the aquatic larva and pupa stages to the flying adult. Both the larva and pupa live in water to develop before taking to the air. Understanding this cycle helps us stop mosquito-borne diseases by removing stagnant water.
