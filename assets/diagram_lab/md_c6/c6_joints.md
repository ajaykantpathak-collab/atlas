## explain
This diagram shows the six major types of movable synovial joints in the human skeleton. Different joint structures allow different kinds of movement, such as the hinge action in the elbow or the wide rotation in the hip and shoulder. Together, they give the skeleton flexibility and mobility while keeping bones securely connected.
