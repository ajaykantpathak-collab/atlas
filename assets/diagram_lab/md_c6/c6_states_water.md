## explain
This diagram illustrates the physical transitions between the three states of matter: solid, liquid, and gas. Heating or cooling substances causes them to shift from one state to another along these pathways. Understanding these phase changes helps explain everyday phenomena like water boiling, ice melting, and frost forming.
