## explain
You cannot see a magnet's field — but compass needles can feel it. Each tiny needle swings to point along the field's direction, and together they trace the looping lines you see here: out of the north pole, around, and back into the south pole. Crowded lines mean a strong pull; spread-out lines mean a weak one.
