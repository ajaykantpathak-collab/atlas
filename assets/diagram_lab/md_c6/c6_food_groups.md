## explain
The pyramid maps a balanced diet: proteins and healthy fats anchor the top, vegetables and fruits form the biggest middle band, and whole grains sit at the narrow point — eat them, but modestly. Each group does a different job: protein builds, fats fuel, plants protect, grains energise.
