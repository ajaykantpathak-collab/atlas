## explain
This diagram shows the complete structure of a mature flowering plant, including zoomed-in views of the female pistil and male stamen. The colorful petals attract insects, which carry pollen to the sticky stigma. From there, pollen travels down to fertilize the ovules inside the ovary, eventually forming seeds and fruit.
