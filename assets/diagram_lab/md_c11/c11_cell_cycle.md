## explain
A cell's life is a cycle, not a straight line: grow in G1, copy the DNA in S, check and prep in G2, then divide in M. Interphase — the first three — takes 90% of the time. Checkpoints at each transition can pause the cycle or send cells into G0 rest if something is damaged.
