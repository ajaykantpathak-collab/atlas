## explain
Three specialists run the nervous system: sensory neurons report from skin and organs to the brain, motor neurons carry commands back to muscles, and interneurons connect them in the middle — the reflex arc's entire cast. Myelin wraps the long axons like insulation, making signals jump node to node.
