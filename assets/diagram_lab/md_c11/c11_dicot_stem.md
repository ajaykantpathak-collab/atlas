## explain
Slice a dicot stem and the architecture shows: pith at the centre, then xylem's blue vessels carrying water up, the cambium ring that keeps making new tissue, phloem carrying sugar down, and cortex under the epidermis skin. The transport bundles sit in a ring — the dicot signature, unlike the monocot's scattered bundles.
