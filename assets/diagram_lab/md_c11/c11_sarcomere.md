## explain
Muscle contracts by sliding, not squeezing: myosin heads on the thick filaments grab the thin filaments and ratchet them toward the centre, pulling the Z lines closer. The H zone shrinks, the sarcomere shortens, and thousands contracting together is a biceps flex. ATP fuels every ratchet.
