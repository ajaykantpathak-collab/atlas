## explain
Periplaneta americana is the classic insect dissection — three body parts (head, thorax, abdomen), three leg pairs, two wing pairs, and a tough chitin armour. The NCERT figure labels the head with its compound eye and antennae, the three thorax segments each carrying legs and wings, and the ten-segment abdomen ending in anal cerci and styles.
