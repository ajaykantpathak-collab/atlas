## explain
One heartbeat is 0.8 seconds of ordered chaos: the P wave fires the atria, the QRS spike fires the ventricles, the T wave resets them — then a long diastole while chambers refill. The bars below line up electrical events with mechanical squeezing: atria push, ventricles pump, everyone rests.
