## explain
An enzyme is a folded protein with one special pocket — the active site. Binding residues grip the substrate like a lock takes a key; catalytic residues do the chemistry and release the product unchanged. If heat or acid unfolds the protein — denatures it — the pocket vanishes and the enzyme dies.
