## explain
The Calvin cycle is a wheel, not a line: RuBisCO bolts CO2 onto RuBP, the pieces are reduced with ATP and NADPH into G3P, and most G3P recycles to regenerate RuBP while a fraction exits to build glucose. Three turns net one G3P — slow, but it feeds the whole planet.
