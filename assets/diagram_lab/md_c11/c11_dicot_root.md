## explain
A dicot root's cross-section shows the star pattern: xylem arms radiating from the centre (the stele), phloem tucked between them, a gatekeeper endodermis ring deciding what reaches the pipes, wide cortex for storage, and epidermis sprouting root hairs. That xylem star is how you tell a root section from a stem section at a glance.
