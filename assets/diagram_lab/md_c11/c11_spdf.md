## explain
The table's shape IS its electron story: the two left columns fill s orbitals, the six right columns fill p, the ten middle columns fill d, and the two rows parked below fill f. Block position tells you an element's valence electrons, its chemistry, and where it sits in the periodic logic — the shape is the point.
