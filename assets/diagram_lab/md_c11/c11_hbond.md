## explain
Oxygen hogs the shared electrons, leaving it δ⁻ while the hydrogens sit δ+ — a permanent polarity. The δ+ hydrogen of one molecule attracts the δ⁻ oxygen of a neighbour: the hydrogen bond, ~20 times weaker than a covalent bond but everything depends on it — ice floating, water climbing plants, DNA's rungs, protein folding.
