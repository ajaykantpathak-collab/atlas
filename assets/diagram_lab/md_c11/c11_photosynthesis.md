## explain
Photosynthesis runs in two stages: light reactions on the thylakoid stacks split water, release oxygen, and bank energy as ATP and NADPH; the Calvin cycle in the stroma spends that currency fixing CO2 into sugar. Sunlight in, oxygen out, sugar made — the reaction every food chain starts from.
