## explain
A projectile is two independent motions: horizontal velocity stays constant (no force sideways), vertical velocity gets eaten by gravity — shrinking to zero at the peak, then growing downward on the way back. The result is a parabola: same landing speed as launch, symmetric arc, and the peak is the only point where vertical velocity vanishes.
