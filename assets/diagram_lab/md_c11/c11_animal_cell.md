## explain
The animal cell runs on division of labour: the nucleus commands, ribosomes build proteins on the rough ER, the Golgi packages them, lysosomes recycle, mitochondria power everything, and centrioles organise division. No wall, no chloroplasts, no giant vacuole — that's what separates it from a plant cell.
