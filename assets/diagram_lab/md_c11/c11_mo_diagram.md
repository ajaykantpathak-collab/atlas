## explain
When two orbitals overlap, they never just add — they split into a lower bonding level and a higher antibonding level. The electrons drop into the lower one, which is why forming a bond releases energy and stabilises the molecule. The same splitting logic explains the MO diagrams of H₂, O₂ and every covalent system — bonding wins because it pays energetically.
