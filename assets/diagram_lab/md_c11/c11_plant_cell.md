## explain
A plant cell is an animal cell plus extras: a cellulose wall gives it the box shape, a giant vacuole presses the cytoplasm thin against the wall, and chloroplasts run photosynthesis inside. Pores called plasmodesmata punch through the wall so neighbours can share materials — one tissue, many cells.
