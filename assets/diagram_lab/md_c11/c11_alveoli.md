## explain
Gas exchange is a two-cell handshake: type I cells flatten the alveolar wall paper-thin so oxygen slips through to the capillary's red blood cells in one step. Type II cells secrete surfactant — the soap that stops the wet sacs collapsing. Macrophages patrol the space, eating dust and germs.
