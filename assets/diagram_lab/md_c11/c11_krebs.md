## explain
The Krebs cycle spins in the mitochondrial matrix: acetyl-CoA enters, joins oxaloacetate to make citrate, then the wheel sheds two carbons as CO2 while banking three NADH, one FADH2 and one ATP per turn. The oxaloacetate is regenerated — the wheel never runs out of track. The carriers then feed the electron transport chain for the real ATP harvest.
