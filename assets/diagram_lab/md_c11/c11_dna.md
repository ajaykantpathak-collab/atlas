## explain
Two backbones wind into a spiral staircase, and the rungs are base pairs following strict rules: A only pairs with T (two bonds), G only with C (three bonds). Those weak hydrogen bonds are the genius — strong enough to hold, weak enough to unzip when the cell needs to read or copy the code.
