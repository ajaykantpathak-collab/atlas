## explain
When the Moon slides directly between the Sun and Earth, its shadow lands on us in three zones. Inside the thin umbra, day turns to night — totality. In the wide penumbra, only part of the Sun is covered. If the Moon is too far away for the umbra to reach, the antumbra gives a fiery ring — an annular eclipse.
