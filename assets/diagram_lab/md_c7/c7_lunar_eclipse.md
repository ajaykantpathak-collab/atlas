## explain
A lunar eclipse happens when Earth lines up exactly between the Sun and the Moon. Earth casts a two-part shadow: the dark umbra in the centre and the pale penumbra around it. When the Moon's orbit carries it through the umbra, it goes dark — a total lunar eclipse. Only grazing the penumbra gives a fainter partial dimming.
