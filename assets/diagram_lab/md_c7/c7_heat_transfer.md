## explain
One kettle, three heat highways. Conduction carries heat molecule-to-molecule through the metal base; convection makes the hot water rise and cold sink in a rolling loop; radiation shines warmth straight out through the air — you can feel it without touching anything. Three mechanisms, one pot of boiling water.
