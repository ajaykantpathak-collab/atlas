## explain
Stomata are the leaf's gates. To open, potassium ions flow into the guard cells, water follows, and the cells swell — bowing apart into an open pore. To close, the ions leave, water drains, and the cells go limp — sealing shut. Open pores trade CO2 in and oxygen out; closed pores keep water in.
