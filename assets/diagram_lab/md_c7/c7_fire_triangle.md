# The fire triangle — three things every fire needs

- id: fuel | label: Fuel | x: 0.500 | y: 0.880 | r: 0.130 | fn: something to burn — wood, paper, petrol

- id: oxygen | label: Oxygen | x: 0.280 | y: 0.350 | r: 0.100 | fn: air's supporter — without it nothing burns
- id: fire | label: Fire | x: 0.500 | y: 0.570 | r: 0.110 | fn: the flame — all three sides balanced together
- id: balance | label: Proper balance | x: 0.500 | y: 0.680 | r: 0.045 | fn: the right mix — too little of any side, no fire

## explain
Fire is a recipe with exactly three ingredients: fuel to burn, heat to start it, and oxygen to keep it going. Remove any one side of the triangle and the flame dies — that is how every fire extinguisher works. Water removes heat, foam removes oxygen, and clearing the brush removes fuel.
