## explain
Food takes a 9-metre trip. It is chewed in the mouth, squeezed down the oesophagus, and churned with acid in the stomach. The liver adds bile, the pancreas adds juices, and the small intestine absorbs the nutrients into the blood. What is left travels through the large intestine — which reclaims water — and out through the rectum.
