## explain
Litmus paper is the quickest acid/base detective. Red litmus dipped in a base turns blue; blue litmus dipped in an acid turns red. Here the red strip flipped to blue — the liquid is basic. If a strip keeps its colour, the solution is either neutral or the other type.
