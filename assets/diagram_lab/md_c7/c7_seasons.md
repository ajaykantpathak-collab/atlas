## explain
Earth orbits tilted at 23.5°, and this image catches the June solstice — the moment the Northern Hemisphere leans fully toward the Sun. The North Pole gets 24-hour daylight while Antarctica sits in darkness. Sunlight hits the Tropic of Cancer most directly, which is why June is summer up north and winter down south.
