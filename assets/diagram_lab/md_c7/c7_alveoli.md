## explain
Every breath ends at the alveoli — millions of tiny air sacs wrapped in capillaries. Oxygen slips across the thin wall into the blood, and carbon dioxide slips the other way, out into the air sac. The pulmonary artery brings in the blood to be cleaned; the pulmonary vein carries it away oxygen-rich.
