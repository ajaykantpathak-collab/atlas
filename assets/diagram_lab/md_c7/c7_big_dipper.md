## explain
Seven bright stars trace a ladle shape in the northern sky — the Big Dipper. The two stars at the bowl's outer edge, Dubhe and Merak, are called the Pointers: draw a line through them and it leads straight to Polaris, the North Star. The dipper is not a constellation itself — it is an asterism inside Ursa Major, the Great Bear.
