## explain
Fish pull oxygen from water the way we pull it from air — through a huge blood-rich surface. Water flows over the red filaments, oxygen diffuses into the blood, and the cover flap pumps fresh water across constantly. The filaments' feathery spread maximizes the exchange area — the same trick lungs use.
