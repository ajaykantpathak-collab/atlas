## explain
The Moon does not change shape — we just see its sunlit half from shifting angles as it orbits Earth. Waxing means the lit edge grows (right side first), full moon puts the whole face in view, then waning shrinks it away until the dark new moon returns. The cycle takes about 29.5 days — a lunar month.
