## explain
A convex mirror bulges outward — parallel rays hitting it spread apart after reflecting. Trace them backward behind the mirror and they appear to meet at one point: the virtual focus. This is why car side-mirrors show a wide, small view — and why "objects are closer than they appear" is printed on them.
