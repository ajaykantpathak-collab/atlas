## explain
Peel the Earth and you find shells like an onion — only hotter. The crust is a thin rocky skin, then the mantle's slow-flowing rock, then a liquid outer core of iron, and at the very heart a solid inner core as hot as the Sun's surface. The liquid core's swirl is what makes the whole planet a magnet.
