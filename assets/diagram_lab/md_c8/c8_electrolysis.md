## explain
Run current through water and it splits: hydrogen bubbles at the negative electrode, oxygen at the positive — and hydrogen collects at exactly twice oxygen's volume. That 2:1 ratio is the signature of H₂O: two hydrogens for every oxygen. Electrolysis is how rockets and fuel cells make clean hydrogen.
