## explain
A cyclone is a heat engine. Warm ocean air spirals up through the rain bands, dumps heat, and pours out the top as cirrus. Around the calm eye, the eye wall holds the worst wind and rain. In the northern hemisphere the whole system spins counterclockwise — flip to the southern hemisphere and it spins the other way.
