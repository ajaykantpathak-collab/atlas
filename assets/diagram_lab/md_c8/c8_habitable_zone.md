## explain
The habitable zone is a brightness-balance problem. Too close to a star and water boils off; too far and it freezes. Each row shows a different star's brightness — brighter stars push the green band outward, dimmer ones pull it in. On our Sun's row, Earth sits inside the green while Mars grazes the cold edge.
