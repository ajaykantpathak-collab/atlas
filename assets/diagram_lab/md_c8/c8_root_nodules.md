## explain
Legume roots carry a secret workforce — Rhizobium bacteria living inside the little bumps called nodules. The bacteria grab nitrogen from the air, turn it into a form the plant can eat, and the plant feeds them sugars in return. That partnership is why beans and peas enrich the soil they grow in.
