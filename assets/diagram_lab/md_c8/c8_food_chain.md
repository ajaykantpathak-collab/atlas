## explain
A food chain is a dinner queue. The shrimps graze on plants, small fish eat the shrimps, perch eat the small fish, the pike eats the perch — and the eagle at the top eats the pike. Energy climbs one step at a time, and most of it is lost at each hop, which is why top predators are rare.
