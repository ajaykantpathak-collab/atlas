## explain
The same molecules make all three states — only the packing changes. In a solid they sit locked in a lattice; in a liquid they stay close but slide; in a gas they fly free, filling any space. Heat is the key: add it and particles loosen — melt, then boil; remove it and they lock down — condense, then freeze.
