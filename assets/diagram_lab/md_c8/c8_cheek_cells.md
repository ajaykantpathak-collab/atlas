## explain
Scrape the inside of your cheek, stain it with methylene blue, and this is what you see — flat epithelial cells stacked like tiles. The dark dot in each is the nucleus holding your DNA; the pale blue around it is cytoplasm, wrapped in a thin membrane. Real cells are far smaller than a textbook drawing suggests.
