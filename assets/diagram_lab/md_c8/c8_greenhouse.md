## explain
Sunlight pours in and Earth must send the same energy back or heat up. The surface glows infrared — but greenhouse gases catch most of it and re-warm the ground, like a blanket. Right now the books do not balance: 0.6 W/m² more comes in than leaves, and that small excess is global warming.
