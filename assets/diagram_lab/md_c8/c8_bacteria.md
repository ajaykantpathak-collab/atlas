## explain
Bacteria are prokaryotes — cells without a true nucleus. Their DNA floats in a tangled nucleoid region, with small bonus plasmid rings alongside. A wall and capsule wrap the membrane for protection, ribosomes dot the cytoplasm building proteins, and flagella spin like propellers to swim.
