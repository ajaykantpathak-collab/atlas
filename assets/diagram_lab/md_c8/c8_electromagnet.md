## explain
Wrap wire around an iron nail, connect a battery, and the nail becomes a magnet — strong enough to lift a pile of nails. Cut the current and the magnetism vanishes instantly. That switchable magnetism is what makes electromagnets power everything from doorbells to scrapyard cranes.
