## explain
This diagram shows the skeletal structure of the right pectoral girdle and upper limb in frontal view. It starts from the shoulder girdle (clavicle and scapula) which connects the arm to the axial skeleton, leading down through the heavy humerus bone of the upper arm. The forearm contains two parallel bones—radius and ulna—which terminate in the wrist carpals, palm metacarpals, and finger phalanges to allow complex hand movements.
