## explain
This diagram shows the fluid mosaic model of the plasma membrane, composed of a phospholipid bilayer with embedded proteins and sugar chains. The integral proteins span across or bury deep within the membrane, while peripheral proteins stay on the outer surface. Together, these molecules make the membrane flexible and selectively permeable.
